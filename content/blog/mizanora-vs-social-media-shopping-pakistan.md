Instagram, Facebook aur WhatsApp par local businesses se achi cheezein mil sakti hain. Lekin sirf photo dekh kar order karne ke bajaye details likhit mein check karna zaroori hai. Mizanora ek website-based option deta hai jahan available products ko page aur checkout ke zariye dekha ja sakta hai. Har social seller ko unsafe kehna bhi durust nahi.

## Product ki tafseel pehle dekhein

Mizanora ke product page par seller ne jo description, photos, choices aur specifications add ki hain wo dekhein. Jahan available ho wahan highlights, box contents, care aur warranty bhi show hoti hai. Har item par har field hona zaroori nahi. Agar size ya material clear na ho to [support](/contact) se poochhein.

Social media par bhi yahi sawal likh kar poochhein: exact size kya hai, package mein kya hai, aur jo photo hai kya wohi variant milega? Replies aur listing ka screenshot apne record ke liye rakhna useful ho sakta hai.

## Order se pehle total verify karein

1. Product ki price aur quantity likhein.
2. Delivery fee aur destination confirm karein.
3. Agar code lagaya hai to checkout mein actual discount dekhein.
4. Payment method aur final amount verify karein.
5. Size, colour aur phone number dobara check karein.

Mizanora par cart aur checkout total dikhate hain; delivery aur payment ke current options checkout par confirm honge. Social sellers ke rules mukhtalif ho sakte hain.

## Delivery aur returns ka record

Mizanora ka [order tracking](/track), [delivery information](/shipping) aur [returns page](/returns) order ke baad madadgar ho sakta hai. Social seller se bhi tracking method, return conditions aur contact channel pehle se poochhein. Return accept hoga ya nahi, yeh item aur current policy par depend karta hai.

## Kahan Mizanora convenient hai?

Agar aap categories browse karna, product page par details dekhna aur aik website se checkout karna pasand karte hain, Mizanora aap ke liye convenient ho sakta hai. Agar kisi local artist ya small seller se custom cheez banwani hai, direct social conversation useful ho sakti hai. Dono cases mein identity aur terms verify karein; kabhi bhi OTP ya bank PIN share na karein.
