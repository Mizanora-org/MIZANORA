Children put everything in their mouths and pull everything apart, so a toy must be safe first and fun second.

## Check the age

Choose a toy made for your child's age. Toys for older children can have small parts that are dangerous for toddlers. If the listing does not state an age, ask the seller.

## Look for these signs of a safe toy

- Smooth edges and no loose small parts
- Strong stitching on soft toys
- No strong chemical smell
- A battery cover that closes tightly (button batteries are very dangerous if swallowed)

## Baby items

For feeding bottles, pacifiers and clothes, choose soft, skin-friendly materials and wash everything before first use.

## Read the description

Look for the material, the size and what is in the box. Photos next to a known object (a hand, a coin) show the real size. See [how to check product quality](/blog/how-to-check-product-quality-before-you-order-online).

## Clothes for kids

Children grow quickly, so choose a slightly bigger size, and in winter leave room for layers. See the [size guide](/size-guide).

## When it arrives

Check the toy before you give it: pull gently on small parts, test the batteries and keep the packaging until you are sure it is right. If it is faulty, read our [returns page](/returns).
