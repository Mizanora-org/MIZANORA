"Channel chal raha hai magar ruk ruk kar" — yeh IPTV ki sab se aam shikayat hai. Masla aksar line ka nahi, **aap ke ghar ke network** ka hota hai. Neeche wohi 9 cheezein hain jo support par sab se pehle poochi jaati hain.

## 1. Speed naapo, mehsoos na karein

Speedtest sirf phone par nahi, **usi device par** chalayein jis par TV dekhte hain. HD ke liye 10 Mbps, FHD ke liye 20 Mbps, 4K ke liye 40 Mbps — aur yeh **aap ke device tak** pahunchti ho, line tak nahi.

## 2. Wi-Fi ki jagah cable

TV aur router ke darmiyan deewar ho to 5 GHz bhi kamzor hoti hai. Aik LAN cable lagane se aadhi shikayatein khatam ho jati hain. Cable nahi lag sakti to **Wi-Fi extender** TV ke qareeb lagayein, router ke paas nahi.

## 3. 2.4 GHz se 5 GHz par jayein

2.4 GHz door tak jati hai magar slow hoti hai, aur mohalle ke 15 router usi band par hote hain. TV ko 5 GHz par shift karein — farq foran mehsoos hoga.

## 4. Peak hours

Raat 8 se 11 baje ISP par load sab se zyada hota hai. Agar sirf usi waqt buffer hota hai to masla line ka nahi, ISP ka hai. Test: subah 10 baje wohi channel chalayein.

## 5. Device limit

Aik line par 1–3 device chalte hain (plan ke hisaab se). Ghar mein 4 logon ne line share ki ho to teesra device chalne par purana ruk jata hai — yeh "buffering" nahi, **limit** hai. [Apna plan dekhein](/iptv#plans).

## 6. Router ko reset karein (sach mein)

Router 24/7 chalta hai, uska memory bhar jata hai. Mahine mein aik dafa **bijli se band kar ke 30 second baad** chalu karein. Purane routers (5+ saal) 50+ devices par bekaar ho jate hain — badalne ka waqt aa gaya hai.

## 7. VPN band karein

VPN on ho to speed aadhi reh jati hai aur kuch cases mein stream hi nahi khulta. IPTV ke liye VPN ki zaroorat nahi.

## 8. App ka cache clear karein

TiviMate / Smarters: Settings → Apps → app → **Clear cache**. (Data clear na karein, warna playlist dobara add karni paregi.)

## 9. Doosre device par test karein

Agar phone par wohi channel theek chalta hai aur TV par nahi, to masla **TV ka network** hai. Agar dono par rukta hai, to reseller ko batayein — unhein source side check karna hoga.

## Support ko kya batayein (yeh 5 cheezein)

1. Kaun sa channel aur waqt
2. Speedtest ka screenshot (usi device par)
3. Wi-Fi ya cable
4. App ka naam aur version
5. Kitne device chal rahe hain

In 5 jawabon se 80% masle aik message mein hal ho jate hain.

## Kab line badalni chahiye

Agar **3 mahine** mein 4 se zyada dafa source down ho aur reseller foran jawab na de, to provider badalna behtar hai. Sasta restream pehle din acha lagta hai, 40 din baad client chala jata hai — [is par yeh guide](/blog/iptv-channels-legal-pakistan) parhein.
