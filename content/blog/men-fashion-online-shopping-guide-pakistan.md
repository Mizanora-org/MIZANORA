Buying men's clothes online is easy once you know what to check. Use this guide for shirts, trousers, jackets, shoes and accessories.

## Start with the fit

Measure a shirt or trouser that already fits you well and compare it with the size chart on the product page. Do not trust the letter alone: a Medium from one maker can be a Large from another. See the [size guide](/size-guide).

## Fabric decides comfort

- **Cotton:** cool and easy to wash, good for summer
- **Wool blend and fleece:** warm, good for winter
- **Polyester blends:** lighter and wrinkle-resistant, can feel warm
- **Denim:** strong, look at the weight and the stretch

Read [how to check product quality before you order](/blog/how-to-check-product-quality-before-you-order-online).

## Jackets and winter wear

Check the lining, zip quality, collar and whether the jacket still fits over a sweater. Our [winter collection guide](/blog/winter-collection-online-shopping-pakistan-2026) lists what to buy first.

## Shoes and boots

Measure your foot in the evening and choose by length, not just the number. For boots, read [how to care for your boots](/blog/how-to-care-for-your-boots) and the [tactical boots buying guide](/blog/tactical-boots-buying-guide).

## Colours that work

Navy, black, grey and olive match almost everything. Buy these before bright colours.

## Save money

Buy one good piece instead of three weak ones. Compare the total price with delivery: [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).
