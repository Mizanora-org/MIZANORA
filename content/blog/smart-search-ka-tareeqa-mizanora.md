Aksar log kehte hain: *"mujhe woh cheez nahi mili"*. Zyada tar waqt cheez hoti hai — bas search ka tareeqa ghalat hota hai. Ye guide Roman Urdu mein hai aur 7 aise tareeqe batati hai jin se aap kisi bhi online shop par 30 second mein sahi cheez dhoondh sakte hain.

![Muaaz Iqbal - Mizanora par search ka sahi tareeqa](/img/muaaz/muaaz-iqbal-05.jpg "Smart search: naam se nahi, kaam se dhoondhein")

## 1. Naam se nahi, **kaam** se dhoondhein

Sab se badi ghalti ye hai ke hum product ka *naam* type karte hain jo humein yaad nahi hota. Us ki jagah **kaam** likhein:

- "kurti" ke bajaye → **"summer kapray"**
- "power bank" ke bajaye → **"phone charge backup"**
- "mixer" ke bajaye → **"kitchen kaam"**

Achhi search engine ko *mujhe ye kaam karna hai* samajh aata hai, *is product ka kya naam hai* nahi.

## 2. Roman Urdu mein likhein — ghalat spelling se mat daro

Mizanora ki search Roman Urdu samajhti hai: **kurti**, **kurty**, **qurti** — teeno ek hi cheez hain. Is liye himmat se apni zaban mein likhein. Yahi wajah hai ke Noor (hamari assistant) Urdu aur Roman Urdu mein bolti hai: [Noor ka tareeqa](/blog/mizanora-ai-assistant-noor-urdu-voice-shopping).

Ye [Urdu-first theory](/blog/urdu-first-design-theory-technology-in-your-own-language) ka hissa hai: user ki zaban design ki shuruat hai, aakhir mein chipkaya gaya translation nahi.

## 3. Awaaz se poochhein (voice search)

Agar type karna mushkil ho ya spelling yaad na ho to **mic** dabayein aur bol kar poochhein. Ye sab se tez tareeqa hai — khaas kar un logon ke liye jo English mein type nahi karte. Poori guide: [voice search shopping guide](/blog/voice-search-and-smart-search-shopping-guide).

## 4. Rate likh kar dhoondhein

Ek badi wajah jo log ko "mehnga" lagta hai: wo product dhoondhte hain, phir rate dekh kar hat jate hain. Ulta karein — **pehle apna budget likhein**:

- **"winter jacket under 3000"**
- **"gift under 1500"**
- **"kitchen items 500 se kam"**

Is se aap ko wohi cheezein milti hain jo aap khareed sakte hain, aur aap un cheezon par waqt zaya nahi karte jo budget se bahar hain. Isi soch par [search by price](/blog/search-by-price-not-by-name-pakistan-shopping) ka poora article hai.

## 5. Category mein ghus kar chhanein (filter)

Search box sab kuch nahi hai. Pehle badi category kholein (**Winter**, **Kitchen**, **Beauty**), phir filter lagayein. Do-qadam wala tareeqa aksar ek lambi search se tez hota hai.

Hamari categories: [/products](/products) aur [/winter](/winter).

## 6. Photo dekh kar faisla karein, sirf description padh kar nahi

Search ke baad jo results aayein, un mein photo par 10 second zaroor dein. Photo hi batati hai ke cheez wahi hai jo aap chahte hain ya nahi. Kaise padhni hai photo: [how to read product photos](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## 7. Jo na mile, woh likh kar bhej dein

Agar aap ko cheez na mile to **WhatsApp** par likh bhejein — hum dhoondh kar batate hain, ya agle import mein laate hain. Ye "search failed" nahi, "request" hai.

---

## 30 second ka formula

1. Kaam likho (naam nahi)
2. Apni zaban mein likho
3. Budget saath likho
4. Photo dekh kar confirm karo

Chaar qadam, tees second. Agali baar jab koi kahe "mujhe nahi mila", to ye chaar qadam usay bhej dein.
