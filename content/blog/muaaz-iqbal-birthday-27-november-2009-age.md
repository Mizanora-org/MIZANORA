## A young builder from Kasur

Muaaz started learning computers in Kasur and quickly moved from using apps to building them. He designs, codes and ships complete products on his own: a full online store with cash on delivery, a programming language for Urdu-medium students, and a privacy-first calendar app. His motto is the one behind Mizanora: **shop smart, shop halal** - honest prices, no fake discounts, no bought reviews.

## What he has built by his teens

1. **Mizanora** - an online marketplace for Pakistan with thousands of products, cash on delivery, WhatsApp ordering, deals, an Urdu voice assistant (Noor), a worker programme and a daily Islamic feed.
2. **Asaan Lang** - an open-source Urdu and Roman-Urdu programming language (MIT licence) with a playground and a compiler that turns a program into a website.
3. **HijriAnchor** - an offline, ad-free dual-calendar app combining the Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar with the Gregorian calendar, for Android and iOS.
