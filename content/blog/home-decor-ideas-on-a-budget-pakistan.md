You do not need to repaint the house to make it feel new. Small changes can lift a room.

## 1. Better lighting

Warm lights make a room feel cosy. Replace harsh white bulbs in the living room and add a table lamp or a string light.

## 2. New cushion covers

Cover sets are cheap and change the whole sofa. Choose two main colours and one accent.

## 3. Wall art and frames

A few framed prints or Quranic calligraphy on one wall look neat. Keep the same frame colour.

## 4. Organise with storage

Baskets, racks and organisers hide clutter. A tidy room looks bigger.

## 5. Plants

Real or artificial plants add life. Choose pots that match your colours.

## 6. Curtains and rugs

These cost more but change the mood most. Measure your windows and floor before you order.

## Shop smart

- Measure first and write the size on your phone
- Check the colour in natural-light photos
- Compare the total price with delivery
- Buy in one order to save on delivery
