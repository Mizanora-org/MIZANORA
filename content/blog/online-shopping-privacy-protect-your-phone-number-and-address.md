Every online order needs some personal details. The key is to share only what a real order needs, and nothing more.

## What a normal order needs

- Your name
- A phone number the rider can call
- A delivery address

That is enough for cash on delivery. A shop does not need your card PIN, your OTP code or your account password.

## Never share these

- **OTP or verification codes** sent to your phone. Nobody from a real shop will ask for them.
- **Card PIN or banking password.**
- **A photo of your CNIC** just to place a normal order.

If someone on a call or chat asks for these, stop and close the conversation.

## Use cash on delivery when unsure

With [cash on delivery](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) you pay when the parcel arrives, so you do not hand over payment details to a shop you do not know well.

## Check the shop before you trust it

Look for a real contact page, clear return information and a working order tracking page. Read [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## Be careful with links

Scam messages often copy a shop name and send a link that looks similar. Type the shop address yourself or open it from the official profile.

## After you order

Keep the order number. You can use [order tracking](/track) to see the status. Delete screenshots of private details that you no longer need.

## Quick rules

1. Share the minimum details.
2. Never share OTP codes.
3. Prefer cash on delivery with new shops.
4. Open links from official profiles only.
