A good gift is not the most expensive one. It is the one that suits the person. Use this simple plan to choose and send a gift online without stress.

## Step 1: Fix a budget

Decide the amount first, including delivery. It stops you from drifting upward while browsing. Use the price filters on the [products page](/products) to stay inside it.

## Step 2: Think about the person

- What do they use every day?
- What do they complain about that you could fix?
- What are they interested in: cooking, travel, fashion, gadgets?

A useful small gift beats a fancy item that sits unused.

## Step 3: Match the occasion

Eid, birthdays, weddings and housewarmings call for different gifts. For festive seasons, order early. See [Eid and sale season shopping tips](/blog/eid-and-sale-season-shopping-tips-pakistan).

## Ideas by price range

- **Under Rs. 1,000:** phone accessories, kitchen tools, small home items, hair and beauty basics.
- **Rs. 1,000 to 3,000:** clothing, bags, watches, kids toys and bigger home items.
- **Above Rs. 3,000:** a better quality set, a gadget or a bundle of several small things.

These are only examples. Choose based on the person, not the price tag.

## Step 4: Check size and colour

For clothes, choose a size you are sure about, or pick items with flexible sizing like shawls and scarves. When in doubt, ask the seller on [WhatsApp](/contact).

## Step 5: Plan the delivery

Order a few days early. If you send a gift to another city, write the receiver's phone number carefully so the rider can reach them. Cash on delivery to someone else can be awkward, so tell them first or arrange the payment.
