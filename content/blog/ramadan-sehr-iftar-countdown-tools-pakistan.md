In Ramadan every minute matters. A good countdown tool shows how long is left until Sehr ends and until Iftar, in **your** city.

## Why times differ by city
Sunrise and sunset change with the place. Kasur, Lahore, Karachi and Quetta have different Sehr and Iftar times, sometimes by several minutes. Always use the times of your own city and, when in doubt, follow your local masjid.

## What a good tool shows
- A live countdown to Sehr end and Iftar.
- Location-based times.
- Dua of Iftar and of Sehr for daily reminder.
- Easy sharing with family.

## Namaz times on Mizanora
The [Islamic feed](/islamic) on the home page shows today's Hijri date, the next Islamic occasion and the five namaz times for Kasur. Times are approximate: prefer your masjid's timetable.

## A reminder
The Prophet (peace be upon him) said that the one who fasts has two joys: one when he breaks his fast, and one when he meets his Lord (Sahih Bukhari 1904, Sahih Muslim 1151). Make the most of both moments.
