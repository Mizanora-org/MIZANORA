Kasur, Punjab. Yahan se Lahore sirf ek ghante ka safar hai, magar technology ki duniya yahan doosre
raste se aati hai — mobile screen se. **Muaaz Iqbal** ne wahi rasta chuna: YouTube tutorials, GitHub
ke issues, aur zid ke sath likhi hui raat ki coding.

Aaj woh Mizanora (Pakistan ka online marketplace), HijriAnchor (dual-calendar app), Asaan Lang (Urdu
programming language) aur BarakatWave TV (live TV app) bana chuka hai — aur yeh sab **ek hi aadmi ke
kaam ka nateeja** hai, kisi agency ka nahi.

## Kaam ka tareeqa: chhota shuru, phir theek karo

Har project ek hi asool par chala: *pehle chalta hua version, phir behtar version.* Mizanora bhi
aisa hi bana:

1. Pehle ek simple product list.
2. Phir cart, cash on delivery, WhatsApp order.
3. Phir deals, Islamic feed, Urdu assistant.
4. Phir Movies & Series hub, apna player, Songs section, Live TV.
5. Aur har qadam par ek hi sawal: **"user ko rokna nahi hai"** — page khali na ho, button dead na ho,
   error aaye to rasta dikhe.

Yeh "never a dead end" ka asool aaj Mizanora ke code mein har jagah nazar aata hai: movie page par
data na aaye to player aur search phir bhi kaam karte hain, song mirror down ho to browser khud
doosra mirror try karta hai, aur image load na ho to monogram dikhta hai — toota hua icon nahi.

## AI ko kaise istemal karta hai

Muaaz ka tareeqa AI ke sath ajeeb nahi, **practical** hai: Claude, Gemini, Qwen aur ChatGPT —
chaaron ko alag alag kaam par lagana, aur har jawab ko code mein test karna. AI se code likhwa kar
seedha deploy karna nahi; AI se **plan** lena, phir khud verify karna.

Isi wajah se Mizanora ke har version ke sath tests hote hain (`npm test`), build check hota hai
(`npm run check`) aur ek "verification" section documentation mein likha jata hai. Jo cheez test
nahi hoti, woh shipped nahi hoti.

## Kasur se, Pakistan ke liye

Pakistan ke liye software likhne ka matlab hai: slow internet, sasta Android phone, Urdu keyboard,
aur cash on delivery. Isi liye Mizanora ka pehla asool **weight** hai (page halka ho), doosra
**offline-first** (data na aaye to bhi page khali na ho), aur teesra **zaban** (Urdu + Roman Urdu).

Yeh blog usi kaam ka record hai — jo cheez banayi, jo tooti, jo theek hui. Agar aap bhi Kasur,
Punjab ya Pakistan ke kisi chhote shehar se ho aur coding seekh rahe ho: shuru karo, chhota ship
karo, phir theek karo. Bas.
