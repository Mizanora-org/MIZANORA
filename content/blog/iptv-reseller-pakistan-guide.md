Pakistan mein IPTV ka kaam cable se zyada **reseller** chalate hain: aik banda lines banata hai, doosra unhein mohalle mein bechta hai. Mizanora IPTV isi model par bana hai — magar hisaab bilkul khula hua.

## Numbers (yaad rakhnay wali 4 cheezein)

| Cheez | Kitna | Kisko |
|---|---|---|
| Connection fee | **Rs 50** | Rs 30 aap, Rs 20 Mizanora |
| Client ka monthly | **Rs 200** | Rs 100 aap, Rs 100 Mizanora |
| Cap | Rs 200 | is se zyada charge nahi kar sakte |
| Clients | **Unlimited** | jitne chahein |

Yani aik client, aik saal:

- Aap: Rs 30 + (11 × Rs 100) = **Rs 1,130**
- Mizanora: Rs 20 + (11 × Rs 100) = **Rs 1,120**

100 clients: aap ka **Rs 10,000 mahina**, Mizanora ka bhi Rs 10,000 mahina.

## Kaam kaise chalta hai

1. **Register** — WhatsApp par naam, shehr, JazzCash number. OTP se verify, admin approve.
2. **Line banayein** — panel se username/password ya MAC. 10 second ka kaam.
3. **Client ko bhejein** — WhatsApp par details + setup link.
4. **Payment lein** — har mahine, apne JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank par.
5. **Mizanora ko pay karein** — 1 se 10 tareekh ke darmiyan, JazzCash 0306-2015326.

## 1–10 ki wasooli ka matlab

Mizanora aap se mahine ki shuruat mein wasooli karta hai:

- **1 tareekh:** invoice WhatsApp par
- **3, 5, 7:** reminders
- **9:** aakhri warning
- **10:** aakhri din
- **12:** auto-suspend (aap ki lines band, clients par recharge screen)

Payment ke foran baad sab wapas chalu. Is liye behtar hai ke client se payment **1–5 tareekh** mein le lein — phir aap ke paas 5 din ka buffer hota hai.

## Cap Rs 200 kyun hai

Cap ka faida aap ko bhi hai:

- Market mein price fix rehti hai, koi "sasta wala" aap ka client nahen le jata.
- Client ko overcharge ka darr nahi hota — is liye woh rukta nahi.
- Aap ka margin har line par **Rs 100** guaranteed rehta hai.

Panel cap khud lagata hai — aap Rs 250 set nahi kar sakte, aur yehi baat client ko bata kar aap trust banate hain.

## Kya kya sahulat milti hain

- Unlimited clients, unlimited sub-reseller
- Trial lines free (24 ghante) — naye client test kar ke aate hain
- Apna payment number aur apni price (cap ke andar)
- Apni branding: recharge screen par **aap ka naam aur number**
- Client-wise expiry, device limit, suspend/extend — sab panel se
- Statement: kis client ne kab pay kiya
