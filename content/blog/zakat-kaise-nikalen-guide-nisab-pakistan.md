Zakat Islam ka ek bunyadi rukn hai. Is guide mein hum asaan lafzon mein samjhate hain ke **zakat kaise nikalen**, nisab kya hota hai, aur kis kis maal par zakat aati hai. Hisab ke liye hamara free [Zakat Calculator](/tools/zakat-calculator) istemal karein.

> Ye aam rehnumai hai, fatwa nahi. Apne khaas masle (shares, zameen, qarz, jewellery) ke liye kisi mustanad aalim se poochein.

## Zakat kis par farz hoti hai

Zakat us musalman par farz hai jis ke paas **nisab** ke barabar ya zyada maal ho aur us par **ek qamari (Islami) saal** guzar jaye. Qarz jo abhi ada karna ho, wo maal se minhaa (kam) kiya jata hai.

## Nisab kya hai

- **Chandi ka nisab:** 52.5 tola, yaani taqreeban 612.36 gram.
- **Sone ka nisab:** 7.5 tola, yaani taqreeban 87.48 gram.

Pakistan mein bohat se ulama chandi ka nisab istemal karte hain kyun ke wo kam hai aur ghareebon ka faida zyada hai. Aap jis aalim ko follow karte hain un se poochein.

## Kin cheezon par zakat hai

1. Naqd rakam: ghar, bank, wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa) mein.
2. Sona aur chandi (zewar sameet, aam rai ke mutabiq).
3. Tijarat ka samaan (business stock) jo bechne ke liye ho.
4. Wo qarz jo dusron se wapas milna yaqeeni ho.

## 2.5% ka hisab

Zakat = kul zakatable maal x 2.5 / 100 (yaani 1/40).

**Misal:** Aap ke paas 10,00,000 rupay hain aur 1,00,000 ka qarz abhi dena hai. Zakatable maal 9,00,000. Zakat = 9,00,000 x 0.025 = **Rs 22,500**. Ye tabhi, jab 9,00,000 nisab se zyada ho (chandi ka nisab aaj ki qeemat par nikal kar dekhein).
