Mizanora par pehle se **free Live TV** tha — channel kholo, dekho, agla laga do. Ab uske saath aik naya section hai: **Mizanora IPTV**. Farq sirf itna hai ke wahan sab kuch website par chalta hai, aur yahan aap ki apni **line** hoti hai jo aap ke TV, phone ya MAG box par chalti hai.

Yeh guide un logon ke liye hai jo poochte hain: *plan kya hai, paisa kitna, aur chalega kaise?*

## Aik line ka matlab kya hai

Line ka matlab: aik username + password (Xtream), ya aik MAC address (Stalker). Us line se aap:

- **Live channels** dekh sakte hain — news, sports, Islamic, entertainment, movies, kids.
- **Movies aur series** (VOD) dekh sakte hain — Family aur Premium plans mein.
- **EPG guide** dekh sakte hain — 7 din ka schedule, "ab kya chal raha hai" aur "agla kya hai".

Wohi line TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, Smart TV, MAG box ya browser — jahan chahein daal dein. Device badalna ho to purana band karein, naya chalu.

## Paisa: Rs 50 + Rs 200

| Cheez | Kitna | Kab |
|---|---|---|
| Connection fee | **Rs 50** (pehla month isme shamil) | line banwate waqt, aik dafa |
| Monthly | **Rs 200** | doosre mahine se, har mahine |
| 3 mahine | Rs 550 | ek saath |
| 12 mahine | Rs 1,900 | ek saath |

Plan ke hisaab se:

- **Starter** — Rs 200/mahina, 1 device, HD.
- **Family** — Rs 350/mahina, 2 devices, FHD + movies/series.
- **Premium** — Rs 500/mahina, 3 devices, 4K jahan available ho.
- **Trial** — 24 ghante free, 1 device, koi card nahi.

> **Cap ka rule:** reseller client se Rs 200 mahina se zyada **nahi** le sakta (Starter plan par). System khud rok deta hai — is liye koi aap ko overcharge nahi kar sakta.

## Payment kaise hoti hai

Har mahine ki **1 se 10 tareekh** ke darmiyan apne reseller ko pay karein — JazzCash, Easypaisa ya bank. Reseller apna number panel mein set karta hai, aur wohi number aap ke TV par **recharge screen** par dikhta hai jab line band ho jaye.

Agar 12 tareekh tak payment na aaye to line auto-suspend ho jati hai. Payment ke foran baad wapas chalu — koi naya setup nahi.

## Xtream ya Stalker — kaunsa?

| | Xtream Codes | Stalker / MAC |
|---|---|---|
| Login | username + password | MAC address |
| Apps | TiviMate, Smarters, OTT Navigator | MAG box, STB emulator |
| Device badalna | har app mein naya login | MAC whitelist karni parti hai |
| Sharing | device limit se rokti hai | MAC lock ki wajah se mushkil |

Dono support hain, aur dono ki price same hai. Aam user ke liye **Xtream** aasan hai; purane MAG box walon ke liye **MAC**.

## Free Live TV aur IPTV mein farq

- **`/live` (free):** browser mein khulta hai, koi login nahi, ads chalti hain. Test karne ya kabhi kabhar dekhne ke liye theek.
- **IPTV line:** apni app mein, apne TV par, EPG ke saath, ads nahi, aur poora VOD.

Dono ke channels aik hi list se aate hain — jo admin Live TV mein add karta hai.

## Aam sawal

**Kya card dena parta hai?** Nahi. JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank — jaisa aap ka reseller kehta hai. Trial par kuch bhi nahi.

**Ek line par do TV?** Starter par nahi — Family (2) ya Premium (3) lein.

**Channels legal hain?** Mizanora sirf woh channels rakhta hai jinke liye ijazat hai — apne channels, official/free-to-air streams, ya written distribution rights. Kisi channel ka haq na ho to woh list se hata diya jata hai.

**Line share kar sakta hoon?** Device limit server par lagti hai — teesra device chalane par purana ruk jata hai. Is liye sharing ka faida nahi.
