You do not need a shop or money to start earning online. With a worker program, you share products and earn a commission when the customer receives the order. Here is how to do it well.

## 1. Start with products you understand

Choose products you would happily recommend to a friend. Cheaper, everyday items are easiest to sell at first, and they are also the ones available to new workers.

## 2. Share where people already trust you

- Family and friends WhatsApp groups (do not spam; share 1 or 2 products a day)
- Your WhatsApp status
- A Facebook page or profile with real posts
- Short videos on TikTok, Reels or Shorts showing the product

## 3. Use real photos and honest words

Customers return orders when the product is not what they expected. Describe size, colour and material honestly. A high delivery rate is what moves you up to higher commission levels.

## 4. Follow up with the buyer

Tell the customer to keep the phone on for the confirmation call. Many cancellations happen because nobody answers.

## 5. Track and improve

Check your dashboard weekly. See which products get orders, and share more of those.
