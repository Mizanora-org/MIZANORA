Your trust matters more than any sale. Here is how we keep your account and information safe.

## We ask for the minimum

An order needs your name, phone number and delivery address. We never ask for your card PIN, your OTP code or your banking password. Cash on delivery means you do not enter payment details on our site.

## How login works

Mizanora uses Google's secure sign-in system (Firebase Authentication). You can sign in with your Google account or an email and password. We do not see or store your Google password.

## Your devices

Open **My account** and the **Security and devices** tab. You will see the device you are using, how you signed in and when. If you lose a phone or used a shared phone, press **Sign out everywhere**. Other devices are signed out within about an hour; your orders and addresses stay safe.

## Your data

Your saved addresses, order history and wishlist belong to your account. Only you can read them while signed in. Read the full [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).

## Habits that keep you safe

1. Use a strong, unique password and never share it
2. Never share an OTP code with anyone
3. Sign out on shared phones
4. Open links only from our official website or WhatsApp number
5. If something feels wrong, message us at once
