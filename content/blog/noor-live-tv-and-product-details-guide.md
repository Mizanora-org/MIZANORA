Mizanora mein shopping ke saath tools aur entertainment bhi hain. In features ki availability admin settings, network aur external services par depend karti hai. Yeh guide unhein samajhne mein madad karti hai.

## Noor se sawal poochhna

Assistant enabled ho to button se text ya supported browser mein voice use karein. Aap product dhoondhne ke saath website pages, Asaan Lang ya aam maloomat ke bare mein bhi pooch sakte hain. Jab general AI connection unavailable ho, Noor basic product search ya kuch page links de sakti hai aur bata sakti hai ke general jawab verify nahi ho saka.

AI ki baat ko final source na samjhein. Jahan source link diya jaye wahan check karein; health, law aur religious rulings ke liye qualified professional ya aalim se rujoo karein. Noor aapka private order status khud nahi jaanti: [tracking page](/track) ya [support](/contact) use karein.

## Live TV mein kya milega?

Agar store admin ne [Live TV](/live) enabled ki hai aur authorised channels add kiye hain, guide mein naam ya group search kar sakte hain. Favourites aur recent channels device par save hote hain. Theme selector se Cinema, Emerald, Ocean ya Midnight choose karein. Agar channel ke multiple sources configured hon to Stream selector se backup choose karein; failed stream par player agla source bhi try karta hai.

Network aur broadcaster ki permissions ki wajah se koi link phir bhi na chale to Retry ya Next use karein. YouTube embeds ki failure detection browser se limited ho sakti hai; backup manually select karein. Site par sirf authorised streams hi add karni chahiye.

## Product ki details kaise parhein?

Product page par price, stock, photos, description aur available size ya colour check karein. Admin ne add kiya ho to brand, highlights, package contents, care aur warranty bhi nazar aate hain. Agar koi field khaali ho to us ke bare mein assumption na banayein: [support](/contact) se confirm karein.

Final checkout par delivery charge aur total amount dekhein. [Product photos aur descriptions ki guide](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions) aur [returns information](/returns) bhi order se pehle kaam aa sakti hai.
