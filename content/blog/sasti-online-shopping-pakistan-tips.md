Sasti shopping ka matlab "sirf sasta" nahi hota. Asli sasti shopping wo hai jahan aap **kam paison mein wo cheez lein jo tikay** - warna do mahine baad dobara khareedna parta hai. Ye 12 tips Pakistan ke online market ke liye likhi gayi hain.

## 1. Pehle budget, phir browsing

Apne phone mein ek simple note banao: "is mahine shopping ke liye Rs 5,000". Jab budget likha ho to aap "sasti cheez" ke chakkar mein zaroorat se zyada nahi khareedte. Online shops mein aankhein bhatakti hain - budget hi aap ki brake hai.

## 2. Price compare karne ke liye 3 jagah dekho

- **Shop ki category page** - wahan sab se sasti cheezein sab se pehle lagti hain.
- **Search** - product ka naam likho aur "cheapest" sorting dekho.
- **Assistant** - Mizanora ki assistant Noor se poochein: "Rs 1000 se kam kurtiyan dikhao" - wo poori catalogue se sasti cheezein nikaal deti hai.

> Aik hi product 5 jagah 5 different price par ho sakta hai. 30 second ka compare aap ke Rs 300 se 800 bacha deta hai.

## 3. Deal season ka intezar karo (magar had tak)

Eid, Ramzan, 11.11 aur winter sale mein prices sach much neeche aate hain. Magar yaad rakho: jo cheez aap ko 3 mahine se chahiye thi, usay sale tak rokna theek hai - jo cheez sirf sale dekh kar pasand aayi, wo aap ki bachat nahi, kharcha hai.

## 4. Delivery fee ko total mein shamil karo

Rs 700 ki cheez + Rs 250 delivery = Rs 950. Agar aap ki shop flat delivery leti hai (Mizanora par Rs 250 poore Pakistan par), to 3 chhoti cheezein aik saath order karna zyada samajhdaari hai. Bohat si shops ke paas "free delivery over Rs 5,000" bhi hota hai.

## 5. Photos ko zoom kar ke dekho

Sasti cheez mein usually photo chhoti aur dhondli hoti hai. Zoom karke check karo: stitching, sole, zip, label. Agar shop sirf 1 photo deti hai, WhatsApp par 2-3 aur mangwao - achi shops foran bhej deti hain.

## 6. Size chart par bharosa karo, "free size" par nahi

Sasti cheez wapas karne ka kharcha aap hi uthate hain. Is liye size chart, inches aur model ki height zaroor poocho. Kapron mein 1 inch ka farq poora mood kharab kar deta hai.

## 7. Reviews mein "kitne din chalay" dhoondo

"Bohot acha hai" walay reviews kam faide dete hain. Un reviews ko uthao jahan likha ho "3 mahine use kiya, aisa hua". Asli quality ka faisla wahan se hota hai.

## 8. Cash on delivery ko apna dost banao

COD ka faida: paisa tab jab parcel haath mein ho. Sasti shopping ka asli rule yehi hai - pehle dekho, phir paisa do. Advance sirf un shops ko do jin se aap ne pehle 2-3 baar order kiya ho.

## 9. Bundles aur multi-buy dekho

- 2 shirts aik saath = per piece sasta.
- Accessories (charger + cable + earbuds) aik order mein = delivery bhi aik.
- Ghar ke items kitchen ke saath order karo - delivery zaya nahi jati.

## 10. Warranty aur return policy parh lo

Sasti cheez ka warranty chhota hota hai, is liye use **parh kar** khareedo. "7 din check warranty" ka matlab hai 7 din mein kharabi batani hogi - is liye parcel kholtay hi test karo.

## 11. Save karo aakhri Rs 200 ko bhi

Coupon codes, partner codes aur "one order per phone" wale codes asli bachat dete hain. Checkout se pehle promo code box zaroor dekho. Mizanora par codes sirf tab lagte hain jab customer khud likhe - koi chhupi hui discount nahi hoti.

## 12. Apni pasand ka record rakho

Jo cheez aapko pasand aayi, uska link save karo (wishlist). Mizanora ki assistant bhi yaad rakhti hai aap ko kis qism ki cheezein pasand aati hain, aur home page par **"Aap ke liye"** shelf wahi dikhati hai - bilkul aap ke phone par, koi account banane ki zaroorat nahi.

## Aakhri baat

Sasti shopping = **kam cheezein, achi cheezein, sahi price par**. Ek mahine mein agar aap 5 cheezein kharidte ho jo aap waqai use karte ho, to aap ne 10 "sasti" cheezon se zyada bachaya.
