## What Makes a Movie Public Domain?

A film enters the public domain in one of three legitimate ways:

1. **Expired Copyright Term:** Older cinema landmarks — such as silent-era masterpieces from the 1920s — have completed their statutory copyright term, making them part of humanity's shared cultural heritage.
2. **Unrenewed or Missing Statutory Notice:** Certain mid-20th-century Hollywood studio films entered the public domain upon release or when renewal paperwork was omitted under earlier US copyright rules.
3. **Open Creative Commons Release:** Modern studios such as the Blender Institute fund open movies specifically to share them with the world under **Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)** licenses.
