**BarakatWave TV** is the newest project of **Muaaz Iqbal**, the founder of Mizanora: a modern live TV streaming web application that connects viewers across the Subcontinent - **Pakistan and India** - in one fast, clean platform. It is built for real-time broadcasting and named after two ideas: **Barakat**, a blessing, and **Wave**, the modern digital waves that carry the signal.

## What it does

BarakatWave TV is a live television streaming platform. It curates live channels and serves them to viewers on the web with a sleek, mobile-friendly interface - so people in Pakistan and India can watch their favourite content in one place, without slowness or clutter. The name says the mission: bring **barakat** (blessing) to homes through modern streaming.

## Key features

- **Lightning-fast performance:** optimised for top-tier speed with global CDN delivery through Vercel.
- **Cross-border entertainment:** curated live channels connecting audiences across Pakistan and India.
- **Responsive and modern UI:** a sleek, phone-first interface that works on every device.
- **Secure and scalable:** modern serverless architecture with real-time streaming protocols.

## Tech stack

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript
- **Hosting:** Vercel
- **Version control:** Git and GitHub
- **License:** MIT (open source)
