Naye [Mizanora Live TV](/live) player mein ab woh sab control hai jo pehle sirf baray TV apps mein milta tha. Yeh guide har cheez ko asaan alfaaz mein samjhati hai: **video quality (resolution)**, **audio track**, **captions**, **photo capture** aur **stats**. Sare options player ke neeche dayein taraf **gear (Settings) button** mein hain.

## 1. Video quality — asal resolution khud chunein

Settings kholte hi pehla tab **Quality** ka hai. Wahan do tarah ke options hain:

| Option | Matlab | Kab use karein |
|---|---|---|
| Auto | Player net ki speed dekh kar khud quality badalta hai | Roz marra ke liye sab se behtar |
| High | Sab se oonchi resolution par fix | Wi-Fi tez ho, bari screen ho |
| Balanced | Darmiyani quality par fix | Data bhi bachana hai, tasveer bhi saaf chahiye |
| Data saver | Sab se halki quality | Mobile data khatam honay wala ho, net slow ho |
| 1080p · 720p · 480p... | Isi channel ki **asal resolutions** (bitrate ke saath) | Jab aap bilkul wohi quality chahte hain jo stream bhej raha hai |

Asal resolutions wali list mein jo quality **is waqt screen par chal rahi hai**, uske neeche "Ab chal rahi hai" likha nazar aayega. Aik resolution chunne se woh isi channel ke liye lag jati hai — channel badlein to player wapis aapke Auto/High wale mode par aa jata hai, taake har channel apni behtreen speed se shuru ho.

**Note:** Kuch streams mein sirf aik hi quality hoti hai, aur safari (iPhone) mein browser khud quality chunta hai. Wahan list khali hogi — iska matlab yeh nahi ke kuch kharab hai.

## 2. Audio track — apni zubaan ki awaaz

Buhat se channels aik saath kai zubanom mein awaaz bhejte hain. Settings ke **Awaaz** tab mein:

- Har track us ke naam ke saath aata hai, maslan **Urdu (Stereo)**, **English**, **Arabic**.
- Jis par tick (✓) hai wahi chal raha hai. Doosre par tap karein, awaaz usi lamhe badal jayegi — channel dobara load nahi hota.
- Agar list mein sirf aik awaaz hai to tab par likha hoga ke is channel mein aik hi awaaz hai.

Yeh feature khaas tor par un channels par kaam aata hai jahan commentary ya programme do zubanom mein hota hai. Isi topic par hamari pehli guide [Audio tracks aur video quality ka intekhab](/blog/mizanora-live-tv-audio-tracks-video-quality) bhi parh sakte hain.

## 3. Captions — alfaz screen par

**Captions** tab mein Off ke ilawa woh tamam subtitle tracks aate hain jo stream ke saath bheje gaye hon. Kisi ko chunein aur guftagu ke alfaz tasveer ke neeche nazar aane lagenge. Yeh un logon ke liye behtreen hai jo kam awaaz par dekhte hain ya doosri zubaan seekh rahe hain. Agar list khali hai to is channel ke saath captions bheje hi nahi gaye.

## 4. Photo capture — lamha mehfooz karein

Koi goal, koi khoobsurat manzar, khabar ki surkhi — jo bhi screen par hai:

1. Player ke neeche **camera wala button** dabayein (computer par **C** key bhi chalti hai).
2. Jo tasveer us lamhe player mein hai, woh usi asal resolution mein **PNG photo** ban kar download ho jayegi — file ke naam mein channel ka naam aur size (maslan 1920x1080) likha hota hai.

Kuch protected streams photo capture ki ijazat nahi dete; wahan player saaf bata dega ke is channel ki picture capture allow nahi hai.

## 5. Stream stats — sab kuch live dekhein

Settings > More mein **"Stream stats dikhayein"** on karne se screen ke konay mein aik chhota panel aa jata hai:

- **Resolution** — asal size jo abhi screen par hai, maslan 1280x720 (720p)
- **Quality / Speed (andaza)** — chalne wala level aur aapke net ki estimated speed
- **Buffer** — kitne second ki video aage se tayyar hai
- **Live se peeche** — aap asal live lamhe se kitne second peeche hain
- **Dropped frames, codec, awaaz aur rasta (Direct/Relay)**

Buffering ka masla ho to yeh panel foran wajah bata deta hai: speed kam hai to quality kam kar lein, "Live se peeche" zyada hai to neeche **LIVE** wala laal chip dabate hi aap live edge par wapis aa jayenge.

## 6. Chhoti chhoti tez tricks

- **Swipe:** Mobile par tasveer par dayein/bayein swipe = pichla/agla channel.
- **Keys (computer):** ← → channel, Space play/pause, ↑ ↓ awaaz, M mute, L live edge, C photo, S settings, F full screen, R random.
- **Data bachana hai?** Quality ko Data saver par rakhein — player aap ki yeh pasand yaad rakhta hai.
- **Channel atak jaye to?** Player pehle khud recover karta hai, phir relay rasta azmata hai, phir backup stream. Aap bas **Reload** ya **Agla** daba dein. Load wale masail ki tafseel [fix guide](/blog/live-tv-channels-load-problem-fix-guide) mein hai.

Ab [Live TV](/live) kholein, koi channel lagayein aur gear wala button daba kar khud azmayein — do minute mein saare control hath mein aa jayenge.
