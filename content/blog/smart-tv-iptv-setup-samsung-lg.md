Puraane LED TV par IPTV chalane ka sawal roz aata hai: "mere TV par app hi nahi hai." Sach yeh hai ke **Samsung (Tizen)** aur **LG (webOS)** par app store chhota hai, magar teen aise raste hain jinse kaam ho jata hai.

## Pehle apna TV pehchanlein

- **Samsung**: Tizen OS — 2018 aur baad ke models
- **LG**: webOS — 4.0 se aage
- **Purana TV (2015 se pehle)**: app store band, sirf box lagega

Setting mein **About** se model aur OS version dekh lein.

## Rasta 1 — "Set IPTV" (sab se aasan)

Samsung aur LG dono par milta hai (kuch regions mein miyaad ke saath):

1. TV ke **Apps** mein jayein → "Set IPTV" search karein
2. App kholein → **Add playlist**
3. **M3U URL** daalein (reseller se mila hua)
4. **EPG URL** alag khane mein daalein — warna guide khali rahegi
5. Save → channels load

Note: kuch models par app paid hoti hai (aik dafa), aur us ka interface purana hai. Bohat log isi liye box par shift karte hain.

## Rasta 2 — Box lagana (recommended)

Android TV box (ya Fire Stick) lagane se TV sirf **screen** ban jata hai, aur saara kaam TiviMate karta hai:

| Cheez | Smart TV app | Box + TiviMate |
|---|---|---|
| Channel switch | 2–4 second | ~1 second |
| EPG | kamzor | poora, 7 din |
| Favourites | basic | behtareen |
| Updates | band ho sakti hain | chalti rehti hain |

Box ek dafa ka kharcha hai, magar tajurba behtar. Detail: [TiviMate setup guide](/blog/tivimate-playlist-setup-guide).

## Rasta 3 — Browser (sirf test ke liye)

Smart TV ka browser khol kar M3U link chalane ki koshish na karein — Samsung ka browser bari playlist par crash ho jata hai. Yeh sirf is baat ke liye theek hai ke link zinda hai ya nahi.

## TV ki settings jo zaroor badlein

- **Picture mode** → "Movie" ya "Standard" (Vivid mein rang over ho jate hain)
- **Motion smoothing / Auto Motion Plus** → off (sports mein ajeeb lagta hai)
- **Network → DNS** → auto par chhorein; slow ho to `1.1.1.1` try karein
- **Auto update apps** → off, warna naya version achanak interface badal deta hai

## Aam masle

| Masla | Hal |
|---|---|
| App store mein app nahi | region change karein ya box lagayein |
| Playlist add hoti hai, channel nahi chalta | TV ki speed 5 Mbps se kam |
| EPG khali | EPG URL alag khane mein daalein |
| Awaaz hai, tasveer nahi | picture mode / HDMI cable badlein |
| Roz password mangta hai | app ka cache clear karein |

## Khulasa

- Purana TV ho to **box** behtar hai — kharcha kam, tajurba behtar.
- Naya Samsung/LG ho to "Set IPTV" se shuru karein, pasand na aaye to box.
- EPG URL dena na bhoolein — warna guide khali milegi.
