Har online shop "sasti" likhti hai. Asli sawal ye hai: **price kaise banti hai aur kya uske neeche chhipi hui cheez hai?** Mizanora ka tareeqa khula hai - aur ye guide usi ka hisab hai, taake aap bharose se kharid sakein.

## Price kaise banti hai

Mizanora par price = **supplier ki cost + chhota profit** (Rs 2 se 30 tak sasti cheezon par, Rs 100 tak medium par, aur bari cheezon par Rs 200-500 tak). Ye table shop ke andar likha hua system hai - marketing ka koi jhamela nahi.

Isi liye:
- Aap ko price ke upar "old price" ka jhoota bulan nahi milta.
- Jahan discount hai, wo **sach mein** hai (product ka old price zyada tha).

## Delivery ka hisab

- **Rs 250 flat** poore Pakistan mein (chhote bare order par aik hi rate).
- Bohat si shops delivery profit par chalati hain; yahan flat fee hai taake aap 3 cheezein aik order mein le kar bacha sakein.
- Kuch shopping par **free delivery** ka system bhi hai (agar cart ek had se barh jaye).

## Deal aur bargain: server faisla karta hai

Mizanora ki assistant Noor se aap "rate kam karo" keh sakte ho. Faisla **server** karta hai, wo AI nahi:

1. Pehli dafa maangne par wo pyar se price hi rakhti hai.
2. Dusri dafa par aadhi chhoot.
3. Tisri dafa par poori chhoot - aur wahi final hoti hai.

Chhoot ki had admin (shop owner) set karta hai, aur kabhi bhi us had se neeche **cost + Rs 50** ka rule torta nahi. Is liye deal kabhi nuqsan wali price nahi banti - aur aap ko bharosa hota hai ke deal asli hai.

## Promo codes ka qanoon

- Code **customer khud likhta hai** checkout par. Apne aap kuch apply nahi hota.
- Har code kisi partner (ya campaign) ka hota hai, apni shart ke saath (minimum order, max discount, use limit, expiry).
- "One order per phone" wale codes bhi ho sakte hain (misuse rokne ke liye).
- Cancelled orders khud ba khud counters se nikal jate hain.

## Sasti shopping kaise karein (shop ki taraf se 5 paidar tareeqe)

1. **"Sab se sasti" shelf** - home page par har category ki sasti cheez.
2. **Budget buttons** - "Under Rs 500 / 1000 / 2000" aik tap.
3. **Assistant Noor** - "Rs 1000 se kam sandal dikhao" - poori catalogue se jawab.
4. **Aap ke liye shelf** - aap ki pasand ki categories (jo Noor ne yaad rakhi) ki sasti cheezein. Ye aap ke phone par hi hota hai, koi account nahi.
5. **Aik order mein sab** - delivery aik dafa, bachat Rs 250 tak.

## Kya hum cheepa rahe hain?

Nahi - aur system khud bata deta hai:

- Supplier cost aur hamara profit **public nahi** hota (hack nahi ho sakta), magar price kaisay banti hai, wo upar likha hai.
- Hamara pura catalogue (50,000+ products) **files** se chalta hai, Firebase par bharosa nahi - is liye free quota khatam hone wale din bhi shop chalti rehti hai aur orders nahi rukte.
- Har product ka stock sold-out hone par "Sold out" saaf likha aata hai - jhooti availability nahi.

> Asli sasti shopping = seedhi price, asli deal, aur COD. Jahan in teenon ka intezam ho, wahan bharosa banta hai.
