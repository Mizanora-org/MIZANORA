**Muaaz Iqbal** is a young developer, AI builder and online-business founder from **Kasur, Punjab, Pakistan**. He is the person behind **Mizanora**, a Pakistani online shop with cash on delivery, and several other projects that he designs, codes and runs himself.

- **Main project:** Mizanora, "Shop Smart. Shop Halal"
- **Other projects:** HijriAnchor (a privacy-first Hijri and Gregorian calendar app), Muslim Islam Organization (an Islamic community portal), Ramadan tools, study notes for students

## How he works

Muaaz works the way many modern solo builders do: he writes the product idea in plain words, uses AI tools (Claude, Gemini and others) for drafts, code and data cleaning, and then **tests and decides himself**. The result is a shop that has features usually found in much larger companies:

1. An Urdu-speaking AI assistant called **Noor** that helps customers find products.
2. A **worker programme** where young people earn by sharing products.
3. A **live Quran session**, a **hadith reader** and a **Live TV** page inside the same website.
4. An honest review system where only real customers can review, and every review is read before it is shown.

## His values in business

- **Honest prices and honest words.** No fake countdowns, no invented discounts and no bought reviews.
- **Halal earning.** No interest, no gambling, no cheating.
- **Privacy.** A shop should not ask for more of your data than it needs.
