You already use Google every day. These eleven operators are the difference between "searching" and "finding", and every one of them takes two seconds to type.

## 1. Quotes lock a phrase

`"winter jacket price in pakistan"` returns pages with that exact phrase in that order. Without quotes, Google happily gives you pages about winter, jackets, prices and Pakistan separately.

## 2. Minus removes a word

`kurta -women` or `boots -military`. The minus sign is the fastest way to delete an entire category of useless results, and it is the most under-used operator on this list.

## 3. site: searches one website

`site:mizanora.vercel.app winter jacket` searches only this shop. It works on every site, including marketplaces where the built-in search is weak.

## 4. Two dots make a price range

`laptop 80000..120000` returns results mentioning prices inside that range. It is the single best trick for the [search by price](/blog/search-by-price-not-by-name-pakistan-shopping) method.

## 5. OR widens, and needs to be capitalised

`charger OR adapter 20w`. Lower-case `or` is treated as a normal word, which silently ruins the search.

## 6. related: finds competitors

`related:mizanora.vercel.app`. Genuinely useful for price comparison — it is how you find the other three shops you should be checking.

## 7. filetype: finds documents

`filetype:pdf price list 2026`. Manufacturers publish price lists as PDFs that never appear in normal shopping results, and those PDFs are where the real wholesale numbers live.

## 8. before: and after: limit by date

`winter sale pakistan after:2026-09-01`. Essential for anything seasonal, because Google loves serving last year's sale page.

## 9. Define nothing — just add "price in pakistan"

Adding `price in pakistan` (or `rate`, or `PKR`) to any product query moves results from global stores to Pakistani ones. It is crude and it works.

## 10. Read the result date, not just the headline

Google shows a date on many results. An article about "best power banks" from 2022 is describing products that no longer exist. Check the date before believing a price — this is the same habit as [reading product photos properly](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## 11. Use the shopping and images tabs

The **Images** tab does two jobs at once: it shows you what a product looks like across shops, and it reveals whether every seller is using the same stock photo. Identical photos across ten shops usually means none of them have handled the item. For searching *inside* Mizanora instead, use [smart search](/blog/smart-search-ka-tareeqa-mizanora).

## One warning

Google results include paid ads and affiliate listicles at the top. Neither is evidence that a product is good. Before you trust a recommendation, check whether the page earns a commission from it — that single habit explains a surprising number of "top 10" lists.

For checking whether a shop itself is real, use [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## The 20-second routine

1. `"exact phrase"` for the product
2. `-word` to delete what you do not want
3. `price..price` for your budget
4. `after:2026-01-01` to kill old pages
5. Check the date on whatever you open

That is the whole method, and it works on every search engine, not just Google.
