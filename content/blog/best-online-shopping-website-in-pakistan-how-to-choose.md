There is no single "best" website for everyone. The best online shopping website in Pakistan is the one that is **safe, clear and right for what you need**. Use this checklist.

## 1. Trust signs

- A real contact page and WhatsApp support
- Clear return and delivery information
- A working order tracking page
- Honest product photos from several angles

## 2. Payment

**Cash on delivery** is the safest choice for a new shop, because you pay only after you receive the parcel. See our [cash on delivery guide](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## 3. Delivery

Look for a clear delivery fee and a realistic time. Beware of shops that hide delivery charges until the last step.

## 4. Price honesty

Compare the same product elsewhere, with delivery included. A fake "70% off" is not a deal. Read [what market rate means](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price).

## 5. Help in your language

If you are more comfortable in Urdu or Roman Urdu, choose a store with support in that language. Mizanora's assistant Noor chats and speaks in Urdu.

## 6. Reviews and reputation

Look for specific reviews, not only five-star lines. Search the shop name with the word "reviews" to see real experiences.

## 7. Easy returns

Read the return policy before ordering. If it is hidden or confusing, choose another shop.
