Saving money online is not about finding the lowest number on the screen. It is about paying a fair price for something that arrives on time and lasts. These habits work for almost any product, from a kitchen gadget to a suit.

## Compare the total cost

Add the product price, the delivery fee and any extra charge. A product that looks cheap but carries a high delivery fee or a weak quality can cost more than a fairly priced one. Our guide on [how to compare prices before buying online](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online) shows the full method.

## Wait for a real sale, not a fake one

A "70% off" tag means nothing if the old price was invented. Look at the product over a few days. If the price keeps changing between two numbers, the discount is only a display. Real sale seasons are covered in our guide to the [best time to shop online in Pakistan](/blog/best-time-to-shop-online-pakistan-sale-seasons).

## Use coupon codes carefully

Use codes only from the shop's own pages or official social accounts. Read the conditions: minimum order, valid dates and products that are excluded. Read more in [how to use coupon codes safely](/blog/how-to-use-coupon-codes-safely).

## Make a list before you open the shop

Impulse buying is the biggest hidden cost. Write down what you need, check the budget, and close the app when the list is done. If something extra catches your eye, save it to the wishlist and decide the next day.

## Buy in one order when you can

Each order can carry its own delivery fee. If you need three things, put them in one cart. Where a shop offers free delivery above a certain amount, check whether a small addition brings you over it, but only add something you really need.

## Pay cash on delivery and check the parcel

Cash on delivery means you pay only when the parcel is in your hands. Open it in front of the rider where possible and compare it with the photos. Our [parcel checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check) lists what to look for.

## Quick checklist

- Total cost, not just the product price
- Sale history over a few days
- Coupon conditions read in full
- One order instead of three
- Parcel checked before you pay
