“Yeh movie aap chalate kaise hain?” — yeh sawal kisi bhi movie site par sab se pehle aana chahiye. Mizanora ka jawab saaf hai: **sirf woh content jo chalane ka haq hamare paas hai.** Yeh guide us policy ko aasan lafzon mein kholti hai.

## Char qism ke content

1. **Mizanora ka apna** — product photos, guides, design, blog.
2. **Licensed** — jis ka stream karne ka agreement ya ijazat ho.
3. **Creative Commons** — jahan banane wale ne CC licence ke tehat ijazat di ho.
4. **Public domain** — jis par copyright khatam ho chuka ho (aksar 1920s–1950s classics).

“Free Legal Film” ka badge sirf tab lagta hai jab teen cheezein saath hon: `https://` link, seedhi media file (`.mp4`, `.webm` ya `.m3u8`), aur likha hua licence record. Yeh record movie page par nazar aata hai.

## Play servers ka kirdar

Mizanora koi movie file **host ya upload nahi** karta. Cinema Player un servers se chalta hai jo site admin **Admin > Movies > Play Servers** mein add karta hai. Server add karte waqt admin tasdeeq karta hai ke server us ka apna hai ya us ke paas licence hai.

Is liye jab kisi ko aitraz ho, hum sab se pehle woh server **off** karte hain — phir maloom karte hain ke source kahan se aaya aur aap ko bhi batate hain.

## Trailers aur metadata

- **Trailers**: YouTube ke official embed se. Rights studio ke paas rehte hain.
- **Posters / cast / story**: TMDB (admin ki free key ke saath), TVmaze public API, IMDb public suggestion JSON, aur Internet Archive public-domain collection.
- Mizanora TMDB ka API istemal karta hai magar TMDB se endorsed ya certified nahi.

Kabhi koi poster ya cast photo ghalat aa jati hai. Aap sirf us image ke hataane ki darkhwast bhi kar sakte hain — page rehne dein, tasveer hata dein.

## Ads

Movie pages par chalne wala pre-roll **Mizanora ka apna house ad** hai. Us overlay ke andar koi third-party ad network, tracker ya pop-up nahi load hota. Ads ke products wohi hain jo shop par mojood hain — COD ke saath.

## Takedown — kitni jaldi?

1. Aap email karein <a href="mailto:mezanora.market@gmail.com">mezanora.market@gmail.com</a> par, subject: **Copyright takedown request**.
2. Message mein: URL, aap ka naam/contact, asal kaam ka link, aur ikhtiyar ki tasdeeq.
3. Hum **24 ghante** mein acknowledge karte hain.
4. Saaf case mein **48 ghante** ke andar content ya server off ho jata hai.
5. Aap ko likhit jawab milta hai — kya hataya, kab, aur source kya tha.

Agar content ghalti se hat gaya ho (public domain ya aap ke paas licence), to usi email par saboot bhejein — 7 kaam ke dinon mein wapis laga diya jata hai.

## Aap ke liye ek chhoti aadat

Agar aap bhi content banate hain (YouTube, Reels, blog), to **“rights ka saboot”** rakhein: kahan se liya, kis licence par, kab. Yeh ek screenshot aage chal kar aap ko bachata hai. Mizanora khud isi usool par chalta hai.
