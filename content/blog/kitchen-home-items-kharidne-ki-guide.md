Ghar aur kitchen ka saman wo hai jo roz chalta hai - is liye yahan "sasta" se zyada "tikne wala" ahem hai. Ye guide batati hai ke pehla order kya hona chahiye aur kis cheez par kharcha karna chahiye.

## Pehla order: 6 cheezein jo sab se zyada chalti hain

1. **Storage boxes / air-tight jars** - ata, cheeni, chawal, masalay.
2. **Non-stick frying pan** - roz ka kaam, ye 1 achi lein.
3. **Drain aur tap ka small repair kit** - leak foran theek hota hai.
4. **Cloth drying clips + rope** - chhota kharcha, bara faida.
5. **Light bulbs (LED)** - bijli ka bill kam karte hain.
6. **Toilet cleaning brush set** - safai ka basic.

## Kis cheez par mehnga aur kis par sasta

| Cheez | Sasta le sakte hain | Mehnga lein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chamach, churri, chopper | Haan | Nahi |
| Non-stick pan, pressure cooker | Nahi | Haan |
| Storage boxes | Haan | Nahi |
| Steel utensils (kadhai patila) | Thora sasta | Behtar (lambi chal) |
| Electrical extension board | Nahi - safety first | Haan |

> Kitchen mein sirf 3 cheezein mehngi lein: pan, cooker aur extension board. Baqi sab sasta lo.

## Online kharidne ka tareeqa (step by step)

1. **Measure karo** - kitchen shelf ki width, fridge ki height. Photos mein size likha hota hai.
2. **Material parho** - stainless steel 304, food grade plastic, ya glass. "Heavy" likha ho to behtar hai.
3. **Reviews** - 3 mahine baad wale reviews sab se qeemti hote hain.
4. **Bundle karo** - ghar + kitchen + cleaning, aik order mein: delivery sirf aik baar.
5. **COD** - parcel kholtay hi check: crack, dent, ya missing piece.

## Delivery aur packing

- Glass aur steel items mein **extra packing** ki request WhatsApp par kar dein - achi shops bubble wrap lagati hain.
- Kinaron wale shehr mein 3-5 din normal hai; Eid se pehle 2 hafte tak lag sakte hain, is liye pehle order karo.

## Kitchen ko sasta chalane ke 3 practical tips

- **Seasonal sabzi** kharido, aur unhein air-tight boxes mein rakho (2 din faida).
- Har cheez ka aik hi "jagah" tay karo: dhoondna kam, khareedna kam.
- Bijli wale items (bulb, fan, extension) mein **quality** par kharcha karo - yeh bijli aur safety dono ka masla hai.
