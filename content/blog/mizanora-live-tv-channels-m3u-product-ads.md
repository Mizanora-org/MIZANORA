**Live TV** is a new part of Mizanora. Open [Live TV](/live) and watch the channels the shop owner has added.

## For viewers

- Press **Agla** (Next) or **Pichla** (Previous) to change channel.
- Open **Channels ki list** to search and pick any channel.
- While the next channel loads you may see a short **product ad**: a photo, the name, the real price and a button to open the product. You can skip it after a few seconds.
- If a channel does not work, press Next. Some streams are closed by their owners or refuse to play on other websites.

## For the shop owner

In **Admin > Live TV** you can:

1. Upload an **M3U / M3U8 file**, load an **M3U link**, paste the playlist text or add a channel by hand.
2. Rename, reorder, switch off or delete channels.
3. Choose the **products** whose ads play between channels, and set the ad length and when the viewer may skip.
4. Press **Save**. The page updates within seconds.

## Good to know

- Only **https** streams can play on an https website. Plain http links are skipped.
- Add only streams you are allowed to show: your own, or channels that permit embedding, such as many official YouTube live channels. The owner is responsible for what is added.
- Prices and photos in ads are read from the shop itself, so an ad can never show a wrong price.

## Why ads for products?

The ad time is the natural pause of switching channel. It helps the shop show offers without covering the programme while you watch.
