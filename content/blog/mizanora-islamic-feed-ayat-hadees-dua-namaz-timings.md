Shopping should not take you away from what matters. That is why Mizanora added an **Islami Feed** to the home page.

## What you will see

- **Ayat:** short verses of the Quran with Urdu meaning and the surah and ayah number
- **Ahadees:** well-known hadith with the book and number
- **Duas and aqwal:** supplications and sayings with their source
- **Hijri date:** today's Islamic date
- **Next Islamic occasion:** a countdown to events like Ramadan or Eid, shown as approximate
- **Namaz timings:** the five daily prayers for Kasur, using the Karachi method with Hanafi Asr

## How it works

Swipe through the cards. Tap a tab to filter ayat, hadees or dua. Use **Copy** or **Share** to send a reminder to family on WhatsApp. The first card changes every day. In the cold months, winter-related reminders come first.

## Accuracy and care

Every text comes with a reference. The Hijri date follows the Umm al-Qura calendar with a Pakistan adjustment, so it can differ by a day from moon sighting. Prayer times are approximate, so please prefer your local masjid's timetable. For any question of fiqh, ask a qualified scholar.

## Why on a shopping site?

Because honest business is part of faith. Read [honest trade in Islam](/blog/halal-business-and-honest-trade-in-islam-for-online-shops) to see how the teachings apply to online shops.
