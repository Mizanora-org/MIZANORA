Pakistan mein tareekh ka sawal aksar do jawabon ke sath aata hai: *"Angrezi tareekh se 12 tareekh,
aur Hijri se 1 Ramadan."* Dono sahi hain — masla yeh hai ke dono ek jagah nazar nahi aate.
**HijriAnchor** isi masle ke liye bana: ek screen par **Hijri (Umm al-Qura)** aur **Gregorian**
tareekh, sath sath, bina internet.

App: [mizanoraapp.com](https://mizanoraapp.com) · Android + iOS.

## Teen asool, teen features

### 1. Offline pehle
Calendar ko internet ki zaroorat nahi honi chahiye. HijriAnchor ka calendar data app ke andar hai —
flight mode mein bhi tareekhein, mahine aur conversions kaam karte hain. Yeh un logo ke liye ahem hai
jin ka data pack mahine ke aakhir mein khatam ho jata hai.

### 2. Privacy
Na account, na login, na contacts ki demand, na ads. Jo cheez aap ke phone mein hai woh aap ki hai.
Isi wajah se app "privacy-first, ad-free" keh kar ship hui hai — aur wahi likha hai store page par bhi.

### 3. Dual calendar, ek nazar mein
| Cheez | Faida |
| --- | --- |
| Hijri + Gregorian grid | dono tareekhein ek hi mahine ke box mein |
| Umm al-Qura hisaab | Saudi/Umm al-Qura ke mutabiq mahine |
| Aaj ka din highlight | "aaj" ka confusion khatam |
| Ramadan/Eid planning | roza aur Eid ki tareekhein pehle se pata |
| Reminders | ahem dinon ke liye yaad-dehani |

## Kis kaam ke liye sab se zyada istemal hota hai

- **Roza aur Ramadan:** Sehri/Iftar ka mahina plan karna, 30 rozay ginte rehna.
- **Safar aur ijlas:** Hijri tareekh se meeting ya nikah ki tareekh tay karna.
- **Zakat/accounting:** Saal ka hisaab Hijri saal par hota hai — dono calendars sath dekh kar ghalti nahi hoti.
- **Students:** Islamic studies mein tareekhein convert karna.

## Kyun "Anchor"?

Kyunki calendar ek **anchor** hai: baqi sab cheezein (rozay, tareekhein, reminders, planning) usi par
tiki hain. Agar calendar galat, to sab kuch hil jata hai. Isi liye app ka pehla target **sahi
tareekh, offline, bina shor** hai — extra features baad mein.

## Aage kya aa sakta hai

- Hijri tareekh se reminder (maslan "har Hijri mahine ki 1 tareekh").
- Family sharing (ek hi calendar, kai phone).
- Widgets: home screen par aaj ki dono tareekhein.

Yeh sab user feedback par depend karta hai. App store par review chhor dein ya
[Mizanora contact](/contact) se likhein — har message padha jata hai.
