Mizanora Live TV ka sab se bara update aa gaya hai. Purana player aur purani channel list dono **poori tarah rebuild** kar diye gaye hain. Ab [Live TV](/live) kholte hi aapko aik bara saaf player aur us ke neeche **rangon walay khoobsurat channel cards ka grid** milta hai — bilkul usi andaaz mein jo baray Live TV apps mein hota hai. Har channel ka apna rang, apna logo, LIVE badge aur one-tap play.

## Naya channel grid — pehle se kitna mukhtalif?

- **Har channel aik card hai:** logo beech mein, naam neeche, group chhota sa likha hua, upar LIVE ki surkh patti.
- **Jo channel chal raha hai** us par sunehri (golden) frame aur harkat karti hui awaaz-ki-lakerein (equalizer) nazar aati hain — door se pata chal jata hai kya chal raha hai.
- **Dil wala button** har card par hai: pasandeeda channels save karein, phir upar **♥ Favourites** chip se sirf wahi dekh lein.
- **Search** pehle se tez hai, saath mein **Sort (Naam A–Z / Group)** aur **Grid ya List** view ka button bhi aa gaya hai.
- Scroll karte jayen, channels khud load hotey jayenge — hazaron channels hon to bhi page halka rehta hai.
- Card par ungli ya mouse rakhtay hi us channel ka connection **pehle se garam** ho jata hai, is liye tap karne par tasveer foran aati hai.

## Apna custom player — chhota sa cinema

Player ab browser ke puranay controls par nahi chalta. Apna khaas control bar hai jo dekhte waqt khud ghayib ho jata hai aur hath lagate hi wapis aa jata hai:

- Bara **play/pause** button, **Pichla/Agla** channel, awaaz ka slider, full screen, picture-in-picture.
- **LIVE chip** batata hai aap live se kitne peeche hain (maslan -12s). Usko tap karein to foran live edge par wapis.
- Mobile par tasveer par **left/right swipe** karne se channel badal jata hai.
- Phone ki lock screen par bhi channel ka naam aur next/previous control aata hai, aur dekhte hue screen khud band nahi hoti.
- Gear (⚙) wala **Settings** button: Quality, Awaaz, Captions aur More — sab kuch aik jagah.

## Quality, awaaz aur capture — poora control aapke hath mein

Is update ki sab se bari baat yeh hai ke ab player stream ki **asal resolutions khud capture kar ke dikhata hai**: Settings mein 1080p, 720p, 480p ke saath unki bitrate bhi likhi hoti hai. Jo chahen chun lein, ya Auto par chorein to player net ke hisaab se khud behtreen chunta hai. Jin channels mein aik se zyada **audio tracks** (Urdu, English, Arabic...) ya **captions/subtitles** hote hain, unki list bhi wahin milti hai. Aur **camera button** dabate hi jo tasveer screen par hai woh photo ki surat mein save ho jati hai. Tafseel ke liye hamari [Quality, audio track aur capture guide](/blog/live-tv-video-quality-audio-track-capture-guide) parhein.

Jo log technical tafseel pasand karte hain, unke liye **Stream stats** panel bhi hai — mojuda resolution, speed ka andaza, buffer, live se peeche kitne hain, dropped frames aur codec — sab live.

## Admins ke liye: M3U aur panel import ke bare fixes

Kai logon ka masla tha ke Admin > Live TV mein **M3U link ya Xtream panel se channels load nahi ho rahe thay**. Is update mein iski teen asal wajoohat dhoond kar theek kar di gayi hain: "Straight into the shop" wala rasta jo kabhi chalta hi nahi tha, group chunne par zero channels aana, aur playlist links ka redirect par ruk jana. Ab http/https dono tarah ke playlist links, redirects, 32 MB tak ki baray playlists — sab chalta hai, aur har button hamesha screen par nazar aata hai. Poori kahani aur step-by-step tareeqa [Channels load nahi ho rahe? Fix guide](/blog/live-tv-channels-load-problem-fix-guide) mein hai.

## Kya purana sab kuch waisa hi hai?

Ji — jo cheezen pehle se thin woh sab maujood hain: favourites, recent channels, dead channel khud skip hona, backup streams, har channel ka apna page ([channel pages wali guide](/blog/mizanora-live-tv-all-channels-search-favourites) dekhein), channel badalte hue apni dukaan ke product ads, aur wohi purana bharosay wala relay jo mana karne wale streams ko bhi chala deta hai. Audio tracks par hamari pehli guide [Mizanora Live TV mein audio tracks aur video quality](/blog/mizanora-live-tv-audio-tracks-video-quality) bhi naye player ke hisaab se taza kar di gayi hai.
