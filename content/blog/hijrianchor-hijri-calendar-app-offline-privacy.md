**HijriAnchor** is a calendar and life planner app made by [Muaaz Iqbal](/muaaz-iqbal) in Kasur, Pakistan. Its idea is simple: show the Islamic (Hijri) date and the normal (Gregorian) date side by side, and keep your plans in the same place.

## Why people want a dual calendar

Muslims plan many things by the Hijri calendar: Ramadan, Eid, the white days, Muharram, Hajj. Work, school and bills follow the Gregorian calendar. Switching between two apps is tiring. A dual calendar shows both at once.

## What HijriAnchor offers

- **Umm al-Qura Hijri dates** synchronised with the Gregorian calendar.
- **Scenario planning:** plan a day or a project against both calendars.
- **100% offline:** it works without internet.
- **Privacy first:** no telemetry and no tracking.
- **Ad-free:** nothing interrupts you.
- Available for **Android (Google Play)** and **iOS (App Store)**. More at [mizanoraapp.com](https://mizanoraapp.com).

## Note on moon sighting

Calendar calculations are an aid. In Pakistan the start of a month follows the local moon sighting decision, so always confirm important dates (Ramadan, Eid) with the official announcement.
