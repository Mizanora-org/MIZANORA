Mizanora Live TV ka naya player ab pehle se zyada fast, smooth aur powerful ho gaya hai. Ab koi bhi stream — chahe woh MP4 ho, M3U8 ho ya MPD (DASH) — player mein chal sakti hai.

## Kya naya hai is player mein

- **Har stream type support:** Ab player MP4 video, M3U8 (Apple HLS) aur MPD (MPEG-DASH) teeno ko support karta hai. Chahe aapke paas koi bhi stream link ho, ab woh chal jayega.
- **Audio tracks:** Agar koi stream mein zyada than ek audio language ho (jaise Urdu, English, Arabic), to ab aap player mein se apni pasand ki awaaz chun sakte hain.
- **Video quality:** Ab player mein video quality ka intekhab maujood hai — Auto, High, Balanced ya Data Saver. Apni internet ki raftar ke mutabiq quality chunein.
- **DASH (MPD) streams:** Ab MPEG-DASH streams bhi chal sakti hain, jin mein adaptive bitrate streaming hoti hai aur video kabhi nahi ruki.
- **Smooth aur fast playback:** Player ab zyada smooth hai, buffering kam hai aur channel badalne ki raftar pehle se tez hai.

## Kaise istemal karein

1. [Live TV](/live) kholein.
2. Koi bhi channel chunein.
3. Player ke neeche diye gaye options se **Video quality** aur **Audio language** (agar available ho) chunein.
4. Full screen, Picture-in-Picture aur share ka option bhi maujood hai.

## Shop owner ke liye

Admin panel mein **Live TV** section se M3U playlist, M3U link ya ek-ek karke channels add karein. Ab aap MP4, M3U8 aur MPD teeno tarah ke streams add kar sakte hain. Har channel ke liye backup streams bhi rakhe ja sakte hain taake agar ek link na chale to dusra automatically chal jaye.

Sirf wohi streams shamil karein jin ko dikhane ka aapko haq hai.
