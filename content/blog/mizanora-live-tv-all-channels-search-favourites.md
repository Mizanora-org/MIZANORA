[Live TV](/live) ka naya design aur naye features.

## Kya naya hai

- **Channel groups:** News, Islamic, Pakistan, Kids aur dusre. Ek tap par filter.
- **Search:** channel ka naam likhein, list foran chhoti ho jati hai.
- **Favourites:** dil ka button dabayein. Aap ke pasandeeda channels is device par mehfooz rehte hain.
- **Random aur Next/Previous:** keyboard par left/right arrow, R random, F full screen.
- **Auto-skip:** agar koi channel nahi chal raha to 4 second baad agla khud lag jata hai.
- **Share:** har channel ka apna link hota hai, jaise `/live/channel-name`. WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain.

## Shop ke saath

Channel badalte waqt Mizanora ke products ka chota ad chalta hai. Qeemat hamesha dukaan se aati hai.

## Owner ke liye

Admin > Live TV mein M3U file, M3U link ya iptv-org ki public lists (Pakistan, Islamic, News, Kids, Sports) ek tap se load hoti hain. Sirf wohi streams rakhein jin ko dikhane ka aap ko haq hai.
