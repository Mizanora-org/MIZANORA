A winter gift does not have to be expensive. A warm, useful gift shows you care. Here are ideas under Rs 2,000.

## Under Rs 500

- A pair of warm socks
- A woollen cap or muffler
- Gloves for riding or walking

## Rs 500 to Rs 1,000

- A soft shawl or scarf
- A thermal inner
- A travel mug or a flask

## Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000

- A fleece blanket or a light razai
- A hoodie or a sweater
- A winter bag or a warm slipper set
- A small gift set made of several of the items above

## How to choose

1. Think about the person: do they walk, ride a bike, study late?
2. Pick the right size, or choose items with flexible sizing like shawls
3. Choose one good item rather than many weak ones
