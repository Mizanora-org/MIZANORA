## Landmark Pakistani Feature Films

### 1. The Legend of Maula Jatt (2022)
Bilal Lashari's grand Punjabi epic [The Legend of Maula Jatt](/movies/the-legend-of-maula-jatt) set box-office records worldwide. Starring [Fawad Khan](/movies/actor/fawad-khan) as Maula Jatt, [Hamza Ali Abbasi](/movies/actor/hamza-ali-abbasi) as Noori Natt and [Mahira Khan](/movies/actor/mahira-khan) as Mukkho Jatti, it raised the bar for production design and action choreography in South Asia.

### 2. The Glassworker (2024)
Directed by Usman Riaz at Karachi's Mano Animation Studios, [The Glassworker](/movies/the-glassworker) is Pakistan's first hand-drawn animated feature film. Featuring Urdu voice work with [Sajal Aly](/movies/actor/sajal-aly), it tells a poignant tale of craftsmanship, music and friendship.

### 3. Khuda Kay Liye (2007) & Punjab Nahi Jaungi (2017)
Shoaib Mansoor's [Khuda Kay Liye](/movies/khuda-kay-liye) revived theatrical cinema in Pakistan with gripping courtroom and musical drama, while Nadeem Baig's [Punjab Nahi Jaungi](/movies/punjab-nahi-jaungi) starring [Humayun Saeed](/movies/actor/humayun-saeed) and [Mehwish Hayat](/movies/actor/mehwish-hayat) brought families back to cinemas in droves.
