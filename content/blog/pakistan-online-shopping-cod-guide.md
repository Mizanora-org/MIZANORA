Pakistan mein online shopping ka sab se bara bharosa **cash on delivery** hai — aur sab se bara
risk bhi. Yeh guide usi risk ko kam karne ke liye hai.

## 1. COD: faida aur nuqsan

**Faida:** paisay tab dete hain jab saman hath mein ho. **Nuqsan:** delivery wala jaldi mein hota
hai, aap parcel khol kar nahi dekhte, aur baad mein return ka jhanjhat.

Checklist (parcel lete waqt):

- [ ] Outer packing sahi hai? (phati/geeli ho to wapas)
- [ ] Weight theek lagta hai? (bohot halka = ghlt item)
- [ ] **Khol kar dekhein** — COD mein aksar allowed hota hai; rider se pooch lein.
- [ ] Bill/invoice andar hai?
- [ ] Return policy likhi hui hai?

## 2. Size: sab se zyada return isi wajah se

| Cheez | Tareeqa |
| --- | --- |
| Kurta/shirt | Apne purane pasandeeda kurte ki **chest** naap lein, sirf "L" par bharosa na karein |
| Shoes | Har brand ka size alag — cm mein naap lein |
| Jeans | Waist + length, dono |
| Bachon ke kapre | Umar se zyada **height** dekhein |

Aur: product page par size chart ho to wohi dekhein, kisi doosri site ka nahi.

## 3. Fake reviews pehchanne ka tareeqa

- Sab reviews **ek hi din** ke hain? Shaq karein.
- Sirf "good product" likha hai, kuch detail nahi? Woh review nahi, script hai.
- 5 star ke sath photo nahi? Aksar fake.
- Achi site woh hai jo **kam** reviews dikhaye magar asli.

Mizanora par reviews sirf khareedar de sakta hai (order ID se), isi liye numbers kam magar sach hote
hain.

## 4. Return policy: 3 sawal pooch lein

1. Kitne din mein return?
2. Wapasi ki delivery kaun deta hai?
3. Paisay kab milte hain (cash/wallet/bank)?

Jo site in teeno ka jawab page par nahi deti, wahan se mehnga saman na khareedein.

## 5. Price compare ka tareeqa

- **Per unit** dekhein (500g vs 1kg).
- Delivery charge shamil karein.
- "Sale" ka matlab hamesha sasta nahi — purani price check karein.
- COD charge ho to woh bhi count karein.

## 6. Safe payment

- COD sab se safe hai (jab parcel dekh lein).
- Advance payment sirf **pehchani hui** site par.
- Kisi ke personal account mein paise na bhejein.
- Screenshot + order ID save karein.

## 7. Order track karna

Mizanora par order ID (`MZ-XXXXXXXX`) aur phone number se tracking hoti hai:
[track order](/track). Status: placed → confirmed → shipped → delivered.

## 8. Kya kharidna chahiye, kya nahi

| Online theek | Store se behtar |
| --- | --- |
| Kapre (size chart ke sath) | Joota (fitting) |
| Electronics (warranty ke sath) | Shaadi ka lehnga |
| Books, tools, kitchen | Fresh sabzi/doodh |
| Gifts | Bhaari furniture (delivery risk) |

## Aakhir mein

Online shopping ka asool wohi hai jo dukan ka: **pehchan, phir paisa.** COD isi liye hai. Reviews
padhein, size chart dekhein, return policy parhein — teen minute ki mehnat hazaron rupay bacha deti hai.
