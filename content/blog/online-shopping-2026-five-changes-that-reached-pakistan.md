Every year brings a list of "trends that will change everything". Most of them do not reach a shopper in Kasur. Here is an honest 2026 scorecard: five changes that genuinely arrived in Pakistan, and two that were widely predicted and simply did not happen.

## What actually changed

### 1. Discovery moved into conversations

A growing number of shoppers now start with a question asked in plain language rather than a keyword typed into a box. A 2026 report found **58% of consumers have replaced traditional search with generative AI tools** for product recommendations ([nShift, 2026](https://nshift.com/blog/agentic-commerce-ai-shopping-agents-2026)), and traffic arriving from AI-powered search converted **about nine times better** than traffic from social media during the 2025 holiday season ([Salesforce, via Entagl](https://www.entagl.com/blog/agentic-commerce-2026-ai-shopping-agents)).

For a Pakistani shopper this mostly means it is now worth *asking* rather than guessing the right English keyword — the same idea as [smart search in Roman Urdu](/blog/smart-search-ka-tareeqa-mizanora).

### 2. Logistics got genuinely faster

Next-day and two-day delivery became normal in the big cities, and last-mile networks pushed much deeper into smaller towns. Just as important for sellers, logistics providers began releasing a share of cash-on-delivery value to merchants at pickup rather than after one to two weeks ([industry round-up, 2026](https://www.tycorun.com/blogs/industry-insights/top-10-e-commerce-companies-in-pakistan)).

Fewer days of waiting for a parcel is the change shoppers actually feel.

### 3. Mobile wallets became real alternatives

JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay and SadaPay are now credible options rather than curiosities, and digital payment volumes in Pakistan grew **77% year-on-year to PKR 124.7 trillion** in FY2023, per State Bank of Pakistan figures ([reported 2026](https://www.grand-review.com/article/pakistans-e-commerce-boom-2026-digital-payments-online-marketplaces-and-the-shop-mtnepoce)).

Note the split, though: enormous growth in digital payments overall, and still overwhelmingly cash for online orders. Both are true. See point 6 below.

### 4. Small sellers started selling nationwide

A shop in one city can reach the whole country, and more of them do. For shoppers this is good news (more choice, better prices) and it makes [checking whether a shop is real](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan) more important than ever, because "nationwide" includes sellers who will not be there next month.

### 5. Social commerce became a normal way to buy

Buying through Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp is no longer unusual. It is also where most scams live. If you buy this way, use [how to shop from reels and videos safely](/blog/how-to-shop-from-reels-and-videos-safely) and never pay an unfamiliar seller in advance — [cash on delivery vs advance payment](/blog/cash-on-delivery-vs-advance-payment-pakistan) explains why.

## What did not change

### 6. Cash on delivery is still the default — by a mile

This was supposed to be the year digital payments overtook cash. It was not close. Across roughly 170,000 Pakistani orders over twelve months (June 2025 – May 2026), **94.5% were cash on delivery and 1.6% were digitally prepaid** ([OrderNation, 2026](https://www.ordernation.com/blogs/research/state-of-online-shopping-pakistan-2026)). Pakistan's own draft National E-Commerce Policy 2.0 (2025–2030) still describes COD as the default.

If you take one thing from this article, take this: for a Pakistani shopper, [cash on delivery](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) is not old-fashioned. It is the safest option available and the market has voted with 94.5%.

### 7. Trust is still the bottleneck

Everything predicted that better logistics and more choice would fix trust. They did not, because trust is not a logistics problem. It is about whether the item that arrives matches the photo, whether the size is right, and whether a real person answers when something goes wrong.

That is why the same three habits keep showing up in every guide on this blog: check the photo, check the size, and check the parcel at the door — [how to check product quality](/blog/how-to-check-product-quality-before-you-order-online), [how to choose the right size](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online) and [the parcel checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check).

## What to do with this

For a shopper, the 2026 version of shopping smart is unglamorous:

1. Use the faster logistics — but order early enough that a delay does not matter.
2. Use wallets where you trust the seller, cash on delivery everywhere else.
3. Ask in your own language instead of guessing English keywords.
4. Verify any seller you found on social media before paying.
5. Keep using the three checks. They have survived every trend so far.
