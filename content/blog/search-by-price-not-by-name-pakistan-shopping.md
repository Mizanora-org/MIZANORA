Here is the most common wasted hour in Pakistani online shopping: you search for a product, scroll through forty results, find one you like, discover it costs twice what you wanted to spend, and start again.

There is a better order. **Search your budget first, the product second.** This guide explains why it works and how to do it.

## Why name-first search wastes your time

A name search returns everything with that name, in whatever order the shop's ranking likes. Price is invisible until you open each result. So you spend attention on items you were never going to buy, and the cheapest suitable item is often on page three.

A price-banded search flips the filter: you only ever look at things you can actually afford.

## The three bands that work in Pakistan

Instead of a precise number, think in bands. These are the ones that map to how people actually shop:

| Band | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Under Rs 1,000** | Small gifts, accessories, kitchen bits, impulse buys |
| **Rs 1,000 – 3,000** | Everyday clothing, shoes, home items, most mobile accessories |
| **Rs 3,000 – 10,000** | Winter outerwear, better electronics, a proper gift |

Pick the band before you open the shop. Everything after that is faster.

## How to do it on any shop

1. **Open the deals or category page first**, not the search box. On Mizanora that is [/deals](/deals) and [/products](/products).
2. **Sort by price, low to high**, then scroll to where your band starts.
3. **Filter by category** inside the band, so you are choosing between things you can afford, not between things you cannot.
4. **Only then look at names and photos.**

Our own deals page is built for exactly this: [how the deals page works](/blog/mizanora-deals-page-biggest-discounts-pakistan).

## The trap inside price-first shopping

Price-first has one real danger: you buy the cheapest thing in the band instead of the best value in the band. Those are different.

The correction is to ask one question for each candidate: **how long will this last?** A Rs 900 item that lasts three months is worse than a Rs 2,200 item that lasts three years. The arithmetic is in [cheap vs quality](/blog/cheap-vs-quality-how-to-judge-price-pakistan).

> Buy once, cry once. The cheapest option is only cheapest at the moment you pay.

## When price-first is the wrong approach

- **When fit matters.** For clothing, size and cut beat price every time. Use [the size guide](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online) first, price second.
- **When it is electronics with a safety component.** A charger or a power bank is not the place to buy the cheapest option — see [buying electronics safely](/blog/how-to-buy-electronics-online-safely-pakistan).
- **When the item is a gift.** The recipient will not remember that you saved Rs 400.

## A note on "market rate"

Before you set your band, it helps to know what the item actually costs in the market. Otherwise your band may be built on a price you saw in 2023. The quick method is in [what is market rate](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price) and [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).

## Put it together

Budget → band → category → photo → size → order. Six steps, and the first three take twenty seconds. Everything else in online shopping is the same, whichever shop you use.
