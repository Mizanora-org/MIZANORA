Mizanora is a market, so it needs tools for the people who buy and sell in it. These four tools run in your browser, cost nothing and keep your numbers on your own device.

## 1. COD Order Profit Calculator

Cash on delivery orders look profitable until parcels come back. The [COD Order Profit Calculator](/tools/cod-order-profit-calculator) takes your price, cost, packing, delivery, return delivery, ad cost and return rate, and shows your real profit and the **break-even return rate**: the percentage of refused parcels at which you earn nothing.

## 2. Price per Unit Comparison

Is the 1 kg pack really cheaper than two 500 g packs? Use the [Price per Unit Comparison](/tools/price-per-unit-comparison). It converts everything to a price per 100 g, per 100 ml or per piece and tells you which pack wins.

## 3. Khata: Udhaar Ledger

The [Khata tool](/tools/khata-udhaar-ledger) is a simple credit book. Add a name and an amount, choose whether they owe you or you owe them, and see the total to receive and to pay. Everything is saved only in your browser.

## 4. Image Compressor and Resizer

Heavy photos make shops slow. The [Image Compressor](/tools/image-compressor-resizer) shrinks a photo to a width and quality you choose and lets you download it. The photo is never uploaded.
