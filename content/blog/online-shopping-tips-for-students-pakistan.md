Students have small budgets and big needs: clothes, bags, mobile accessories, stationery and gifts. These tips help you shop smart.

## 1. Set a monthly budget

Decide the amount before you open the app. Use price filters to stay inside it.

## 2. Use cash on delivery

When you are unsure about a shop, cash on delivery protects you because you pay after you see the parcel. Read [cash on delivery in Pakistan](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## 3. Beware of scams

Never share OTP codes. Do not pay in advance to unknown pages on social media. See [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## 4. Buy useful, not trendy

Ask: will I use it every week? A good bag or a pair of shoes is better than five cheap items you never use.

## 5. Compare before you buy

Check the price in two places with delivery included. Read [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).

## 6. Split orders with friends

Ordering together can reduce delivery cost per person. Make sure each person pays their own share.

## 7. Learn while you shop

Notice how product pages are written, how photos are taken and how prices are set. This is real marketing education.

## 8. Earn a little

Students can join the [Mizanora worker program](/workers), share links with family and friends and earn commission on delivered orders. Keep study first and be honest with your customers.

## Final tip

Never spend money you need for fees or food. If an offer feels rushed, wait a day.
