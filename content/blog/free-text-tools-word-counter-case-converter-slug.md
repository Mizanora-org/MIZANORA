## Word Counter

Essay ya post ki hadd (limit) check karni ho to [Word Counter](/tools/word-counter) words, characters, sentences aur reading time batata hai. Urdu text bhi chalta hai. Reading time taqreeban 200 words fi minute se nikala jata hai.

## Case Converter

[Text Case Converter](/tools/text-case-converter): UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case aur kebab-case. Programmers ke variable names aur headlines dono ke liye.

## URL Slug Generator

SEO friendly address chota, lowercase aur hyphen wala hota hai. Misal: "Best Winter Shoes for Men 2026" ka slug `best-winter-shoes-for-men-2026`. [Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator) ye ek second mein bana deta hai.
