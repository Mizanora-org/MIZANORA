Pakistan mein Quran online padhne wale log do cheezein chahte hain: **Urdu tarjuma** saaf ho, aur
**tilawat** aisi ho ke ayat ke sath chale. Mizanora ka [Quran section](/quran) isi par bana hai.

## Kya maujood hai

| Cheez | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| Surahs | Sab 114, apne apne page par |
| Tarjuma | Urdu (aur doosri zabanein bhi) |
| Tilawat | Ayah-by-ayah, qari chunne ka option |
| Audio | Ayat ke sath highlight — jo padha ja raha hai wohi screen par |
| Sitemap | `/sitemaps/quran.xml` — har surah indexable |

Har surah ka apna URL: `/quran/36-yaseen` is tarah. Iska matlab Google par "surah yaseen urdu
tarjuma" search karne wale ko seedha page milta hai — app install ki zaroorat nahi.

## Ayah-by-ayah tilawat kaise kaam karti hai

1. Surah page kholein.
2. Qari chunein (Mishary Alafasy, Sudais, Abdul Basit…).
3. Play dabayein — jis ayah ki tilawat ho rahi hai, wohi highlight hoti hai.
4. Rukna ho to pause; agle ayah par jane ke liye skip.

Yeh cheez bachon ko padhane aur hifz revise karne ke liye kaam ki hai: aankh aur kaan ek sath.

## Ramadan ke tools

- [Sehri/Iftar countdown](/islamic) — apne shehar ke hisaab se.
- [Daily Ayah](/islamic) aur [Hadith reader](/hadith) — roz ka chhota amal.
- **Live Quran channel** — [Live TV](/live) section mein tilawat ka channel.

## Phone par behtar tajurba

- Page halka hai (koi bara video auto-load nahi hota).
- Audio sirf tab load hota hai jab play karein — data bachta hai.
- PWA install karein to home screen se seedha khul jata hai.

## Padhne ka tareeqa (jo asar karta hai)

Roz 10 minute, ek hi surah, tarjuma ke sath — 30 din mein 30 surah ka matlab samajh aa jata hai.
Isi liye Mizanora par har surah ke sath **short intro** aur **theme** bhi hoti hai, sirf Arabic text
nahi.

## Zakat, roza aur namaz ke tools

Quran ke sath sath Mizanora par kaam ke tools bhi hain:

- **Zakat calculator** — sona, chandi, cash, business stock.
- **Namaz timings** — shehar ke hisaab se.
- **Hijri calendar** — [HijriAnchor app](https://mizanoraapp.com) aur site par tareekh.

Sab free, bina login.
