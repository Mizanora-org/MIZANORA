IPTV apne aap mein **qanooni technology** hai — internet par TV dekhna koi jurm nahi. Jo cheez qanoon banati ya todti hai woh hai **content ka haq (rights)**: aap kisi channel ko us ke maalik ki ijazat ke baghair re-broadcast karein, to yeh violation hai. Yeh post isi farq ko saaf karti hai, kyunki Pakistan mein 90% log "IPTV band ho gaya" ka matlab galat samajhte hain.

## 3 alag cheezein, 3 alag qanoon

| Cheez | Kaun dekhta hai | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| **Licensing / content rights** | PEMRA | Channel ya programme distribute karne ke liye ijazat zaroori |
| **Illegal content block karna** | PTA | Website/app Pakistan mein block ho sakti hai |
| **Internet par streaming** | — | Khud technology legal hai |

Is liye jab koi provider kahe "PTA ne band kar diya", asal mein yeh hua ke **us ka source block hua** — aap ka internet kharab nahi hua.

## Konsa connection "safe" kehlata hai

Teen categories hain, aur farq sirf source ka hai:

1. **Free-to-air (FTA) channels** — PTV, AJK TV, kuch news channels. Ye apni official website ya YouTube par khud live hote hain. Inhein embed karna normal hai.
2. **Authorised distribution** — provider ne channel ke saath contract kiya ho. Aap ko bill milta hai, channel ka logo, EPG, aur customer support hoti hai.
3. **Pirated restream** — kisi ka DTH box, card sharing, ya leaked playlist. Sab se sasta, sab se pehle band, aur qanooni tor par sab se bara masla.

Mizanora ka **Live TV** hissa sirf FTA aur authorised feeds par chalta hai — isi liye woh kabhi "band" nahi hota aur aap ke ghar ka internet slow nahi karta.

## Provider se poochne wale 6 sawal

Kharidne se pehle yeh poochein. Jo jawab na de, wohi aap ko chhor dega:

- Channels ka source kya hai — FTA, contract, ya kisi ka account?
- **Bill ya invoice** milta hai? (WhatsApp par "bhai paise bhej do" kafi nahi)
- Payment kahan jaati hai — **company ka JazzCash/Easypaisa/Bank** ya kisi shakhs ke personal number?
- Line **suspend** hoti hai to kitne ghante mein wapis chalti hai?
- EPG aur catch-up hai, ya sirf live?
- Aap ka **data** kahan jaata hai — koi app aap ka password maang raha hai?

## Pakistan mein 3 practical baatein

- **Pehle mahina test karein.** Din 1–3 par speed check, din 7 par peak hours (8–11 baje) check. Problem 10 tareekh ke baad nahi, pehle hafte mein pakrein.
- **Ek se zyada ISP/connection** rakhein agar ghar mein 3+ log dekhte hain — 10 Mbps line par 4K + 3 phones nahi chalte.
- **App ki APK sirf official site** se lein. Telegram groups ki "cracked" APK mein ads aur credential-stealing code hota hai.

## Legal tarike se paisa kamane ka rasta

Reseller banna bhi isi qanoon ke dayre mein hai: aap **Mizanora ki lines** bechte hain, aap ke paas apna bill hota hai, aur **Rs 30 connection fee + Rs 100 har line par mahina** ka hisaab khula hua hai. Aap ko sirf apne clients ka khayal rakhna hai — source, servers aur EPG Mizanora ke paas.

## Khulasa

- IPTV **legal** hai; ghair-ijazat wala re-broadcast nahi.
- Block hona provider ke source ka masla hota hai, aap ke internet ka nahi.
- Sasta restream pehle din acha lagta hai, 40 din baad client chala jaata hai.
- Invoice, JazzCash number aur support — yeh 3 cheezein paper par talash karein.
