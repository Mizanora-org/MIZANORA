The **Hadith** page of Mizanora lets you read the major collections of hadith on your phone, free, in **Urdu, English and Arabic**.

## Books you can read

Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah, Muwatta Malik, the Forty Hadith of an-Nawawi and the Forty Hadith Qudsi.

## How to use it

1. Open [Hadith](/hadith).
2. Pick the **book**, then the **language**, then the **chapter** (baab).
3. Use **"Is baab mein talash"** to find a word inside the chapter.
4. Know the number? Type it in **Hadith number** and press open.
5. Tap **Sunein** to hear it read by your phone's voice, **Copy** to share it, or **Noor se poochein** to ask the assistant about it.

## About grades and authenticity

Where the data includes a grade (for example Sahih or Hasan) it is shown under the hadith with the name of the scholar who gave it. Mizanora never invents a grade. Collections other than Bukhari and Muslim contain hadith of different grades, so always look at the grade.

## Important reminders

- A translation is not a fatwa. For questions about rulings, ask a qualified scholar.
- Do not share a hadith unless you are sure of its source, book and number. The reader shows both so you can quote correctly.
- If a book has no Urdu translation in the data, the reader shows English and tells you.

## Ask Noor

The assistant Noor can fetch a hadith by book and number, for example "Bukhari hadith 1". She reads the real text and tells you the book and number. She will not quote a hadith from memory.
