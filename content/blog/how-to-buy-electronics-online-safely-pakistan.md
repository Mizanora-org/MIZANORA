Electronics and mobile accessories are the category where Pakistani online shoppers lose the most money, because a clone looks exactly like the real thing in a photo and the difference only shows up weeks later — when a battery swells, a charger runs hot, or earbuds stop holding a charge.

This guide is the checklist we wish every customer had before ordering.

![Check the parcel before the rider leaves](/img/muaaz/muaaz-iqbal-07.jpg "Open the parcel, check the seal, test it before the rider goes")

## Before you order: five questions

1. **Is the brand name spelled correctly?** "Samsng", "Boat" written as "BoAt" in a different font, "JBL" on a product JBL never made. Clones live in the spaces between letters.
2. **Are there real photos from more than one angle?** A single studio render usually means the seller has never held the item.
3. **Is there a stated warranty, and who honours it?** "Warranty" with nobody named behind it is not a warranty.
4. **Are the specs written out, or just adjectives?** "Fast charging" is an adjective. "20 W PD" is a spec. Buy the spec.
5. **Does the price make sense?** A genuine 20,000 mAh power bank cannot cost Rs 1,200. Physics has a floor; sellers below it are selling you a smaller battery with a bigger number printed on it.

## Category by category

| Item | The usual trick | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chargers | Rated 20 W, delivers 5 W, runs hot | Feel it after 20 minutes — warm is normal, hot is not |
| Power banks | Capacity printed far above the real cell | Weight: a real 20,000 mAh is heavy; a fake is light |
| Earbuds | Looks right, sounds thin, battery dies in weeks | Pair them and check battery % in the phone's Bluetooth menu |
| Cables | Charges but will not sync data | Test data transfer, not just charging |
| Smart watches | Claimed sensors that do nothing | Compare the reading against a real device |

## The moment the parcel arrives

This part matters more than anything you did before ordering, and it takes two minutes:

1. **Open it while the rider is still there.** Once he leaves, a dispute becomes your word against the seller's. The full routine is in the [parcel arrival checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check).
2. **Photograph the seal and the label before opening.** If the item is wrong, that photo settles it instantly.
3. **Test the actual function, not just that it switches on.** A charger that lights up is not a charger that charges at the advertised speed.
4. **Smell it.** A strong chemical smell on new electronics is a real warning sign, not a fussiness.

## Payment and safety

Use [cash on delivery](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) for any first order from any seller, including us. It is the only method where you control the moment money changes hands.

Never send an advance payment to a social-media seller you have not bought from before, and never share a CNIC number for a COD parcel — no legitimate delivery requires one.
