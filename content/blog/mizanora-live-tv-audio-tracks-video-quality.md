Ab Mizanora Live TV mein aapke paas zyada control hai. Player mein ab **audio tracks** aur **video quality** ka intekhab maujood hai, har channel ke liye alag.

## Audio tracks

Agar koi stream mein zyada than ek audio track ho — misaal ke taur par Urdu, English ya Arabic awaaz — to ab aap player ke neeche diye gaye **Audio language** option se apni pasand ki awaaz chun sakte hain. Ye feature un channels ke liye bohat helpful hai jin ki programming zyada than ek zubaan mein hoti hai.

## Video quality

Apni internet ki raftar ke mutabiq video quality chunein:

- **Auto:** Player khud behtareen quality chunta hai.
- **High:** Sab se behtareen picture quality, lekin zyada data kharch karti hai.
- **Balanced:** Behtareen quality aur data ka durust milap.
- **Data saver:** Kam data, kam buffering — slow internet ke liye behtareen.

## Kiske liye hai

Ye feature un sab viewers ke liye hai jo Mizanora Live TV dekhte hain, chahe woh mobile par hon, laptop par hon ya smart TV par. Full screen, Picture-in-Picture aur channel share karne ke options bhi yahin maujood hain.

## v49 update (November 2026)

Naye player mein yehi control ab aur gehra ho gaya hai: generic "High/Balanced" ke saath ab har stream ki **asal resolutions unki bitrate ke saath** bhi chuni ja sakti hain, audio tracks **HLS aur DASH dono** tarah ke channels mein kaam karte hain, saath mein **captions, photo capture aur live stream stats** bhi aa gaye hain. Player ab poori hls.js build istemal karta hai taake stream ke andar wale (embedded) captions bhi chalen.
