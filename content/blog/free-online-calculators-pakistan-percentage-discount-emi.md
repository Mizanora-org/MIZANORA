Roz ke kaam ke liye calculator chahiye hota hai: sale ka discount, loan ki qist, marks ka percentage, bill ka tax. Mizanora ke [free tools](/tools) mein ye sab maujood hain. Neeche har calculator ka formula bhi hai taake aap khud bhi check kar saken.

## Percentage Calculator

- **X ka Y% kitna hai:** Y x X / 100.
- **Marks percentage:** (obtained / total) x 100.
- **Percent change:** (new - old) / old x 100.

[Percentage calculator](/tools/percentage-calculator) kholein.

## Discount aur Sale Price

Saving = price x discount / 100. Do discount saath hon to dusra discount pehle se kam hui qeemat par lagta hai, isliye 20% aur 10% ka matlab 30% nahi, 28% hota hai. [Discount calculator](/tools/discount-calculator) ye khud nikalta hai.

## Profit, Margin aur Markup

Online seller ke liye zaroori: **margin** = profit / selling price, **markup** = profit / cost. Cost mein delivery aur ads ka kharcha bhi daalein. [Profit margin calculator](/tools/profit-margin-calculator).

## Loan EMI

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1). Yahan r mahana rate aur n mahine hain. Islamic bank ke profit rate ka schedule alag ho sakta hai: bank se confirm karein. [Loan EMI calculator](/tools/loan-emi-calculator).

## BMI aur Umar

BMI = kg / (height in meter)^2. Bara (adult) ke liye 18.5 se 24.9 aam taur par normal range hai, lekin ye doctor ki jagah nahi. [BMI calculator](/tools/bmi-calculator), [Age calculator](/tools/age-calculator), [Days between dates](/tools/date-difference-calculator).

## Tax aur Raqam alfaz mein

Tax shamil price se tax nikalne ke liye price ko (1 + rate/100) se taqseem karein. [Sales tax calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator). Cheque ya invoice ke liye [Amount in words](/tools/amount-in-words) lakh aur crore ke saath alfaz likh deta hai.
