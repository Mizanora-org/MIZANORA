Mizanora's motto is **"Shop smart. Shop halal."** This post explains what honest trade means in Islam and how an online shop can follow it. It is a practical guide, not a fatwa: for detailed questions please ask a qualified scholar.

## Be truthful about what you sell

A well-known hadith says that a truthful and trustworthy merchant will be with the prophets, the truthful and the martyrs (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1209). For an online shop this means: real photos, real sizes, real materials.

## Show the fault, do not hide it

The Prophet (peace be upon him) taught that when the seller and the buyer are truthful and make clear (the defects), their trade is blessed (Sahih Bukhari 2079, Sahih Muslim 1532). If a product has a flaw, say so.

## No cheating

"Whoever cheats us is not one of us" (Sahih Muslim 101). Fake discounts, edited photos and hidden charges are forms of cheating. Read [what market rate means](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price) to see how to price honestly.

## Fair measure

The Quran commands: give full measure when you measure and weigh with a right balance (Surah Al-Isra 17:35), and warns those who give less (Surah Al-Mutaffifin 83:1). In an online shop, "full measure" means the size, quantity and quality you promised.

## Be easy to deal with

Allah's mercy is asked for the person who is easy when selling, buying and asking for his due (Sahih Bukhari 2076). Clear returns, polite support and fair refunds follow this teaching.

## Mutual consent

Business should be by mutual consent (Surah An-Nisa 4:29). Do not pressure customers with fake countdowns or false "only 1 left" messages.

## Practical checklist for an online shop

1. Real photos and honest descriptions
2. Clear price, delivery fee and return rules
3. No fake "old price"
4. Polite answers on WhatsApp
5. Deliver what you promised
