A good product post is short, honest and easy to act on. These tips work for WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram.

## The simple formula

1. **What it is** in one line.
2. **Why it helps** in one line.
3. **Price and cash on delivery** so the buyer feels safe.
4. **Your link** so ordering takes one tap.

## Example

Winter shawl, soft and warm.
Good for office and daily wear.
Price Rs. 1,499. Cash on delivery across Pakistan.
Order here: your link

## Tips

- Use 2 to 4 clear photos, not 10 blurry ones.
- A 15 to 30 second video showing the product in use builds trust fast.
- Post at times people are free: after Maghrib and late evening.
- Reply quickly to questions and be honest if you do not know.
- Never copy a competitor's photos. Download the product photos from your dashboard instead.
