People who search for **Muaaz Iqbal** usually want to know what he has built. Here is the list, in simple words. His full profile is on [the Muaaz Iqbal page](/muaaz-iqbal).

## 1. Mizanora (online marketplace)

[Mizanora](/) is a Pakistani online store with cash on delivery, WhatsApp ordering, a large catalogue, a [deals page](/deals), an Urdu voice assistant, a worker programme and a daily Islamic feed. Its motto: *Shop Smart. Shop Halal.* Read [how Mizanora was built](/blog/muaaz-iqbal-story-how-mizanora-was-built).

## 2. HijriAnchor (mobile app)

A privacy-first, offline, ad-free calendar that shows Hijri and Gregorian dates together. See [HijriAnchor explained](/blog/hijrianchor-hijri-calendar-app-offline-privacy).

## 3. Muslim Islam Organization

A community portal at muslimislam.vercel.app: a project hub and community tools.

## 4. Ramadan tools

Sehr and Iftar countdowns by location, daily duas and easy sharing, made for Pakistani users.

## 5. Study notes

Intermediate (11th class) notes for students in Kasur.

## How he works

Muaaz builds fast and alone, using AI tools (Claude, Gemini, Qwen and ChatGPT) for different jobs: code logic, cleaning product data, batch work and writing. He ships a first version within days and improves it every week. He also designs graphics, edits video and enjoys poetry and reading.

Follow his work on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MuaazIqbalofficial), [X](https://x.com/MizanoraApps) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mizanora).
