Every shop starts with a question. For Mizanora the question was simple: **why should shopping online in Pakistan feel risky and confusing?**

![Muaaz Iqbal at work on Mizanora](https://i.ibb.co/RpLFTKfG/file-00000000eca48208bff02ce8e3e3bdf0.png)

## The problem

Many Pakistani shoppers worry about fake shops, unclear prices, late parcels and no one to ask in their own language. Many young people also want to earn but have no capital to start a business.

## The idea

Muaaz Iqbal, a developer and marketer from Kasur, decided to build one place that fixes these problems together:

- **Cash on delivery** across Pakistan, so you pay when the parcel is in your hands
- **Clear help in Urdu**, by chat and by voice
- **A worker program** where anyone can share product links and earn

## Building it step by step

1. **The shop.** A fast, phone-first website with clear product pages and simple checkout.
2. **Noor, the AI assistant.** It understands Roman Urdu, finds products, answers delivery questions and can speak back.
3. **The worker program.** Workers get a personal link, a dashboard, price bands and the freedom to set their own selling price.
4. **The admin AI manager.** The shop owner can ask an AI to change the season look, add videos to the feed, post Islamic reminders, track tasks and research ideas, with confirmation for every risky change.
5. **Free-limit protection.** The catalogue can live in files, so the shop keeps working even if a free database limit ends.

## Lessons

- Start small, but start for real people.
- Test on a cheap phone and a slow connection first.
- Be honest in every price, photo and promise.
