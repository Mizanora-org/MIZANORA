Website par asli visitors Google se aate hain. Is ke liye sahi keywords aur saaf pages chahiye. Mizanora ke free SEO tools ye kaam asaan banate hain.

## 1. Keyword ideas

[Keyword Generator](/tools/keyword-generator) ek lafz se English aur Roman Urdu phrases banata hai: "best", "cheap", "price in pakistan", "kaise", "near me", shehron ke naam. **Ye asli search volume nahi dikhata.** Volume Google Search Console ya Keyword Planner se dekhein.

## 2. Long-tail phrase chunein

"sandal" bohat mushkil hai. "men leather sandal under 2000 in pakistan" mein muqabla kam aur kharidar ka irada saaf hota hai.

## 3. Title aur description

Title 50 se 60 aur description 110 se 160 characters. [Meta Tag Generator](/tools/meta-tag-generator) Google result ka preview bhi dikhata hai.

## 4. Saaf URL

[Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator) se chota URL banayen.

## 5. WhatsApp se seedha rabta
