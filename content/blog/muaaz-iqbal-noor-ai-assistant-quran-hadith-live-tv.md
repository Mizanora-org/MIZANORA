Mizanora started as a simple online shop. Under the work of **Muaaz Iqbal** it has become a small platform: a shop, a place for the Quran and hadith, and a TV page, all in one fast website. This post explains the newest parts in simple words.

## 1. Shop hours that open and close by themselves

The owner sets the opening and closing time (Pakistan time). Outside those hours visitors see a calm "shop is closed" screen and the shop opens again at the set time. An **emergency close** button still exists and always wins over the timetable.

## 2. The Quran keeps playing when the shop is closed

A closed shop is no reason to stop the recitation. The live Quran continues on the closed screen. If a live stream link is ever broken, the ayah-by-ayah recitation with the text and translation starts by itself, so there is no silence.

## 3. Hadith reader

Read Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah and more in Urdu, English and Arabic. Search inside a chapter, open a hadith by its number, and listen with your phone's voice. Grades are shown exactly as the data gives them.

## 4. A smarter Noor

Noor can now fetch an exact hadith by book and number, knows the shop hours, and gives longer answers when you ask her to explain something. She never quotes an ayah or a hadith from memory: only from the real text.

## 5. Real reviews only

Mizanora does not generate or buy reviews. Reviews come from logged-in customers, are read before they appear, and spam such as links and phone numbers is blocked. See the [reviews page](/reviews).

## 6. Live TV with product ads

The new [Live TV](/live) page plays the channels the owner adds. Press Next or Previous to change channel. While the next channel loads, a short ad for a product chosen by the owner is shown, with the real price and a button to the product.

## What is next

More tools for workers, better search, and more Islamic content. Follow Mizanora and Muaaz Iqbal on Instagram (@mizanora) and YouTube (@MuaazIqbalofficial) for updates.
