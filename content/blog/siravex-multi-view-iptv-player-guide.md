If BarakatWave TV is the broadcast side of Muaaz Iqbal's work, **SIRAVEX** is the viewer's side: a player that takes your sources, remembers them across your devices, and lets you watch several channels at once.

![Muaaz Iqbal, founder of SIRAVEX and Mizanora](/img/muaaz/muaaz-iqbal-08.jpg "SIRAVEX: premium IPTV and universal media player")

## What SIRAVEX does

From the product's own description:

- **Connect an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist** — it fetches live TV, movies, series and EPG automatically.
- **AES-256 Firebase Cloud Sync** — your sources are backed up encrypted, so signing in on a new phone restores everything (the "Cloud Vault").
- **Multi-view** — up to **four channels at once** on one screen.

That last feature is the one that makes it different from the usual IPTV app. Sports fans want three matches and a news ticker; families want two dramas and a cricket match. Multi-view is the honest answer to how people actually watch.

## Setting it up

1. Open [siravex.vercel.app](https://siravex.vercel.app).
2. Go to **Add Playlist / Xtream**.
3. Either:
   - **Xtream Codes** — server, username, password (the same three fields described in [the BarakatWave guide](/blog/barakatwave-tv-xtream-codes-setup-guide)); or
   - **M3U** — paste a playlist URL, or upload the file.
4. Save, then sign in on **Restore Cloud Vault** so the same sources appear on your other devices.

If you have a BarakatWave account, the credentials are the same ones BarakatWave gives you — the two products are designed to work together.

## Xtream or M3U: which should you use?

| | Xtream Codes | M3U playlist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you type | server + username + password | one link, or a file |
| Refreshes itself when the panel changes | Yes | No — you re-download it |
| Movies and series | Fetched separately, with categories | Depends on the playlist |
| EPG | From the panel's XMLTV | Only if the playlist carries it |
| Best for | A provider that gives you a login | A static list you maintain yourself |

Rule of thumb: **if your provider gave you a username and password, use Xtream.** It stays current without you touching it.

## Multi-view without wrecking your connection

Four HD streams at once is roughly four times the bandwidth. Some practical advice for Pakistan, where connections vary a lot:

- **Two or three streams** is usually the sweet spot on mobile data.
- **Drop the quality on the smaller tiles** — you only need the main one sharp.
- **Use Wi-Fi for four-way viewing**, especially for sport.
- **Close other tabs and apps.** Video is greedy; everything else is a distraction.

## Legality and good sense

Add only sources you have the right to watch — your own streams, or a provider's service you are subscribed to. Players are tools; what you point them at is your responsibility.

Mizanora takes this seriously in its own product: the importer refuses private addresses, is admin-only, and the channel list is yours to curate. The reasoning is the same as [the trust-first theory](/blog/trust-first-theory-why-mizanora-shows-real-prices) — a shop is only as good as what it is willing to refuse.
