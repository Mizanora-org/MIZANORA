Punjab aur Khyber Pakhtunkhwa mein November se sardi shuru hoti hai aur January sab se thandi hoti hai. Winter shopping agar October ke aakhir mein kar li jaye to prices kam hotay hain aur sizes bhi milte hain. Ye checklist usi liye hai.

## Pehle ye tay karo: kis cheez ki zaroorat hai?

Sardi ki shopping mein log zyada kapray khareed lete hain aur zaroori cheezein (socks, heater, moisturiser) reh jati hain. Neeche wali list copy karo aur apni zaroorat ke mutabiq mark karo.

### Kapray (pehle ye)
- **Sweater ya hoodie** - roz pehnne ke liye 2 hote hain, 1 acha kaafi nahi.
- **Jacket** - agar aap bahir zyada hote ho to insulated jacket pehle lena chahiye.
- **Shawl / chadar** - ladies ke liye 2 hote hain: ek light, ek bhari.
- **Thermal inner** - sab se zyada faida, sab se kam price.

### Joote aur socks
- Bang-bang wale socks 4-6 jore (yahan sasti shopping ka asli faida hai).
- Band (closed) shoes ya boots: sardi mein chappal pehnna = bimar hona.
- Boots ke liye dekho: sole grip, andar ka fur aur size thora bara (moti socks ke saath).

### Ghar ke liye
- Heater ya room warmer (bijli ka bill pehle estimate karo).
- Blanket / razai - single ya double zaroorat ke hisab se.
- Hot water bag, gloves, earmuffs agar aap bahir kam rate ho to zaroori nahi.

### Skin aur health
- Moisturiser aur lip balm (sardi mein skin phatti hai).
- Vaseline / ghee wala simple nuskha behtar aur sasta hota hai.

## Budget: apna hisab kaise lagayen

| Cheez | Aam price (PKR) | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal inner | 700 - 1,500 | 2 lein, roz badalne ke liye |
| Sweater | 1,500 - 3,500 | Wool mix zyada warm hota hai |
| Jacket | 3,000 - 7,000 | Waterproof bahir ke liye |
| Boots | 2,500 - 5,000 | Size thora bara lena |
| Blanket | 1,500 - 6,000 | Double size zyada versatile |
| Socks (6 jore) | 500 - 1,200 | Sasti aur zaroori |

> Sardi ki shopping ka rule: **jo cheez jism se lagti hai wo pehle** (inner, socks, sweater) - phir fashion, phir extra.

## Kab kharidna best hai

1. **October** - sab se acha waqt: stock poora, demand kam.
2. **November** - prices thora barh jate hain, sizes tez khatam hotay hain.
3. **December - January** - demand peak par, jacket ke sizes bhi "sold out".
4. **February** - sasti sale milti hai magar agla saal use karne ke liye.

## Online kharidne ka tareeqa

- Size chart parh kar hi order karo; kapray mein 1 inch bhi farq karta hai.
- Sab kuch aik hi order mein karo taake delivery (Rs 250) aik dafa lage.
- Sasti aur garm cheezon mein **reviews** dekho, sirf photos par bharosa na karo.
- COD use karo: parcel khol kar check, phir paisa.
