Har IPTV service ads ke bare mein saaf nahi bolti. Kai baar channel kholne par 30 second ka video aata hai aur user ko lagta hai internet slow hai. Mizanora mein ads ka system **likha hua aur control mein** hai — aur premium line par kam ho jata hai.

## Ad kab aata hai

Mizanora mein ad ka rule simple hai:

1. Aap **pehli dafa** us channel ko kholte hain
2. Agar admin ne us channel par ad lagaya hai → pehle ad chalti hai
3. Ad khatam → app dobara channel maangti hai → asli stream shuru
4. Agle **6 ghante** tak usi channel par dobara ad nahi

Matlab: ek channel, ek ad, chhe ghante ki chhutti. Har baar channel badalne par ad nahi aati.

## Skip ho sakta hai?

Technically ad **playlist ki jagah** serve hoti hai, is liye app mein "skip" ka button nahi milta — magar:

- Ad chhoti hoti hai (15–30 second)
- Sirf pehli tune-in par
- Chhe ghante ka cooldown
- Banner-only ads stream ko replace nahi karte — video chalti rehti hai, banner upar dikhta hai

Yeh design jaan boojh kar aisa hai: agar ad sirf overlay hoti, to app usay ignore kar deti aur ad dekhne ka koi matlab nahi rehta.

## Ad-free ka matlab kya hai

Premium / paid line par ads ka bojh kam hota hai kyunki:

- Admin priority channels par ads band rakhta hai
- Paid lines ke liye ad frequency kam ho sakti hai
- Movies aur series par ads nahi aate — sirf live channels par

Ad-free ka matlab yeh nahi ke server par koi ad system nahi. Matlab yeh hai ke **aap dekhne ke tajurbe mein ad kam mehsoos karte hain**.

## Paisa kitna

Mizanora ki pricing fixed hai:

| Cheez | Amount |
|---|---|
| Connection fee (aik dafa) | Rs 50 |
| Pehla mahina | fee ke andar |
| Monthly cap | Rs 200 |
| Reseller wholesale | Rs 100 per line |
| Cycle | 1–10 tareekh |
| JazzCash | 0306-2015326 |

Cap ka matlab: koi reseller Rs 200 se zyada nahi le sakta. Is se zyada maangna policy ke khilaf hai.

## Recharge par animation kyun

`/iptv/recharge` par jab aap plan chunte hain to screen par premium unlock animation chalti hai — lock khulta hai, crown aata hai, features highlight hote hain. Yeh sirf dikhawa nahi: isi screen par aap ko **plan, months, total, bachat aur JazzCash number** aik jagah milte hain, taake payment se pehle sab clear ho.

3 mahine par 8% aur 12 mahine par 21% tak bachat dikhti hai. Hisab live calculate hota hai, koi hidden charge nahi.

## 3 mahine lena chahiye ya 1?

- **Pehli dafa** — 1 mahina lein. Ek mahine mein pata chal jata hai ke speed aur channels aap ke liye theek hain.
- **Pasand aa gaya** — 3 mahine behtar value.
- **Ghar ke liye permanent** — 12 mahine, sab se sasta per month.

Trial pehle free hai — `/iptv/start` se 24 ghante.

## Ads se pareshan hain?

Check karein:

1. Kya aap wohi channel baar baar khol rahe hain? 6 ghante ka cooldown hai — dobara kholne par ad nahi aani chahiye.
2. Kya yeh movie/series hai? Wahan ads nahi aate.
3. Phir bhi har channel par ad? Reseller ko batayein — admin ne shayad sab par ad laga di hai; woh priority channels chhod sakta hai.

## Aakhir mein

Ads IPTV ka hissa hain, magar **aap ko pehle se pata hona chahiye** kab aur kitni. Mizanora mein sirf pehli tune-in par, chhe ghante ke cooldown ke sath, aur banner stream ko kharab nahi karta.
