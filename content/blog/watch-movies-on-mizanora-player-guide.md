Mizanora ka Cinema Player aap ke browser mein hi chalta hai — koi app install nahi, koi login nahi. Yeh guide uski har cheez asaan lafzon mein samjhati hai.

## Step 1 — Title dhoondein

[Movies & Series hub](/movies) kholein aur search box mein:

- film ka naam (`Jawan`, `Maula Jatt`, `Inception`) likhein,
- ya **IMDb ID** paste karein (`tt22084616`).

Naam likhne par “World catalogue” section mein IMDb, TMDB aur TVmaze ke nataij bhi aa jate hain — yani woh titles bhi jo Mizanora ki library mein pehle se nahi thay. Kisi bhi card par tap karein.

## Step 2 — “Watch Now” dabayein

Movie page khulne par neeche player milta hai. Bara **Play** button dabayein:

1. Pehle ek chhota **Mizanora ad** chalta hai (aam tor par 10 second; 5 second ke baad **Skip ad**).
2. Ad khatam hote hi stream load ho jata hai.

Ad Mizanora ka apna hai — us mein koi third-party tracker nahi.

## Step 3 — Server chunein

Player ke neeche “Play from” ki patti hai. Har button par server ka naam, quality aur type (HLS / MP4 / Embed) likha hota hai.

- **Pehla server** hamesha sab se tez jawab dene wala rakhein (admin tarteeb badal sakta hai).
- Agar ek server na chale to dusra tap karein — stream foran switch ho jata hai.

## Step 4 — Quality

Neeche dayein taraf **gear** icon se:

- **Auto** — aap ki internet speed ke mutabiq khud badalta hai (tajweez kiya jata hai).
- **1080p / 720p / 480p** — pin karein to player usi quality par ruk jata hai.

Screen par chhota chip mojooda resolution aur bitrate dikhata hai.

## Step 5 — Audio track aur subtitles

- **Audio** (zubaan icon): agar stream mein multiple tracks hain (misal Urdu + English), yahan se badlein.
- **Subtitles** (CC icon): track chunein ya Off karein.

Yeh options sirf tab nazar aate hain jab stream waqai woh tracks bhej raha ho.

## Step 6 — Speed, PiP aur screenshot

- **Speed**: 0.5× se 2× tak.
- **Picture-in-picture**: doosra tab kholte hue bhi video chalti rehti hai.
- **Full screen**: bara screen, mobile par landscape.
- **Camera icon**: jo frame chal raha hai usi ka PNG screenshot — par note: kuch protected streams screenshot rok dete hain, tab saaf message milta hai.

## Resume — jahan chhorha tha wahin se

Chalte hue player har 4 second baad aap ki position aap ke browser mein save karta hai. Dobara usi title par aayein to **“Resume from 12:34”** ka chip milta hai — tap karein aur wohin se chal parta hai.

Yeh data sirf aap ke device par rehta hai (local storage). Browser data clear karne par chala jata hai.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Kaam |
|---|---|
| Space / K | Play / Pause |
| ← / → | 10 second peeche / aage |
| ↑ / ↓ | Aawaz |
| F | Full screen |
| M | Mute |
| C | Screenshot |

## Series kaise dekhein

Series page par Seasons aur Episodes player ke andar hi hain:

1. Season chunein (Season 1, 2, 3…).
2. Episode tap karein — player usi episode ka link banata hai.
3. Agla/pichla episode ke arrows se aage barhein.

## “Koi server set nahi hai” likha aata hai?

Iska matlab site admin ne abhi play server add nahi kiya. Aap tab tak **Official Trailer** tab istemal karein — cast, story, release date aur official “where to watch” links phir bhi poore mojood hote hain.
