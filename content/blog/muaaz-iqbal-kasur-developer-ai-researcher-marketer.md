Some people see a small city as a limit. **Muaaz Iqbal**, who lives in **Kasur**, sees it as a starting point. He combines three skills that rarely come together in one young person: **development, AI research and marketing**.

## Developer

He writes the code himself: the website, the checkout, the admin panel and the tools that keep the shop fast on cheap phones. Because he builds the product, a customer problem can become a fix on the same day.

## AI researcher

Muaaz spends time testing how AI can help real shoppers: understanding Roman Urdu, answering in a natural voice, finding the right product in a messy search, and now helping the shop owner manage tasks. He tests an idea, measures it, and keeps only what truly helps.

## Marketer

A good shop also needs people to find it. He studies search keywords, writes useful guides, and designs the worker program so that sharing a link is easy and fair. Our [Google SEO checklist for small shops](/blog/google-seo-checklist-small-online-shop-pakistan) comes from this practical work.

## "Unchi soch": thinking big

His working principles:

- **Think in years, act in days.** A big goal is broken into small daily upgrades.
- **Solve for your own people first.** Pakistani shoppers, Pakistani problems, Urdu first.
- **Learn in public.** He studies ICS at Islamia Graduate College and applies what he learns to the shop.
- **Be honest.** No fake discounts, no hidden charges.
