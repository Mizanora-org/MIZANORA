Live TV sirf aadha maza hai. Asli farq tab parta hai jab aap ke paas **movies aur series** ka apna library ho — jab chahein, jahan se chahein, shuru se.

Mizanora par yeh library khuli hui hai: **/iptv/movies** aur **/iptv/series**

## Library mein kya hota hai

Har movie ke sath:

- **Poster** — asli cover art
- **Saal** — release year
- **IMDb rating** — 10 mein se
- **Category** — Action, Comedy, Drama waghera
- **Plot** — 2–3 line ki kahani
- **Cast aur director**
- **Runtime**

Series ke sath iske ilawa **seasons** aur **episodes** ki poori list hoti hai.

## Kya library mein nahi hota

Ek zaroori baat: public pages par **koi stream link nahi hota**. Aap ko poster, naam, plot aur rating dikhti hai — asli file ka address server ke andar sealed rehta hai.

Iska matlab:

1. Koi link copy kar ke bahar nahi bhej sakta
2. Google par leak nahi hoti
3. Line ke baghair kuch nahi chalta

Movie dekhne ke liye login chahiye — browser player ya Xtream app, dono par.

## Step 1: library browse karein

`/iptv/movies` kholein. Upar search, group filter aur sorting milti hai:

- **Rating** — sab se achi rating pehle
- **Naye** — recently added
- **A–Z** — naam ke hisab se

Mobile par 2 column, laptop par 5 column. Poster par click karein to detail page khulta hai — wahan plot, cast aur "browser par dekhein" ka button hota hai.

## Step 2: series ke episodes

Series page par seasons alag alag blocks mein hote hain. Har block mein episodes ki list — `E1`, `E2`, `E3` — aur episode ka title.

Kaam ki baat: **episode order se chalayein**. Kai baar log season 2 se shuru karte hain aur phir kahani samajh nahi aati. Detail page par `Season 1` sab se upar hota hai.

## Step 3: apps mein VOD

Xtream apps mein Movies aur Series ke apne tabs hote hain:

- **TiviMate** — Movies / Series tabs, poster grid ke sath
- **IPTV Smarters** — VOD section, category wise
- **Browser player** — Live / Movies / Series, teen tabs

Apps wohi catalogue dekhti hain jo website par hai, kyunki source aik hi hai. Farq sirf itna hai ke apps mein play button seedha chalta hai, website par login maangta hai.

## Movie chalane ka tareeqa

1. `/iptv/player` kholein
2. Username/password daalein
3. Movies tab → picture chunein
4. Play

Agar buffering ho:

- Wi-Fi par aayein
- Player ka quality switch check karein
- Doosri movie try karein — masla aik file ka bhi ho sakta hai

## Rating ka matlab

IMDb rating admin ke import se aati hai. 7 se upar aam tor par dekhne layak, 8 se upar achi, 6 se neeche sirf shauq ke liye. Rating na ho to poster par kuch nahi dikhta — matlab us item ka metadata abhi bhara nahi gaya.

## Kitni movies hoti hain

Catalogue ka hisab `/iptv/status` par live milta hai — channels, movies, series aur episodes ki ginti. Library barhti rehti hai kyunki admin M3U ya manual entry se naya content add karta hai.

Pehli dafa khali lage to 5 minute baad refresh karein — pages 5 minute ka cache rakhte hain.

## Request kaise karein

Koi movie ya series missing hai? Reseller ko naam bhej dein. Woh admin panel se add kar sakta hai (M3U link ya direct source). Har request ka foran jawab zaroori nahi, magar list regularly update hoti hai.

## Free trial mein library milti hai?

Trial line par live channels aur library dono chalti hai — 24 ghante, 1 device. Is liye pehle `/iptv/start` se trial lein, library browse karein, phir plan chunein.

## Aakhir mein

IPTV ka asli faida live TV nahi, **aik jagah sab kuch** hona hai: news, sports, movies, series — aik hi login se, har device par. Library page se shuru karein, phir app mein wohi login daal dein.
