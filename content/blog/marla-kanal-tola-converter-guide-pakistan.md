Pakistan mein zameen marla aur kanal mein, sona tola aur masha mein naapa jata hai. Hamara [Unit Converter](/tools/unit-converter) in sab ko ek jagah badal deta hai.

## Zameen ke units

- **1 marla** = 272.25 square feet (aam maqbool maiyar).
- **1 kanal** = 20 marla = 5,445 square feet.
- **1 acre** = 43,560 square feet = 8 kanal (taqreeban).
- **1 sq yard** = 9 square feet.

> Kuch ilaqon mein marla ka naap mukhtalif hota hai. Registry ya patwari record se tasdeeq zaroor karein.

## Sone ke units

- **1 tola** = 11.664 gram.
- **1 tola** = 12 masha = 96 ratti.
- **1 masha** = 0.972 gram taqreeban.

Zewar kharidte waqt wazan aur karat ka sahi hisab zaroori hai. Zakat ke liye bhi tola gram mein badalna parta hai: [Zakat calculator](/tools/zakat-calculator).

## Wazan aur lambai
