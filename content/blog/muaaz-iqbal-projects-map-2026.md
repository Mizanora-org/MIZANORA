## How they fit together

They are not random. There is one idea running through all of them, and it is the same one in [the Mizan mindset](/blog/muaaz-iqbal-mizan-mindset-balance-theory): **remove the barrier that stops an ordinary Pakistani from using the thing.**

- **Mizanora** removes the barrier of trust — you see the parcel before you pay.
- **BarakatWave** removes the barrier of format — whatever a channel broadcasts, the player takes it.
- **SIRAVEX** removes the barrier of the app — your playlist and Xtream login work in one place, on every screen.
- **Asaan Lang** removes the barrier of English — you can learn to code in the language you think in.
- **HijriAnchor** removes the barrier of connectivity and surveillance — it works offline and shows no ads.

That is a coherent body of work, not a pile of side projects.

## BarakatWave TV, in a little more detail

BarakatWave is the largest of these after the shop. Its own site describes a "3D Universal Studio" that plays **DASH (.mpd), HLS (.m3u8), MPEG-TS (.ts), HTTP-FLV and MP4/MKV/WebM**, with ClearKey DRM support, Widevine licence URLs and an automatic CORS relay failover.

It also runs an **Xtream Codes v2.9** server: sign in with Google or Gmail and you can generate a personal Xtream Codes username, password, M3U Plus playlist link and XMLTV EPG URL, which then work in IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate, XCIPTV, OTT Navigator, VLC and smart TVs.

The channel line-up is organised the way a Pakistani viewer would arrange it: **Pakistan → Islamic → Drama & Entertainment → News → Movies & Sports**, and there is an IMDb-indexed cinema and series hub.

If you want that same line-up inside Mizanora Live TV, you now can — see [the Xtream setup guide](/blog/barakatwave-tv-xtream-codes-setup-guide).

## SIRAVEX, in a little more detail

SIRAVEX is the viewer's side of the same world. Connect an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist and it fetches **live TV, movies, series and EPG** automatically, backs the sources up with **AES-256 cloud sync**, and plays **up to four channels at once** in multi-view.

The overlap with BarakatWave is deliberate rather than duplicated: BarakatWave is the broadcast and server side, SIRAVEX is the player side. Full notes in [the SIRAVEX guide](/blog/siravex-multi-view-iptv-player-guide).

## Asaan Lang, in a little more detail

```text
naam = lo "Aap ka naam? "
agar naam != "" to
    likho "Khush Amdeed, " + naam + "!"
warna
    likho "Mehman, salaam!"
bas
```

It has loops, functions, lists, dictionaries, try/catch, a playground, and a compiler that turns a program into a website. Errors arrive in Roman Urdu with the line number. MIT licensed, Python 3.8+.

The argument behind it is in [the Urdu-first theory](/blog/urdu-first-design-theory-technology-in-your-own-language), and there are [eight examples for beginners](/blog/learn-coding-in-urdu-asaan-lang-examples-for-beginners).

## Why one person could build all of this

The honest answer is in [the compound builder theory](/blog/compound-builder-theory-one-hour-every-day): one project at a time, extended continuously, with every decision written down and every day shipping something. Plus [the AI orchestra theory](/blog/ai-orchestra-theory-claude-gemini-qwen-chatgpt-together) — several models, one conductor.

And the reason it survives is [the zero-quota theory](/blog/zero-quota-theory-systems-that-survive-free-limits): every one of these is built so it keeps working when the free tier runs out.
