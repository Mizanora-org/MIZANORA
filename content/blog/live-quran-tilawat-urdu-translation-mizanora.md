Mizanora has a **live Quran session**. At the times the owner sets, a recitation plays on the website, and it can also continue when the shop is closed.

## What you see and hear

- The **Arabic text** of the ayah being recited, with words lighting up as it is read.
- The **translation**: Urdu (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry or Muhammad Junagarhi) or English (Saheeh International), or none.
- A **LIVE** badge. The recitation moves on by itself, surah after surah, and all visitors join at the same place.

## Choose your voice and translation

Open the settings in the Quran screen and pick a reciter (Alafasy, Sudais, Abdul Basit, Husary, Minshawi, Shuraim, Muaiqly), the translation and the text size. Your choice is kept on your own phone.

## Why do I need to touch the screen first?

Browsers do not allow sound until you touch the page once. After the first touch the recitation starts by itself.

## Is the Quran text typed by hand?

No. The text, translation and audio come from public Quran data (Tanzil and AlQuran Cloud), so there is no typing mistake from the website. If the translation is slow to arrive, the page keeps trying quietly and shows a small message meanwhile.

## Read the whole Quran

Open [Quran](/quran) to read any of the 114 surahs with translation and listen ayah by ayah.

## When the shop is closed

Outside the shop hours or during an emergency close, the Quran keeps playing on the closed screen. If a live stream link set by the owner fails, the ayah-by-ayah recitation takes over automatically.

## Respect

Please keep the volume suitable for your place, and remember that the Quran should be listened to with attention and respect.
