Log aksar poochhte hain: **Muaaz Iqbal ka dimaag kaise kaam karta hai?** Ek chhote se shehar (Kasur) ka ek student, jis ne market banayi, programming language likhi, live TV app banayi — ye sab kaise soch kar hua?

Jawab talent nahi hai. Jawab **sochne ke tareeqe** hain — chand aadatein jo har project mein baar baar nazar aati hain. Ye article unhi ko tod kar likha gaya hai.

![Muaaz Iqbal, founder aur developer of Mizanora, Kasur Punjab Pakistan](/img/muaaz/muaaz-iqbal-08.jpg "Muaaz Iqbal ki soch: 7 tareeqe")

## 1. Pehle masla likho, solution nahi

Muaaz kisi bhi kaam ka aaghaz "main kya banaoon ga" se nahi karta. Wo likhta hai: **masla kya hai**. Masala tha: *chhote shehar ka aadmi online order kyun nahi karta?* Isi ek sawal se cash on delivery, WhatsApp ordering aur Urdu voice assistant sab nikle.

Aap bhi ye karein: agle kaam par "main ye feature banaoon ga" ke bajaye likhein — *ye kis ka masla hal karta hai?*

## 2. Chhota qadam aaj, bada khwaab barqarar

Ye unka sab se mashhoor tareeqa hai: **unchi soch, chhote qadam**. Bada khwaab saalon ke liye likha jata hai aur kabhi nahi badalta. Magar aaj ka qadam itna chhota hota hai ke ek hi baithak mein khatam ho jaye. Poori theory [small steps theory](/blog/small-steps-theory-unchi-soch-chhote-qadam) mein hai.

## 3. Har cheez ka "aur phir kya?" poochho

Kya hoga jab internet slow ho? Kya hoga jab free limit khatam ho? Kya hoga jab photo load na ho? Ye sawal poochhne ki aadat ne Mizanora ko aisa bana diya ke wo free tier khatam hone par bhi chalti hai — yehi [zero quota theory](/blog/zero-quota-theory-systems-that-survive-free-limits) hai.

## 4. Apni zaban se shuru karo

Duniya English-first banati hai. Muaaz **Urdu-first** banate hain: Noor ki awaaz Urdu mein, Asaan Lang ke alfaaz Urdu mein, search jo Roman-Urdu ki ghalat spelling bhi samajh le. Wajah saaf hai — aadmi wahi sochta hai jo uski maa boli hai. Detail: [Urdu-first theory](/blog/urdu-first-design-theory-technology-in-your-own-language).

## 5. Trust ko becho mat

Photo tootay to product chhupa diya jata hai. Jhooti discount nahi dikhai jati. Jab API jawab na de to `CACHED` likh kar bata diya jata hai. Har baar jab imaandaari ka nuqsan hota hai, wo nuqsan qabool kiya jata hai — kyunki ek baar toota hua bharosa dobara nahi banta. Ye [trust-first theory](/blog/trust-first-theory-why-mizanora-shows-real-prices) hai.

## 6. AI ko orchestra ki tarah chalao

Ek model nahi — chaar: Claude (logic), Gemini (data), Qwen (Urdu), ChatGPT (copy). Aur sab se zaroori: **jo model likhe, wo usay check na kare**. Insaan conductor hai, passenger nahi. Poora tareeqa: [AI orchestra theory](/blog/ai-orchestra-theory-claude-gemini-qwen-chatgpt-together).

## 7. Har din kuch na kuch ship karo

Din ka aakhir mein ek chhoti si cheez poori honi chahiye. Perfect hona zaroori nahi, **mukammal hona** zaroori hai. Ye aadat hi wo cheez hai jo ek ghante rozana ko saal bhar mein ek market bana deti hai — [compound builder theory](/blog/compound-builder-theory-one-hour-every-day).

## In sab ko jodne wali cheez

In saaton aadaton ka ek hi naam hai: **mizan** — balance. Bada khwaab aur chhota qadam. AI ki raftaar aur insaan ki zimmedari. Apna faida aur customer ka faida. Yehi wo scale hai jis par poori dukaan khari hai: [Mizan mindset](/blog/muaaz-iqbal-mizan-mindset-balance-theory).

> Sochna seekhne ka sab se tez tareeqa: soch ko likh lo. Dil mein sochi baat kabhi ghalat nahi lagti; kaagaz par likhi baat turant pakdi jati hai.
