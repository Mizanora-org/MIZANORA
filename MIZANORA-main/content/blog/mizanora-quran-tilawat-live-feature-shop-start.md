Many Muslim shopkeepers begin the day by playing the Quran in the shop. Mizanora now does the same on the website, at the times the owner chooses.

## What you see

When the Quran session is live, a small green button appears: **Tilawat-e-Quran**. Tap it and a beautiful screen opens. The Arabic ayah appears with a soft animation, the recitation plays, and the **words light up as they are recited**. Under the ayah you see the Urdu translation. When the ayah ends, the next one appears. The ayah on the screen is always the one you are hearing.

Browsers do not allow sound before you touch the page, so the recitation starts with your first tap. You can pause, go to the next or previous ayah, change the speed, go full screen, or close it for the day. The shop music pauses while the Quran plays.

## Read and listen anytime

You do not have to wait for the live time. The [Quran page](/quran) has all 114 surahs. Open any surah, for example [Surah Al-Kahf](/quran/18-al-kahf) or [Surah Ya-Sin](/quran/36-ya-sin), read every ayah with Urdu translation and listen verse by verse.

## Where the text comes from

No ayah on this website is typed by hand. The Arabic text (Uthmani script) and the translations come from AlQuran Cloud (Tanzil) and the recitation from the Islamic Network audio library. If the service is not reachable, the session simply does not start: we never show a wrong text.
