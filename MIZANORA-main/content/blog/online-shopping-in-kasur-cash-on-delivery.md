Kasur is a busy city, but not every product you want is available in the local market. Online shopping brings fashion, footwear, mobile accessories and home items to your door. Here is how to do it safely.

## How to order from Kasur

1. Browse the [Mizanora products](/products).
2. Choose size and colour, then add to cart.
3. Enter your name, phone number and full address (area, street, landmark).
4. Choose **cash on delivery**.
5. Keep your phone on, so the rider can call.

## Write a good address

For Kasur and nearby towns, include the area or mohalla, a known landmark (a mosque, school, shop) and an alternate phone number. A clear address avoids delays.

## Check before you pay

Open the parcel in front of the rider where possible and compare it with the listing. Our [parcel checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check) explains what to look for.

## Track your order

Use the [tracking page](/track) with your order number to see its status.
