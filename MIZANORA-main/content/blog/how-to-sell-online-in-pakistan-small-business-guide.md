Starting an online shop does not need a big budget. It needs a clear plan, honest dealing and patience. Here is a simple path for a new seller.

## 1. Pick products you understand

Choose a small group of products you can describe well, check yourself and source reliably. A focused shop of 10 good products beats 200 products you know nothing about.

## 2. Work out your real costs

Add the product cost, packaging, courier charges, cash on delivery fees and returns. Then set a price that leaves a profit even after a few returns. Our guide on [comparing prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online) shows what buyers look at.

## 3. Take clear photos

Customers buy what they can see. Use daylight, a plain background and several angles. See [product photography tips with your phone](/blog/product-photography-tips-with-your-phone).

## 4. Write honest descriptions

State the material, size, colour and what is in the box. Add a size guide. Honest descriptions reduce returns, as explained in [how to read product photos and descriptions](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## 5. Offer cash on delivery and clear rules

New customers trust cash on delivery. Publish your delivery time, returns rules and contact details, so buyers can [tell you are a real shop](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## 6. Add video

A short video of the product in use answers questions faster than text. See [why product videos help sales](/blog/product-videos-help-customers-buy).

## 7. Promote step by step

Start with friends and family, then post on social media, then try small paid ads. Read the [basics of Facebook and Instagram ads](/blog/facebook-instagram-ads-basics-small-online-shop).

## 8. Answer fast and keep promises

Reply quickly, confirm orders, dispatch on time and share the tracking ID. Happy customers bring new customers.

## Starter checklist

1. 5 to 10 products chosen.
2. Costs and profit calculated.
3. Photos and descriptions ready.
4. Delivery and returns rules written.
5. Contact number active.
