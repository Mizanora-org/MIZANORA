Many young Pakistanis search for **Markaz** and **Mizanora** because they want to buy cheaply or earn online. These two work differently, so the right choice depends on your goal.

## Your goal: buy products for yourself

Mizanora is made for shoppers. You browse, order with cash on delivery and receive the parcel. You do not need to be a business owner or manage stock.

## Your goal: resell products as a business

Markaz is mainly known as a platform connecting resellers with suppliers. A reseller usually handles customers, sets prices and deals with orders, returns and customer questions. Check the Markaz app for its current rules.

## Your goal: earn without handling stock

Mizanora's **worker program** is designed for people who do not want to hold stock. You share product links, and the shop packs and delivers. You can set your own selling price within the price band and earn commission on delivered orders. Read [how to join](/blog/mizanora-worker-program-kaise-join-karein).

## Differences in one view

- **Stock and packing:** reseller platforms often expect you to manage customer orders. In Mizanora's worker program, the shop packs and delivers.
- **Risk:** a reseller can face returns and unpaid orders. A Mizanora worker earns on delivered orders.
- **Learning curve:** reselling teaches full business skills but takes more time.

## Which one should you start with?

If you want to learn business seriously, try reselling and learn customer handling. If you want to start today with no stock and low risk, try the [Mizanora workers page](/workers). Many people do both.
