The moment the rider arrives is important, especially with cash on delivery. These six checks take two minutes and can save you a lot of trouble.

## 1. Check the name and address

Make sure the parcel is addressed to you. Parcels sometimes get mixed up between neighbours.

## 2. Look at the outside of the parcel

Check for torn packaging, wet patches or open seals. If the parcel looks damaged, take a photo before accepting it and tell the rider.

## 3. Confirm the amount

Ask the rider for the amount and compare it with your order confirmation. The total should match the price and delivery cost you agreed. If it is different, call the shop before paying.

## 4. Pay and keep the receipt

Pay the agreed amount and keep any receipt or message. See [how cash on delivery works](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## 5. Open the parcel soon

Open it the same day. Check the product, the size and the colour against the order. Taking a short video of the opening can help if there is a problem.

## 6. Contact the shop quickly if something is wrong

If the item is wrong, damaged or the wrong size, contact the shop the same day with photos. Read the [returns page](/returns) to understand the steps. Using the [size guide](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online) before ordering reduces size problems.

## What to keep

- Order confirmation
- Product page screenshot
- Photos of the parcel and the product
- Messages with the shop

## Parcel checklist

1. Name and address correct.
2. Parcel not damaged.
3. Amount matches.
4. Receipt kept.
5. Parcel opened the same day.
6. Problems reported quickly.

These habits make online shopping safer for everyone.
