Facebook and Instagram have become busy marketplaces in Pakistan. Many honest small businesses sell there, but so do scammers, because creating a page takes minutes. Use this guide to tell the difference.

## Check the page, not just the post

A single nice photo proves nothing. Open the whole profile and look at:

- How old the page is and how long it has been posting products.
- Whether it has a real website or a clear business name.
- Whether the same photos appear on other pages (a sign of stolen images).
- Whether the page lists a phone or WhatsApp number and a city.

## Look at the comments and tagged photos

Real customer photos and honest comments are a good sign. Be careful when all comments look identical or when comments are turned off.

## Verify the shop outside the app

Search the shop name on Google. A real business often has a website, a Google listing or mentions elsewhere. Our guide to [spotting fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan) has a longer checklist.

## Prefer a shop with its own website

Websites make it easier to see prices, delivery rules, returns and contact details in one place. Order pages also create a record of your purchase. Official accounts of a shop should be listed on its own site, like our [official accounts page](/connect).

## Use cash on delivery for new sellers

Never send advance money to a personal account for a seller you have not used before. Cash on delivery means you pay only when the parcel arrives. See [how cash on delivery works](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## Red flags on social media

- Price far below every other shop.
- Pressure such as "only 2 left, pay now".
- Seller refuses to share a phone number or address.
- Seller asks for payment to a different name each time.
- Seller refuses cash on delivery.

## If you were scammed

Stop contact, save screenshots and payment proof, and report the page on the platform. For money lost, ask your bank or wallet provider what options exist and consider reporting through official channels in Pakistan.

## Safe-buying checklist

1. Profile checked, not only the post.
2. Shop verified on Google.
3. Phone number and address available.
4. No advance payment.
5. Order details saved.
