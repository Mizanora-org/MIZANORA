A coupon code is a short code that gives a discount at checkout. Used well, it saves money. Used carelessly, it can lead to scams or disappointment.

## Where real coupons come from

Real codes come from the shop itself: its website, official social media pages, a newsletter or a message from the shop. Be careful with random pages that promise "100% working codes" and ask you to log in or pay first.

## How to apply a code

At checkout, type the code in the coupon box and tap Apply. The bill should show the discount before you confirm. If the discount does not appear, read the message: the code may have expired, need a minimum order, or have been fully used.

## Read the conditions

Common conditions are a minimum order amount, a maximum discount, an expiry date and a limit on uses. Our [budget shopping tips](/blog/smart-budget-shopping-save-money-online) explain how to plan around them.

## Check the final bill

Always check that the total to pay matches what you expect, including delivery. Your [order bill](/blog/how-to-track-your-order-and-read-your-bill) shows the discount line.

## Do not pay to get a coupon

A real coupon is free. If someone asks for money, personal passwords or card details to give you a code, it is a scam. Learn more in [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## Checklist

1. Code from the shop's official channel.
2. Conditions read.
3. Discount visible on the bill.
4. Total to pay checked.
