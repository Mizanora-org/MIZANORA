In a shop you can feel the fabric and test the zip. Online you cannot, so you need a different method. These checks take a few minutes and prevent most disappointments.

## Look at the photos carefully

- Are there several photos from different angles, not just one?
- Do the photos show the back, the stitching or the inside?
- Is the colour the same in every photo?

If every photo looks like a stock image, ask the seller for a real photo. More tips in [how to read product photos and descriptions](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## Read the material and size details

A good listing names the material (cotton, lawn, leather, stainless steel), the size or measurement and what is included in the box. "High quality" on its own is not information. Choose the listing that gives details.

## Check the size properly

Measure a similar item you already own and compare it with the size chart. Do not guess from the letter alone. Read [how to choose the right size online](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## Look for honest reviews

Reviews that mention the real fit, colour or delivery are more useful than a row of five-star lines with no detail. A few mixed reviews that describe a real problem are usually more honest than perfect ones.

## Ask the seller before you order

Good questions: Is the colour the same as the photo? What is the length or measurement? Is exchange possible if the size is wrong? You can ask on our [WhatsApp support](/contact).
