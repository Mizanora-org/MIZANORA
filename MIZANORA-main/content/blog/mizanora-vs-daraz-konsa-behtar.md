People often ask: **Mizanora ya Daraz, konsa behtar hai?** The honest answer is that they are built for different needs. Here is a fair comparison so you can choose.

## What Daraz is

Daraz is a very large marketplace where many different sellers and brands list products. Its strength is range: thousands of categories, big sale events and many brand stores. Policies and offers change often, so always read the current terms on the Daraz app.

## What Mizanora is

Mizanora is a focused Pakistani online market with a smaller, checked catalogue. It is built around cash on delivery, simple ordering, **help in Urdu and Roman Urdu**, and a worker program that lets people earn.

## Side by side

- **Range:** Daraz is far bigger. Mizanora is smaller and more curated.
- **Language help:** Mizanora has the AI assistant Noor that chats and speaks in Urdu. Daraz offers its own support channels.
- **Ordering:** Mizanora works without a mandatory account and with cash on delivery. Check Daraz for its current payment options.
- **Earning:** Mizanora has an open worker program with clear price bands (10% up to Rs 1,000, 15% up to Rs 2,000, 20% above). See [how workers set their own price](/blog/how-mizanora-workers-set-their-own-price-and-market-rate).
- **Personal support:** Mizanora support replies on WhatsApp. Marketplaces handle millions of orders.

## When Daraz is the better choice

When you want a specific big brand, a very wide choice, or a long-running sale event.

## When Mizanora is the better choice

When you want a simple, honest order with cash on delivery, help in your own language, or a way to earn by sharing products.

## Our advice

Compare the **total price**, not just the product price. Read [how to compare prices before buying](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online) and [what market rate means](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price). Then use the shop you trust most.
