Sometimes a page opens but photos stay blank or a video does not play. Most of the time the cause is on your phone or connection, and a quick fix works.

## Quick fixes

1. **Refresh the page.** Pull down or press reload.
2. **Check your connection.** Switch between mobile data and Wi-Fi.
3. **Turn off data saver.** Some phones and browsers block images to save data.
4. **Open the site in Chrome or Safari.** In-app browsers (inside Facebook or Instagram) can block photos, videos and sign-in. Tap the menu and choose "Open in browser".
5. **Clear the browser cache** if an old broken copy is stored.
6. **Update your browser.** Old versions may not play some videos.
7. **Turn off ad blockers** for the site.

## Videos

Some videos come from YouTube, Facebook or other sites. If one does not play, tap the link to open it in its own app. A slow connection can also stop a video; wait a few seconds or switch to Wi-Fi.
