Reels and short videos are now a big part of how people discover products. They are great for seeing how a product looks and moves. They are not proof of quality, so use these checks.

## What a good product video shows

- The real product from several sides, in daylight.
- Size next to a hand or a body.
- The material up close.
- The same item you will receive.

## Before you buy

1. **Open the product page.** On Mizanora the Shop now button in a reel takes you to the product with the price, options and delivery details.
2. **Check the price and delivery cost.** Compare with other shops, as explained in [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).
3. **Choose your size and colour carefully.** Use the [size guide](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).
4. **Pay on delivery.** With cash on delivery you pay only when the parcel reaches you. See [how COD works](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## Red flags

- A price far below every other shop.
- Pressure like "only 2 left, send money now".
- No shop name, address or contact number.
- Comments turned off or only identical comments.

Read more in [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan) and [buying from Facebook and Instagram sellers](/blog/facebook-instagram-shopping-safe-buying-pakistan).
