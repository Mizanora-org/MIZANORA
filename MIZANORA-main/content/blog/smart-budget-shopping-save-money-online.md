Saving money online is mostly about habits, not tricks. These ten ideas work for everyone.

## 1. Decide before you browse

Know what you want and your maximum price before opening the shop. Browsing without a plan leads to impulse buying.

## 2. Use a wishlist and wait 48 hours

Add items to your cart, then close the app. If you still want the item after two days, buy it. Many impulse wants disappear.

## 3. Compare the total price

Include delivery and COD charges. See [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).

## 4. Choose quality for things you use daily

Shoes, bags and kitchen tools used every day often cost less per use when they last longer. A cheaper item that breaks in a month is not a saving. Our [boot care guide](/blog/how-to-care-for-your-boots) shows how care makes products last.

## 5. Buy the right size

A wrong size wastes money and time. Use the [size guide](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## 6. Watch for free delivery

Free delivery can be worth more than a small discount, so check the total price before you decide.

## 7. Time big purchases

Prices drop in sale seasons. Read the [sale season guide](/blog/eid-and-sale-season-shopping-tips-pakistan).

## 8. Avoid "too good to be true" offers

A price far below normal is often a scam. Pay only on delivery for new sellers.

## 9. Keep a simple spending list

Write down what you bought and what it cost each month. You will see where money leaks.

## 10. Return or exchange quickly

If something is wrong, contact the seller the same day. Late claims are harder.

## Quick budget checklist

1. Budget set.
2. Wishlist waiting 48 hours.
3. Total price compared.
4. Delivery cost known.
5. Cash on delivery chosen.
