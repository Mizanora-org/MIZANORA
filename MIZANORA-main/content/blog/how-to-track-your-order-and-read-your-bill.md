After you place an order on Mizanora you get an order ID like MZ-1A2B3C4D and a bill. Here is how to use them.

## Your order bill

Your bill shows every item with its price and quantity, the subtotal, any coupon discount, the delivery charge and the **total to pay** when the rider arrives. It also shows your address so you can check it. If anything looks wrong, contact support right away.

## Track your order without an account

Open the [Track order page](/track), enter your order ID and the mobile number you used. You will see the bill and the progress:

1. **Order placed:** we received your order.
2. **Confirmed:** we verified it with you.
3. **On the way:** it was handed to the courier. Your courier tracking ID appears here.

## Use an account for your history

If you create an account, every order you place while signed in is saved with its bill. You can open any order, print or save the bill as a PDF, and order the same items again.

## Save your addresses

In your account you can save Home, Work and other addresses and choose a default. At checkout you pick one with a tap.

## When the parcel arrives

Check the parcel before paying, using our [parcel arrival checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check). The amount the rider asks for should match the total on your bill.

## Need help?

Use the Support button for WhatsApp help. WhatsApp is for support only; orders are placed on the website. See also [how cash on delivery works](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).
