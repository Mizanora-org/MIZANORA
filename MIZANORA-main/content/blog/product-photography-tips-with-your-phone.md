You do not need a studio or an expensive camera. A phone and a little care give photos that sell.

## Use daylight

Place the product near a window and avoid direct harsh sun. Soft daylight shows true colours. Do not use the flash.

## Choose a simple background

A plain white sheet, a clean table or a light wall keeps attention on the product. Remove clutter.

## Shoot several angles

Take the front, back, side, close-up of the material and one photo showing the product in use or next to a hand for scale. For shoes and boots, include the sole and the inside.

## Keep the phone steady

Rest your elbows on a table or use a small stand. Tap the screen to focus on the product and clean the lens first.

## Show the true colour

Do not over-edit. If the photo looks different from the real item, customers will return it. Honest photos reduce returns, which saves money.

## Size and quality of the image

Use photos at least 1200 pixels wide in a square or 4:5 shape. Mizanora shrinks photos automatically when you upload them in the admin, so pages stay fast.

## Add a short video

A 15 to 30 second clip showing the product from all sides is very helpful. See [why product videos help sales](/blog/product-videos-help-customers-buy).

## Checklist

1. Daylight, no flash.
2. Plain background.
3. At least 4 photos.
4. True colours.
5. Clean lens.
