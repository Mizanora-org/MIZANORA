Meta ads can bring customers to a small shop, but only if the basics are in place. This is a plain overview, not a promise of results.

## Before you spend anything

- Have a website where people can order, with clear prices and contact details.
- Install the **Meta Pixel** so Meta can see visits, add to cart and purchases. Mizanora includes this and also sends events from the server (Conversions API) for better accuracy.
- Use good photos and, if possible, a short video. See [product photography tips](/blog/product-photography-tips-with-your-phone).

## Verify your domain and connect your catalog

In Meta Business Suite you can verify your website domain and connect a product catalog. A product feed keeps your prices and stock in your ads in sync with your shop.

## Start with a small test budget

Run one or two ads for a few days with a small daily budget. Look at which photo or video gets clicks and orders, then keep the best one.

## Know your numbers

Track the cost to get one order and compare it with your profit per order. If an order costs more in ads than you earn, change the product, price or ad.

## Common mistakes

- Sending ads to a page that does not match the ad.
- No clear price or delivery information.
- Changing everything every day.
- Ignoring customer messages.

## Build trust first

Buyers check a shop before ordering. Show a real address, contact number and returns page. See [how buyers spot fake shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan) and [safe buying from social media sellers](/blog/facebook-instagram-shopping-safe-buying-pakistan).

Meta changes its tools often, so check the latest guidance in Meta Business Help Center before you start.
