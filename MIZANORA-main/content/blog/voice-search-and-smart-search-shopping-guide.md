You do not need to type perfect English to find a product. Mizanora search understands the way people really talk.

## Search the way you speak

Try sentences like these in the search box:

- **laal suit 2000 se kam**: a red suit under Rs. 2000
- **black joota under 5k**: black shoes under Rs. 5000
- **sasta watch**: the cheapest watches first
- **kids toys**, **mobile cover**, **perfume**

The search also understands Urdu script, such as the words for shoes, watch or bag, and forgives small spelling mistakes. Under the box you will see what it understood, for example the colour and the price limit.

## Use your voice

Tap the microphone in the search box and say what you want. Voice search works on most Android phones with Chrome. If your phone does not show the microphone, type instead.

## Narrow the results with filters

On the products page you can filter by price range, category, colour and in stock only. Tap a filter again to remove it, or use Clear filters.

## If you cannot find it

Try one word, such as shoes, then add filters. Our shop owners also see which searches found nothing, so they know what to add next.
