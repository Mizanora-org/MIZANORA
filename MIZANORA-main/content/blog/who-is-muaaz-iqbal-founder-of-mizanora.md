**Muaaz Iqbal** is a developer, an AI researcher and a marketer from **Kasur, Punjab, Pakistan**. He is the founder of **Mizanora**, an online market built for Pakistani shoppers, and the person who wrote the shop from the first line of code to the AI assistant that talks to customers in Urdu.

![Muaaz Iqbal, founder and developer of Mizanora](https://i.ibb.co/Kj0c67jy/file-0000000071bc8211be27cfb9f09fc98a.png)

## Quick facts

- **Name:** Muaaz Iqbal
- **City:** Kasur, Punjab, Pakistan
- **Work:** Developer, AI researcher, marketer and founder of Mizanora
- **Studies:** ICS (Intermediate in Computer Science) at Islamia Graduate College
- **Contact:** WhatsApp 03062015326 (the Mizanora support line)

## What kind of person is he?

People who work with Muaaz describe the same thing: he thinks big and builds fast. He does not wait for a big company or a big budget. He picks a real problem, for example "how can a shopper in a small city order safely and get help in Urdu?", and builds the tool himself, tests it, and improves it again the next day.

He believes in three simple ideas:

1. **Honest business.** Fair prices, clear photos and cash on delivery, so the customer sees the parcel before paying.
2. **Technology for ordinary people.** An AI assistant that understands Roman Urdu is more useful to a Pakistani shopper than a perfect English chatbot.
3. **Shared success.** The worker program lets young people earn from sharing products, with clear price bands and fair rules.

## What did he build?

- The Mizanora website and phone-friendly shopping experience
- **Noor**, the AI shopping assistant with Urdu and English voice
- The **worker program** with links, wallets and own selling prices
- An **admin AI manager** that helps run the shop
- A catalogue system that keeps the shop working even when free limits run out
