Pakistan now has three kinds of online buying platforms. Knowing which type fits your need saves time and money.

## Type 1: Large marketplaces

Thousands of sellers, many brands, big sale events. Best when you want maximum choice. Weakness: with so many sellers, quality and service can differ from one seller to another, so always read seller ratings and return rules.

## Type 2: Reselling and wholesale apps

Built for people who want to run a small business by selling supplier products to their own customers. Best for learning trade. Weakness: you handle customers, orders and problems yourself.

## Type 3: Focused shops like Mizanora

A smaller catalogue, a simple order flow, help in Urdu and cash on delivery. Best for ordinary shoppers who want less confusion, and for people who want to earn through a worker program without stock.

## How to choose

1. **Need a specific brand?** Use a large marketplace.
2. **Want to run your own business?** Try a reselling app.
3. **Want simple shopping or no-stock earning?** Try Mizanora.

## Checklist for any platform

- Is cash on delivery available for your city?
- Are there clear photos and sizes?
- Is there a visible return and contact page?
- Can you track your order?
- Is the price fair after delivery?
