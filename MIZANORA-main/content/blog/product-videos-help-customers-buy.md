Buyers cannot touch a product online, so they look for proof. A short video is often the best proof.

## Why videos help

- They show size, movement and material better than a still photo.
- They answer common questions before the customer has to ask.
- They show the product is real, which builds trust.
- They can reduce returns because customers know what to expect.

## What to film

Keep it between 15 and 45 seconds. Show the product from all sides, a close-up of the material, the size next to a hand or body, and the product in use. Film in daylight and hold the phone steady. See [product photography tips](/blog/product-photography-tips-with-your-phone).

## Where to host the video

Upload the video to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, or use a direct video link ending in .mp4. Then paste the link in your product's **Video links** box in the Mizanora admin. The video appears on the product page with a play button, and a "Watch and shop" strip on the home page.

## Tips for better videos

- Start with the most interesting moment.
- Use vertical video for reels and horizontal for YouTube.
- Say or show the price and delivery clearly.
- Do not exaggerate. Honest videos bring fewer complaints.

## Use video in your ads
