Winter has arrived in Pakistan, and online shopping makes it easy to get warm clothes without walking through crowded markets. Here is how to shop well.

## What to buy first

- **Jacket or coat:** the most important piece. Choose one with a zip, pockets and a collar that protects your neck.
- **Sweaters and hoodies:** wool blend or fleece for warmth.
- **Shawls and mufflers:** cheap, useful and easy to match.
- **Socks, gloves and caps:** small items that make a big difference.
- **Boots:** look for grip and a warm lining.
- **Razai or blanket:** for the home.

## How to choose online

1. **Read the fabric.** "Winter" is not a fabric. Look for wool, fleece or thick cotton.
2. **Check the size chart.** Winter clothes are worn over other layers, so leave room.
3. **Look at close-up photos.** Stitching and zips tell you about quality.
4. **Read the care note.** Wool and fleece may need gentle washing.
