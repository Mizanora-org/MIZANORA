Sardi aate hi sab ko garam kapron ki zaroorat hoti hai. Ye checklist aap ki **online sardi ki shopping** asaan kar degi.

## Zaroori cheezein

1. **Jacket ya coat:** zip, pockets aur garam lining dekhein.
2. **Sweater aur hoodie:** wool blend ya fleece behtar rehta hai.
3. **Shawl aur muffler:** sasti aur kaam ki cheez.
4. **Socks, gloves, topi:** chhoti cheezein, bara farq.
5. **Boots:** grip aur garam andar ki lining.
6. **Razai ya blanket:** ghar ke liye.

## Online khareedne se pehle

- **Kapre ka naam dekhein.** Sirf "winter" likha ho to poochein ke fabric kya hai.
- **Size chart mein apna naap dekhein.** Sardi mein upar se aur kapre pehne jate hain, is liye thori jagah rakhein.
- **Photos ghor se dekhein.** Silai aur zip ki quality nazar aati hai.
- **Wash ki hidayat parhein.** Wool aur fleece ko narm dhulai chahiye.

## Paise bachayein

- Pehle zaroori cheezein lein, baqi baad mein.
- Delivery samet total qeemat compare karein. [Online paise bachane ke tareeqe](/blog/how-to-save-money-shopping-online-pakistan) parhein.
- Budget ke hisab se [products page](/products) ke filters use karein.

## Cash on delivery

Mizanora par aap parcel dekh kar paise dete hain. Parcel kholne ke baad [parcel checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check) se check karein.
