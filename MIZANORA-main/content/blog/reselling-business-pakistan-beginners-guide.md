You do not need a shop or big money to start earning online. Many beginners in Pakistan start with **reselling** or **link sharing**. This guide explains how to begin safely.

## Two ways to start

1. **Classic reselling:** you buy or hold stock, sell to your customers and handle delivery.
2. **No-stock sharing:** you share product links and the shop delivers. This is how the [Mizanora worker program](/workers) works.

If you are a student or a beginner, no-stock sharing is lower risk.

## Step 1: Choose a niche

Pick one category you understand: women fashion, kids items, mobile accessories or home items. Focus makes your posts clearer.

## Step 2: Check products honestly

Only share what you would buy yourself. Check photos, sizes and descriptions. Read [how to check product quality](/blog/how-to-check-product-quality-before-you-order-online).

## Step 3: Price fairly

If your platform lets you set a selling price, add a fair profit and be honest about the market rate. Read [what market rate means](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price).

## Step 4: Share smartly

- Post real photos and a short, honest caption.
- Reply fast on WhatsApp.
- Never promise what you cannot deliver.

See [how to write posts that sell](/blog/how-to-write-product-posts-that-sell-on-whatsapp-and-facebook).

## Step 5: Track results

Note which products get messages and which get orders. Repeat what works.

## Common mistakes

- Sharing too many categories
- Over-pricing and losing trust
- Using other people's photos with false claims
- Not replying to customers
