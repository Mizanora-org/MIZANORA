Online shopping 2026 mein pehle se aasan hai, magar sawal wahi hain: **kis shop se lein, price sahi hai ya nahi, aur parcel kaisa aayega?** Ye guide aap ko step by step batati hai - pehli purchase se lekar repeat order tak.

## 1. Pehle shop check karo, phir product

Kharidne se pehle 60 second ye 5 cheezein dekho:

1. **Contact number aur WhatsApp** - asli shop ka number hota hai, sirf form nahi.
2. **Address aur about page** - "hum Pakistan mein hain" likha ho, city bhi ho.
3. **Return policy** - likha hua, saaf. (Zaroori nahi ke aap return karein, magar policy wajood me ho.)
4. **Payment** - COD ka option ho to bharosa zyada. Advance mangne wali nayi shop se hoshiyar raho.
5. **Reviews aur social accounts** - purane comments parh lo, sirf stars nahi.

> Rule: jis shop ke bare mein aap ko kuch nahi pata, wahan pehla order **chhota** rakho (ek item), phir bara order karo.

## 2. Price: 3 minute me faisla

- **Aik hi cheez 3 jagah** dhoondo (category page, search, aur ek doosri shop).
- **Delivery** ko total me shamil karo: Rs 700 + Rs 250 = Rs 950.
- **"Old price" se na dhoka khao** - asli question ye hai: "ye cheez aaj kis rate par thi"? Sale ka matlab tab hota hai jab pehle wala price sach me zyada tha.
- **Bundle** dekho: 2 shirts aik order me = delivery aik dafa.

Mizanora par delivery **Rs 250 flat** hai poore Pakistan par, aur zyada cart par free delivery ke offers bhi chalte hain.

## 3. Order ka tareeqa (1 minute)

1. Product page kholo, size/colour chuno.
2. Cart me daalo ("Add to cart").
3. Name, phone, pura address likho - **landmark** zaroor likho, courier wahan se call karta hai.
4. COD chuno aur order confirm karo. Order ID foran mil jata hai - us se aap **/track** se status dekh sakte ho.

## 4. Delivery: kitna waqt lagega

| Shehr | Aam waqt |
| --- | --- |
| Lahore, Kasur, Faisalabad, Gujranwala | 2 - 3 din |
| Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Multan | 3 - 4 din |
| Chhote shehr aur gaon | 3 - 6 din |
| Sale / Eid ke din | +2 din (bheed ki wajah se) |

Delivery se pehle shop aksar **confirm call** karti hai - ye is liye ke ghalat address par parcel zaya na ho. Phone uthana zaroori hai, warna order cancel ho sakta hai.

## 5. Parcel kholtay waqt (30 second ka check)

- Dabba sahi ho, phitte hue ya kholay hue na ho.
- Product **photo** se match kare.
- Size theek ho (kapron me 1 inch ka farq hota hai).
- Kharabi ho to **foran** delivery person ke saamne batayein ya 24 ghante me shop ko WhatsApp karein.

## 6. Sasti shopping ke 5 asool

1. Sale season ka faida lo, magar **zaroorat** walay items par.
2. Wishlist banao - 2 din rakh kar dekho, phir kharido.
3. Sasti cheez me **reviews** parho; mehngi cheez me **warranty**.
4. Aik order me poora set - delivery bachegi.
5. Assistant se poocho: "Rs 2000 se kam kya behtar hai?" - insan ki tarah jawab milta hai, foran.

## 7. 2026 ki nayi baatein

- **AI assistant** se shopping: Mizanora par **Noor** asli awaz me baat karti hai, Urdu aur English dono me, aur poori catalogue se options dikhati hai.
- **File-based catalogue**: hamari poori shop JSON files se chalti hai, is liye "server ne jawab nahi diya" wala masla nahi aata.
- **Public team page**: kis ne banaya, kaun support karta hai, kaun worker hai - sab khula. Trust chhupane wali cheez nahi.
