Online shopping in Pakistan has grown quickly. Today you can order shoes, clothes, gadgets and household items from your phone and receive them at home, even in smaller cities like Kasur, Sahiwal or Okara. But growth also brings risk. Fake pages, copied photos and sellers who disappear after payment are real problems. This guide gives you a simple checklist to shop with confidence.

## Why online shopping feels risky

When you shop in a market, you can touch the product, compare it and walk away. Online, you rely on photos, descriptions and the seller's word. That gap is where mistakes and fraud happen. The good news is that a few habits remove most of the risk.

## Step 1: Check who the seller is

Before you message a seller, spend two minutes looking at their presence.

- **Look at their history.** A page with months of posts, real videos and comments from different people is more reliable than a page created last week with ten identical posts.
- **Check that their accounts match.** A real business usually has the same name, logo and phone number across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.
- **Search the name.** Type the shop name with the word "review" or "complaint" into Google.
- **Look for a website or official links page.** It should list the accounts the shop owns, so you know which ones are real.

## Step 2: Ask questions in the chat

A trustworthy seller is happy to answer. Ask about the exact product name, the available sizes or colours, the total price including delivery, the delivery time for your city, and what happens if the item is wrong.

> A seller who avoids questions, rushes you, or says "only one left, pay now" is giving you a warning sign.

## Step 3: Understand the price properly

Ask for the final price in one message: product price plus delivery charges. Save that message. If the price changes later, you have proof of what was agreed.

Be careful with prices that look far too low. If a brand-new pair of branded shoes costs a fraction of the normal price, the item is probably fake, used or not real at all.

## Step 4: Choose the payment method wisely

In Pakistan you will usually see these options:

- **Cash on delivery (COD).** You pay when the parcel arrives. It protects you from paying for something that never comes. Read our guide to [cash on delivery in Pakistan](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) for tips.
- **Bank transfer or mobile wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa).** Fast, but harder to reverse. Only pay in advance to sellers you trust, and always check the account title matches the seller's name.
- **Card payments on a secure website.** Look for a padlock and a web address that starts with https.

Never share your card PIN, OTP codes or CNIC photos with a seller on chat. A genuine shop does not need them to take an order.

## Step 5: Keep proof

Keep a screenshot of the product photo, the agreed price, the delivery address you sent, and the seller's phone number. If anything goes wrong, this is your evidence.

## Step 6: Inspect the parcel when it arrives

Open the parcel carefully and check it against your chat messages. Look at the product, size, colour and condition. If something is wrong, take clear photos right away and message the seller the same day. The sooner you report a problem, the easier it is to solve.

## A quick safety checklist

1. The seller has a real history and matching accounts.
2. You have the full price in writing.
3. Delivery time and method are agreed.
4. You are not paying a large amount in advance to a stranger.
5. You kept screenshots of everything.
6. You will check the parcel on arrival.
