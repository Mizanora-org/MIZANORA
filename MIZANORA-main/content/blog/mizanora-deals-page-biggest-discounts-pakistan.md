Searching for a good price in ten different places wastes time. That is why Mizanora now has **one deals link**: [mizanora.vercel.app/deals](/deals). Every product that is really cheaper than its old price is listed there, with the biggest discount first.

## How the deals page works

- A product appears on the page only when its **price is lower than its old price** and it is in stock. No discount, no listing.
- You can filter by the size of the discount (10%+, 20%+, 30%+, 50%+) and by category.
- You can sort by biggest percentage, biggest saving in rupees, or lowest price.
- Shops can pin "hot picks" at the top and show a countdown for a special sale.

## How to read a deal honestly

1. **Look at the old price.** It is shown crossed out next to the new price. The percentage badge is calculated from these two numbers.
2. **Check the photos and description** before you order. A cheap price is only a deal if the product is what you want.
3. **Compare with the same product elsewhere.** See our guide on [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online).
4. **Remember delivery.** Mizanora delivery is a flat fee and you pay [cash on delivery](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan), after you see the parcel.
