Prices online move through the year. Knowing the usual sale seasons helps you buy at a better moment, and also helps you avoid the rush when stock runs out and delivery slows down.

## The usual sale seasons

- **Before Ramadan and Eid:** clothes, shoes, bags, home items and gift sets. Demand is highest, so good sizes sell out fast.
- **14 August (Independence Day):** flags, badges, green and white clothing and event items. Shops run short offers.
- **11.11 and Black Friday (November):** many online sellers run offers around these dates, especially electronics and accessories.
- **End of season:** summer wear is cheaper when winter starts, and winter wear is cheaper in spring.

Dates and offers differ by shop, so always read the sale page itself.

## Buy early for seasonal items

For Eid clothing, order two to three weeks before. Delivery in busy weeks can take longer, and exchange options shrink close to the festival. We explain this in [Eid and sale season shopping tips](/blog/eid-and-sale-season-shopping-tips-pakistan).

## Buy off-season for basic items

Items that are not tied to a season, such as kitchen tools or phone accessories, are often fine to buy on any ordinary day. You do not need to wait for a big event.

## Do not trust countdown pressure

"Only 2 left!" and countdown clocks can push you to buy fast. Take a minute: is the price actually lower than last week, and do you need the item? If not, leave it.

## A simple plan

1. Save the product to your wishlist.
2. Check the price again after a few days.
3. Check the size, colour and return information.
4. Order before the final rush if the item is seasonal.
