Reading one ayah every day with its meaning is a small habit with a big effect. The **Islamic feed** on the Mizanora home page has a card called **Aaj ki Ayat**: a different ayah each day, in Arabic with Urdu translation.

## What is in the feed
- **Aaj ki Ayat** with a link to listen and read the whole surah.
- Short **ayat, ahadees, duas and aqwal**, each with its reference (for example Sahih Bukhari or Muslim with the number).
- Today's Hijri date, the next Islamic occasion and namaz times for Kasur.
- Copy and WhatsApp share buttons.

## Read and listen
Tap *Sunein aur parhein* to open the surah on the [Quran page](/quran): every ayah in Arabic and Urdu, with verse-by-verse recitation where the ayah on screen follows the voice.

## A note on accuracy
The Quran text is loaded from AlQuran Cloud (Tanzil), never typed by hand. Hadith texts in the feed are short, well known and given with their source. For rulings, ask a qualified scholar. More in [Islamic reminders](/islamic).
