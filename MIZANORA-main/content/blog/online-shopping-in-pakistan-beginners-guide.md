Buying online can feel risky the first time. You cannot touch the product, and you are trusting a shop you may never have met. The good news is that a few simple habits remove most of the risk. This guide walks you through your first order, from choosing a shop to receiving the parcel.

## Step 1: Choose a shop you can check

A trustworthy shop makes it easy to check who it is. Look for a real business name, a working phone or WhatsApp number, an address or city, and clear pages for returns, privacy and terms. If a shop hides all of this, think twice. Our guide on [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan) lists the warning signs.

## Step 2: Read the product page properly

Check the photos, the description, the size or colour options and the price. Make sure the price is shown clearly and that delivery charges are stated before you order. If you are unsure, read [how to read product photos and descriptions](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## Step 3: Pick a payment method you trust

For a first order, cash on delivery (COD) is the safest choice because you pay only when the parcel reaches you. [How cash on delivery works](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) explains the process and the small extra checks to do.

## Step 4: Place the order carefully

Type your name, mobile number and full address slowly. A wrong number or a missing landmark is the most common reason a parcel is delayed. Use the number you will actually answer, because the rider or the shop may call to confirm.

## Step 5: Keep your proof

Save the order confirmation, the product page and any messages. If something goes wrong, this is your evidence.

## Step 6: Open the parcel with care

When the rider arrives, check that the parcel is not damaged and that the name on it is yours. Pay, then open it as soon as you can. If anything is wrong, contact the shop the same day and follow its returns page.

## Common beginner mistakes

- Ordering from a page that has no contact details.
- Choosing a size without checking the size guide.
- Ignoring the delivery time.
- Not saving the order details.
- Paying in advance to a personal account for a first order.

## A simple checklist

1. The shop shows a real contact number and a returns page.
2. The price and delivery cost are clear.
3. You know your size.
4. You chose cash on delivery.
5. You saved the order details.

Do these five things and your first online order will almost always go smoothly. When you are ready, browse the Mizanora market and order with cash on delivery.
