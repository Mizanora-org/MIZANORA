Marketing is not only ads. It is knowing where customers come from, giving them a reason to buy, and a reason to return.

## 1. Know where your orders come from

Use a different link for each place you promote, for example your Facebook post, Instagram bio and WhatsApp status. Add a source to the link like `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio`. Mizanora records the source on every order, so you can see which channel really brings buyers.

## 2. Install the Meta Pixel

The Pixel tells Meta who viewed, added to cart and bought, so your ads can find similar people. Read the [basics of Facebook and Instagram ads](/blog/facebook-instagram-ads-basics-small-online-shop).

## 3. Use honest offers

- **Coupons** for first orders or special days. See [how coupon codes work](/blog/how-to-use-coupon-codes-safely).
- **Flash sales** with a real end time. After the end time the price goes back to normal, so the countdown is never fake.
- **Free delivery above an amount** encourages bigger orders.

## 4. Show your products in video

Short reels convert curious viewers into buyers. See [why product videos help sales](/blog/product-videos-help-customers-buy).

## 5. Ask permission before sending offers

Only message customers who agreed to receive offers. At checkout, a customer can tick the box to receive offers on WhatsApp.

## 6. Watch what people search

The searches that found nothing tell you what to add next.

## 7. Take care of the customer after the order

Confirm quickly, dispatch on time and share the tracking details. Happy customers return and tell others. If you are just starting, read the [small business guide](/blog/how-to-sell-online-in-pakistan-small-business-guide).
