Search is changing: Google now answers many questions with AI summaries, and shoppers also ask AI assistants. The basics still win: clear pages, honest information and a fast, trusted site.

## 1. Give every product its own page

Each product needs a clear title, a real description, price, photos and options. Avoid copying a supplier's text word for word. Write what you know: material, size, who it suits.

## 2. Use good photos with a clear name

Photos should be sharp, in daylight and show several angles. Add a short description of what is in the photo. See [product photography tips](/blog/product-photography-tips-with-your-phone).

## 3. Show price, stock and delivery

Google shows price and availability in results when the page states them clearly. Mizanora adds this information in a form search engines understand.

## 4. Connect Google Search Console and Merchant Center

Search Console shows how Google sees your site. Merchant Center lets your products appear in the Shopping tab for free. Use the product feed at `/api/feed/google` on your site.

## 5. Collect real reviews

Genuine customer reviews build trust and can show star ratings in results. Never write fake reviews.

## 6. Keep the site fast on phones

Most shoppers use a phone. Keep photos reasonably small and avoid heavy pop-ups.

## 7. Write helpful guides

Answer questions your customers ask: sizes, care, delivery, safe buying. Our [shopping blog](/blog) shows examples, such as [safe online shopping in Pakistan](/blog/safe-online-shopping-pakistan).

## 8. Make it easy for AI tools

Clear headings, accurate facts and a simple summary page help AI search understand your shop. Mizanora publishes a `/llms.txt` summary for this.

## Checklist

1. Unique product pages.
2. Clear photos and options.
3. Price, stock and delivery shown.
4. Search Console and Merchant Center connected.
5. Real reviews.
6. Helpful guides.
