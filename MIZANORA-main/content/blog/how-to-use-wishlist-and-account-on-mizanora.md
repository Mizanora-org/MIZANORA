You can shop on Mizanora without an account. An account just makes the next order faster and keeps everything in one place.

## The wishlist (heart button)

Tap the **heart** on any product to save it. Open **Wishlist** from the menu to see everything you saved. If you are signed in, your wishlist is also saved on your account, so it appears on every phone you sign in on. If your browser blocks storage (for example inside the Facebook or Instagram app), the list is kept for that visit and saved to your account when you sign in.

## Create an account

1. Open **My account**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, or create an account with your email.
3. Done. Your name, orders and addresses are kept for you.

## What you get

- **Saved addresses:** order in a few taps
- **Order history and bills:** every order in one place, with its status
- **Wishlist sync:** your saved products follow you
- **Security and devices:** see where you are signed in and sign out everywhere if you lose a phone

## Tips

- Use the same account on every phone.
- On a shared phone, always press **Sign out**.
- If a saved product is sold out, we keep it on your list so you can see when it returns.

## Track without an account

You can also follow an order without signing in: use [order tracking](/track) with your order ID.
