Sale seasons are the best time to save money online, and also the easiest time to overspend or get caught by fake offers. A little planning makes a big difference.

## Know the busy seasons

Shopping gets busy around Ramadan and the Eids, wedding season, and large online sale days such as Black Friday and double-date sales. Delivery is slower in these periods because couriers handle many more parcels.

## Order early

For Eid clothes and gifts, order well before the last week. Late orders can miss the day, and rushed sellers make more mistakes. If delivery time matters, ask the shop for the expected days before ordering.

## Set a budget first

Write down the total you are willing to spend. Sales are designed to make everything look like a bargain. A budget keeps you focused. See [smart budget shopping tips](/blog/smart-budget-shopping-save-money-online).

## Check that the discount is real

Compare the "sale price" with other shops, as explained in [how to compare prices](/blog/how-to-compare-prices-before-buying-online). A crossed-out price alone does not prove a discount.

## Watch out for fake sale pages

During sales, fake shops copy real brand names and offer unbelievable prices. Never pay in advance to a new page. Use cash on delivery and check [the signs of a fake shop](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## Check return rules for sale items

Some sellers do not accept returns on discounted items. Read the [returns page](/returns) before you order and check the size carefully with our [size guide](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## Sale season checklist

1. Wishlist made before the sale starts.
2. Budget written down.
3. Prices compared in at least two shops.
4. Order placed early.
5. Cash on delivery chosen for new sellers.
6. Order details saved.

Smart shoppers do not buy more in sales. They buy what they already planned, at a better price.
