"70% OFF" is written everywhere. Is it real? Here are five checks any shopper in Pakistan can do in two minutes.

## 1. Is the old price believable?
If a plain item shows an old price three times higher than similar items, the discount is decoration. Compare the **new** price with other shops, not the old price.

## 2. Does the sale end for real?
A countdown that restarts every day is not a sale. On Mizanora a timed sale returns to the old price automatically when the time ends, on the website and in the order.

## 3. Check the photos, size and description
A low price with unclear photos is a warning. Read [how to read product photos](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions) and [choose the right size](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## 4. Prefer cash on delivery
With [cash on delivery](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan) you open and check the parcel before you pay.

## 5. Add delivery to the price
A cheap item with a high delivery fee is not cheap. Mizanora shows delivery clearly before you order.
