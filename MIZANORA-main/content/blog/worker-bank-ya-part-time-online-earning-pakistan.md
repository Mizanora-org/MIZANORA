Log "online earning" likhte hain aur resulta **scam** nikalta hai, kyunki asli model kam log batate hain. Ye post bilkul seedhi baat karti hai: commission-based online selling kaise chalti hai, kitna mil sakta hai, aur kahan se bachna chahiye.

## Pehle saaf: ye kaam kaise chalta hai?

Aap **kuch nahi kharidte**, kuch investment nahi karti, stock nahi rakhti. Aap:

1. Aik shop se jud jate ho (worker/affiliate).
2. Product ka **link** apne WhatsApp status, Facebook, TikTok ya doston ko share karte ho.
3. Jo banda aap ke link se order karta hai aur parcel **receive** karta hai, uska commission aap ko milta hai.

Bas. Aap parcel nahi bhejte, paisa nahi wasool karte, customer ki shikayat shop sambhalti hai.

## Kitna milta hai? (asli numbers)

Mizanora ke worker programme me commission shop price ka **10% se 50%** hai - level ke hisab se:

| Level | Commission | Kis price tak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 10% | Rs 1,500 tak ke products |
| Silver | 20% | Rs 3,000 tak |
| Gold | 30% | Rs 8,000 tak |
| Platinum | 40% | Rs 20,000 tak |
| Elite | 50% | har product + apna profit |

**Hisab ki misal:** Rs 2,000 ka suit, Silver level (20%) = aap ko **Rs 400** per delivered order. 10 delivered orders mahine me = **Rs 4,000**. 40 orders = **Rs 16,000**. Ye mehnat par depend karta hai - guarantee koi nahi de sakta.

## Kitna waqt dena hoga?

- **Roz 30 minute** = 5-10 messages/groups me 2-3 products share.
- **Haftay me 2 ghante** = captions likhna, photos lena, status lagana.
- Sabsے behtar cheez: **aik category** pakro (jaise kitchen items) - log aap ko usi cheez ka "expert" samajhte hain.

## 7 tips jo asar karti hain

1. **Product ki asli photo + 2 line** likho. Sirf link phenkna kaam nahi karta.
2. **Price likho.** Jis post par price nahi hoti, wahan reply nahi aata.
3. **COD ka zikr karo** - "cash on delivery, poore Pakistan" - bharosa barhta hai.
4. **Apni honest baat** likho: "main ye use kar raha hoon, ye achhi hai, ye kamzor hai".
5. **Spam na karein.** Aik din me 3-4 posts, 30 messages nahi. Facebook/WhatsApp block kar deta hai.
6. **Follow-up karo** - 3 din baad "kuch chahiye tha?" message. 30% orders isi se aate hain.
7. **Cancelled/return orders par commission nahi milti** - is liye aise logon ko target karo jo parcel waqai lein (jinke address saaf hon).

## Paisa kab milta hai?

1. Order **deliver** hota hai.
2. Return window (**8 din**) tak commission "holding" me rehti hai.
3. Phir khud ba khud **wallet** me aa jati hai.
4. **Rs 500** ya zyada par aap **JazzCash, Easypaisa ya bank** me withdraw kar sakte ho.

## Kahan se bachein (scam ki nishaniyan)

- "Pehle Rs 2,000 deposit karo, phir kaam milega" - **nahi**.
- "Har mahine 50,000 pakka" - **jhoot**. Commission aap ki mehnat se bandhta hai.
- "Sirf ID card photo bhejo, kuch karna nahi parega" - ye identity chori ka case hai.
- Company ka **address, number aur policy** na ho - door raho.
