The lowest price on the screen is not always the best deal. A product that arrives late, breaks in a week or costs extra in delivery can cost more than a fair price from a trusted shop. Here is a simple way to compare prices properly.

## Compare the total price, not the product price

Add up everything you will pay:

- Product price
- Delivery charges
- Cash on delivery fee, if any
- Any tax or extra charge shown at checkout

Two shops can show different prices and end up costing the same once delivery is added. Shops that show a single clear price, such as free delivery, make this easy.

## Compare the same product

Check the brand, material, size range and what is included in the box. Many listings use the same photo for products of very different quality. If a price is far below every other shop, ask why before you order.

## Look at the seller, not only the price

A fair price from a seller with a real address, working contact number and a clear returns page is usually safer than a tiny price from a page you cannot verify. See our guide to [spotting fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## Check the discount honestly

A big "old price" crossed out does not always mean a real discount. Compare with at least two other shops. A real offer should still look reasonable after you do.

## Use a short price log

For bigger purchases, write down the price from three shops with the date. Check again a few days later. You will quickly see the normal price range and spot fake offers.

## Time your purchase

Prices often drop during major sale seasons. Our guide to [sale season shopping tips](/blog/eid-and-sale-season-shopping-tips-pakistan) explains how to prepare.

## Quick comparison checklist

1. Same product, same size and colour.
2. Total price including delivery.
3. Seller contact details and returns page.
4. Delivery time.
5. Payment options, ideally cash on delivery.

Spending ten minutes comparing usually saves more money than any coupon.
