Beauty and personal care items touch your skin, so safety matters more than price. Follow these steps when you buy online.

## Check the basics on the product page

- The full **name and size** of the product (ml or grams)
- The **ingredient list**, if the maker shows it
- Clear photos of the **front, back and seal**

If something is missing, ask the seller before you order. See [how to read product photos and descriptions](/blog/how-to-read-product-photos-and-descriptions).

## Allergies and sensitive skin

If you have allergies or very sensitive skin, read the ingredients and try a small amount on your arm first. For skin problems, ask a doctor or a pharmacist; an online shop cannot replace medical advice.

## Be careful with big discounts

Very low prices on well-known brands can mean old stock or imitations. Compare the same product elsewhere and read [how to spot fake online shops](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-online-shops-pakistan).

## On delivery

1. Check that the seal is not broken
2. Check the name and size against the order
3. Look at the expiry or batch information, if printed
4. Open the parcel in front of the rider where possible

Our [parcel checklist](/blog/parcel-arrived-checklist-what-to-check) explains it step by step.

## Perfumes

Perfumes smell different on every skin. Choose a small size first if you are unsure.
