Size problems are the number one reason people return clothes and shoes. The reason is simple: sizes are not the same across brands. A 42 in one brand can feel like a 43 in another. These steps help you order the right size the first time.

## Measure your feet at home

For shoes and boots, measure your foot in the evening, when feet are slightly larger.

1. Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall.
2. Mark the longest toe.
3. Measure from the wall to the mark in centimetres.
4. Measure both feet and use the larger one.

Then compare the number with the shop's size chart. If your measurement is between two sizes, choose the larger one, especially for boots you will wear with thick socks. Our [tactical boots buying guide](/blog/tactical-boots-buying-guide) has more detail on boot fit.

## Measure your body for clothes

Use a soft tape and measure chest, waist and length. Compare them with the size chart on the product page, not with the size you usually wear.

## Read the size chart, not just the size label

Look for measurements in centimetres or inches. Labels such as M or L are only rough guides.

## Check what the description says about fit

Words such as "slim fit", "regular" or "runs small" matter. If the product page says nothing about fit, ask the shop's support before ordering.

## When two sizes are possible

- For shoes and boots, choose the bigger size.
- For fitted clothes, choose the bigger size.
- For loose or oversized clothes, choose your normal size.

## Know the return rules first

Before you order, read the shop's [returns page](/returns). Check whether exchanges are allowed and who pays for delivery. If you want a safer first order, start with cash on delivery, as explained in [our COD guide](/blog/cash-on-delivery-pakistan).

## Size checklist

1. Foot or body measured today.
2. Size chart checked in cm or inches.
3. Fit notes read.
4. Exchange rules understood.

A two-minute measurement can save you a week of waiting for an exchange.
