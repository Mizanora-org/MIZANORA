Women fashion is one of the most popular online categories in Pakistan, and also one where small details decide whether you love the item or return it.

## Read the fabric and season

Lawn, cotton, linen, chiffon and khaddar behave differently in different weather. Check that the fabric matches the season you plan to wear it in.

## Understand "stitched" and "unstitched"

A stitched suit is ready to wear. An unstitched suit needs a tailor. Check which one you are ordering, and what is included (shirt, trouser, dupatta).

## Check pieces and what is included

Listings such as "1 Pc" or "2 Pcs" tell you how many pieces you receive. Do not assume a dupatta or trouser is included if the listing does not say so.

## Length, sleeve and size

Measure a shirt you already like and compare length, chest and sleeve with the size chart. Do not rely on the letter size alone. Read [how to choose the right size online](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## Colour and print

Screens show colours differently. Look for photos in natural light and read the colour name. A real photo of the item is better than a mock-up.

## Care

Check washing instructions. Printed fabrics may need cold water and shade drying.

## Smart habits

- Save two or three options and compare them side by side
- Ask the seller for a close-up of the fabric
- Keep the order number and photos if you need an exchange
