Mizanora Worker Program ek asaan tareeqa hai ke aap bina paisa lagaye products share karein aur har delivered order par commission kamayein. Na stock rakhna hai, na parcel bhejna hai, na customer se cash lena hai.

## Join karne ka tareeqa

1. Mizanora par apna account banayein ya sign in karein.
2. [Worker page](/workers) kholein aur apply karein.
3. Apna CNIC (samne aur peeche ki photo) aur selfie bhejein. Ye sirf verification ke liye hai.
4. JazzCash, Easypaisa ya bank account likhein jahan commission bhejna hai.
5. 1 se 2 din me approval ke baad aap ko apna worker code aur link mil jayega.

## Commission kaise barhta hai

Sab workers 10% se shuru karte hain. Jitne zyada orders delivered hon aur jitni achi delivery rate ho, utna aap upar ke level par jate hain: 20%, 30%, 40% aur 50%. Chote workers ke liye pehle kam qeemat wali products eligible hoti hain. Level barhne par mehngi products bhi aap ke liye khul jati hain.

> Commission sirf delivered order par milta hai. Cancel ya return hone wale order par commission nahi milta.

## Apna link kaise share karein

- Dashboard me **Products and links** kholein aur product chunein.
- **Link** ya **Caption** copy karein aur WhatsApp group, status ya Facebook par share karein.
- Product ki photos aur videos download kar ke apni post me use karein.

## Payout

Order deliver hone ke baad commission "Ready to withdraw" ho jata hai. Rs. 500 ya zyada ho to dashboard se payout request karein.

## Zaroori usool

- Sirf sach likhein. Product ke baare me jhoota daava na karein.
- Spam na karein.
- Apne hi link se khud order na karein. Aisay orders par commission nahi milta.
