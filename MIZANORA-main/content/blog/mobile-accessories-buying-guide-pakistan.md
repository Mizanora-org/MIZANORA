Mobile accessories sab se zyada kharida jane wala saman hai - aur sab se zyada copy maal bhi. Ye guide batati hai ke kam paison mein **wo** accessory lein jo 6 mahine se zyada chale.

## 1. Charger: watt parhna seekho

- Phone ke saath jo charger aata hai, uska watt likha hota hai (jaise 18W, 25W, 33W).
- Kam watt charger lagane se phone slowly charge hoga aur garam hoga.
- Zyada watt charger nuqsan nahi deta (phone jitna leta hai utna hi leta hai), magar mehnga parta hai.
- **Original ya certified** charger mein voltage protection hoti hai - sasti copy charger phone ki battery kharab karti hai.

## 2. Cable: sab se zyada kharab hone wali cheez

Cable ko roz moda jata hai, is liye:
- Braided (kapre wali) cable zyada chalti hai.
- Connector ke paas **strain relief** hona chahiye.
- Type-C, Lightning ya Micro-USB - apne phone ke port ke mutabiq lo.
- 2 saal mein cable zaya hona normal hai; is liye sasti aur achi cable 2 lein.

## 3. Earbuds: 3 cheezein check karein

1. **Fit** - kan mein theek baithe warna bass zaya.
2. **Battery** - case ke saath total hours likha hota hai (asli test reviews mein milta hai).
3. **Latency** - agar aap video/game khelte ho to "low latency / gaming mode" dekho.

> Earbuds wo cheez hai jahan "sasta aur acha" ka balance milta hai: Rs 1,500-3,000 ki range mein achi options aati hain. Rs 500 wali earbuds mein microphone aam tor par kamzor hota hai.

## 4. Power bank: mah ki haqeeqat

- 10,000 mAh = taqreeban 2 phone charges (asli capacity kam hoti hai).
- Output watt dekho: 18W se kam ho to charging slow.
- Air travel ke liye 20,000 mAh tak allowed hota hai (rules check karo).
- Sasta power bank = garam hona + kam charges. Yahan bachat nahi karni chahiye.

## 5. Asli ya copy: 5 nishaniyan

- **Price bahut kam** - asli 25W charger Rs 700 mein nahi milta.
- **Print ki quality** - copy par spelling ghalat ya font alag hota hai.
- **Weight** - asli charger bhari hota hai.
- **Serial / IMEI** - brand ke saath code check karo.
- **Warranty slip** - asli dealer warranty deta hai.

## 6. Kis cheez par kitna kharcha

| Accessory | Bachat ki range | Achi range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable | 250 - 500 | 500 - 900 |
| Charger (25W) | 900 - 1,500 | 1,500 - 3,000 |
| Earbuds | 800 - 1,500 | 1,500 - 3,500 |
| Power bank | 1,800 - 3,000 | 3,000 - 6,000 |
| Phone cover | 250 - 500 | 500 - 1,200 |

## 7. Order karne se pehle

- Ek hi order mein cable + cover + earbuds lein: delivery aik dafa lagegi.
- Photo mein connector ka type zaroor check karo (USB-C vs Lightning).
- COD mein parcel kholtay hi charger test karo: phone 10 minute charge kar ke dekho, garam na ho.
