Cash on delivery, usually called COD, is one of the most trusted ways to buy online in Pakistan. You place an order, the courier brings the parcel, and you pay the rider when it arrives. This guide explains how it works and how to use it well.

## What is cash on delivery?

With COD you do not pay when you order. You pay in cash when the parcel reaches your door. The courier collects the money and sends it to the seller after deducting delivery and COD charges.

## How COD works step by step

1. **You confirm the order.** You send the product, size, address and phone number, and the seller confirms the price including delivery.
2. **The seller packs the parcel.** They hand it to a courier company.
3. **You get a tracking or contact number.** Keep your phone on, because the rider may call to find your address.
4. **The parcel arrives.** You check the parcel, then pay the rider the agreed amount.
5. **Keep the receipt or message.** It is your proof of the order.

## Benefits of COD

- **Lower risk.** You do not pay for something that never arrives.
- **Convenience.** You do not need a bank card or mobile wallet.
- **Trust.** It is easier to buy from a shop you have not used before.

## Limits of COD

- **Not available everywhere.** Some remote areas have no COD service. Ask the seller about your city or village.
- **Extra charges.** Couriers often add a COD fee. Ask for the final amount in advance.
- **Fake orders hurt sellers.** When buyers refuse parcels for no reason, sellers lose delivery money. Only order what you truly want.

## Tips for buyers

- **Confirm the total price in writing** before the parcel is sent.
- **Give a complete address,** with a nearby landmark and an active phone number.
- **Be available** on the delivery day, or tell a family member to receive the parcel and pay.
- **Keep the exact cash ready,** because riders do not always carry change.
- **Check the outer parcel** for damage before you accept it, and take a video when you open it if the item is valuable.
- **Report problems the same day.** Message the seller with photos.

## Can you check the product before paying?

This depends on the courier and the seller. Some allow you to open the parcel at the door, and some do not. Ask the seller before you order, so you know the rule. If opening is not allowed, record a video when you open the parcel, because it helps if there is a problem.
