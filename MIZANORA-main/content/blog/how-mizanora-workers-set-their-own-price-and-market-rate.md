The Mizanora Worker Program lets people share product links and earn when orders are delivered. Workers can also set their own selling price on each product and, if they wish, a market rate. This guide explains how it works.

## The price bands

The most a worker can add as profit depends on the shop price of the product:

- Products up to **Rs 1,000:** up to **10%**
- Products up to **Rs 2,000:** up to **15%**
- Products up to **Rs 3,000:** up to **20%**
- Products above Rs 3,000: up to 20%

This keeps prices fair for customers and profit clear for workers.

## Setting your selling price

In the worker dashboard, open Products. Each product shows the shop price, your maximum profit in rupees and a box for your selling price. You can set any price from the shop price up to the shop price plus the maximum profit.

## What is the market rate box?

You can also write the market rate: what the same product usually sells for in other markets. The shop then follows a simple rule:

- If the market rate is **higher** than your selling price, customers on your link see the market rate struck through and the discount percentage.
- If the market rate is **equal or lower**, customers see only your selling price. No discount is shown.

Please write a real market rate. Honest pricing builds repeat customers. Read [what market rate means](/blog/what-is-market-rate-and-how-to-know-a-fair-price).

## Where customers see your prices

Customers who open your link see your prices on product pages, product lists, search results, the cart and the order. Other visitors see the normal shop prices.

## Commission and profit

Besides your own profit, the program pays a commission on delivered orders. On products where the shop's own profit is small, the commission percentage is kept low, so your selling price is the main way to earn on those products.
