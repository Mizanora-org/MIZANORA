Gift dene ka asal raaz mehngai nahi, **sahi cheez ka intekhab** hai. Rs 1,000 ka sahi gift Rs 5,000 wale bekaar gift se zyada yaad rehta hai. Ye guide mauqay ke hisab se ideas deti hai - sab online order ho sakte hain.

## Mauqay aur unke liye ideas

### Shadi / mangni
- **Bridal ya party wear** (size pehle confirm karo - ye gift wapas nahi hota).
- Home ka decorative piece (wall clock, lamps).
- Khushbu / attar set - mehnga nahi, magar yaad rehta hai.

### Birthday (bachon ke liye)
- Remote control car, puzzle, drawing set.
- School bag + water bottle set.
- Bachon ke joote (sardi mein boots sab se zyada pasand aate hain).

### Birthday (bare logon ke liye)
- Watch, wallet, ya perfume.
- Koi acha kitchen item (jo wo roz use karein).
- Comfortable chappal / shoes.

### Eid
- Kapray (kurta, kurti, suit) - Eid se 2 hafte pehle order karo.
- Bachon ke liye 2 sets: ek ghar ke liye, ek bahir ke liye.
- Mithai ki jagah data ho to home items (bedsheet, crockery).

### Naye ghar / naye kapre ki rasm
- Bedsheet set, towel set, crockery.
- Kitchen starter: pan, cooker, storage set.
- Aisa gift jo ghar mein **nazar aata** hai - log bar bar yaad karte hain.

## Gift ka budget kaise tay karein

| Rishta | Achi range (PKR) |
| --- | --- |
| Dost / colleague | 1,000 - 2,000 |
| Cousin / bhanja bhanji | 1,500 - 3,000 |
| Sakhi / close rishta | 3,000 - 7,000 |
| Shadi ka khaas tohfa | 5,000+ |

> Rule: gift ki qeemat se zyada **packing aur waqt par dena** asar karta hai. Der se aane wala acha gift aadha asar deta hai.

## Online lay kar dene (deliver) karne ke tips

1. **Address dobara confirm karo** - gift address ghalat hua to sab zaya.
2. **Order par "gift" likhwao** - achi shops gift note aur simple packing karti hain.
3. **Delivery ka din** - khaas din se 2 din pehle pahunchwao.
4. **Invoice alag** - gift ke saath price slip jana theek nahi; WhatsApp par shop ko keh dein "price slip na lagayen".
5. **COD** - agar aap khud deliver nahi kar sakte, COD se order kar ke us se keh dein parcel receive karle.

## Gifts jo "sirf Pakistani" hain

- Ajrak / ralli / handloom ka piece.
- Khussa / kolhapuri chappal (handmade).
- Copper / brass ka kitchen item.
- Khajoor, chhuare aur nuts ka hamper.

## Aakhri tip

Gift khareedne se pehle 30 second socho: **"Kya wo ise 3 mahine baad bhi use karega?"** Agar jawab haan hai, to khareed lo - chahe sasta ho.
