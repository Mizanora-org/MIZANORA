Winter clothes cost more than summer clothes, so a wrong purchase hurts. Here is what to look at before you order warm wear online.

## Check the fabric first

Warmth depends on the material and thickness. Look for the fabric name (wool blend, fleece, cotton fleece, corduroy) and any note about weight. If the listing only says "winter", ask what the fabric is.

## Think in layers

A thick single piece is not always better than two thinner layers. Inner wear, a shirt or suit, and a shawl or jacket on top give you flexibility indoors and outdoors.

## Leave room in the size

Winter clothes are worn over other clothes. If you plan to wear a sweater under a jacket, choose the size that fits comfortably with it. Use the size chart and compare with a jacket you own. See [how to choose the right size online](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-size-online).

## Look at the details

- Zips and buttons: are they shown clearly?
- Cuffs and neckline: do they hold shape?
- Lining: is it mentioned?

## Care instructions matter

Wool and fleece may need gentle washing. Check the care note so the item does not shrink or lose shape after the first wash.

## Do not buy everything at once

Start with the basics you need most: a warm shawl, one good jacket and warm socks. Add more if the weather demands it. Off-season prices usually fall later, as explained in [when to shop online](/blog/best-time-to-shop-online-pakistan-sale-seasons).
