Online shopping me sab se bara kharcha **bharosa** ka hota hai. Galat shop se itna nuqsan hota hai ke log shopping chhor dete hain. Ye 12 nishaniyan batati hain ke shop asli hai ya sirf page bana kar baitha hai.

## Shop kis cheez se asli lagti hai

1. **WhatsApp number jo WAQAI reply kare** - message par 24 ghante me insan ka jawab. Robot nahi.
2. **Pura address** (shehr + ilaqa) - sirf "Pakistan" nahi.
3. **Saaf return aur refund policy** - agar return mushkil hai, likh kar batao.
4. **COD ka option** - advance-only nayi shop se hoshiyar.
5. **Orders ka pata** - order ID aur track karne ka page.
6. **Asli photos** - aik hi stock photo sab jagah nahi; product ki 3-4 photos alag angle se.

## Ye nishaniyan KHATRA hain

1. **Price bohat zyada sasti** - market se 60% kam ka matlab aksar copy ya advance scam.
2. **"Sirf aaj" ka dabao** har roz chal raha ho.
3. **Sirf screenshot reviews** - naam aur date ke bina.
4. **Bank transfer/advance par zor**, COD ka zikr hi nahi.
5. **Mobile number na ho**, sirf form. Ya number ka WhatsApp band ho.
6. **Instagram page sirf 2 hafte purana**, comments off.

> Sab se asaan test: shop ko WhatsApp par aik **mushkil sawal** poochein ("is ka size chart bhej dein", "ye glass ka hai ya plastic?"). Asli shop theek jawab degi. Fake shop "hmm", "yes sir" ya ghayab ho jayegi.

## COD ka bharosa kaise kaam karta hai

COD (cash on delivery) me aap parcel kholtay waqt dekhte ho aur phir paisay dete ho. Is me aap ka risk kam hota hai, magar:

- Courier **phone par call** karta hai - uthana zaroori hai.
- 2-3 baar order kar ke cancel karna aap ka **COD record** kharab karta hai; kuch shops aise customers se advance mangti hain.
- Parcel ka dabba kholtay hi check karo, aur **delivery person ke samne** kharabi batayein.

## Reviews kaise parhein

- "Bohot acha" = kam faida. "3 mahine use kiya, ye problem aya" = asli review.
- **Photo reviews** sab se bharosemand hain.
- Sirf 5 star wali shop shak wali hoti hai - asli duniya me 4.4-4.8 normal hai.

## Aik chhota experiment (5 minute)

1. Shop par aik sasti aur aik mehngi cheez ka rate compare karo.
2. Support se aik sawal poocho; jawab ka **waqt** aur **tareeqa** dekho.
3. Policy page kholo - likha hua hai ya copy-paste?
4. Ye 3 cheezein theek hain to **pehla order chhota** rakho (ek item).
