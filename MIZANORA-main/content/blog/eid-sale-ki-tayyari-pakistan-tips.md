Eid sale har saal aati hai, aur har saal log do ghalatian karte hain: **der se order** aur **bina list shopping**. Ye plan aap ko bachat bhi dega aur bheed bhi nahi.

## Eid se pehle: 3 hafte ka plan

| Waqt | Kya karein |
| --- | --- |
| Eid se 3 hafte pehle | List banao, sizes naapo, wishlist me save karo |
| Eid se 2 hafte pehle | Rates compare, phir first order (bara) |
| Eid se 10 din pehle | Doosra order (chhote items, gifts) |
| Eid se 7 din pehle | **Last safe order** - is ke baad late arrival ka risk |
| Eid se 3 din pehle | Sirf local/wishlist items, ya kuch na |

**Sab se bara rule:** Eid se 7 din pehle order band kar do. Eid ke aakhri hafte me courier network par bheed hoti hai aur parcel 1-2 din late aata hai.

## Kis cheez par sab se achi bachat hoti hai

- **Kapre**: Eid ke kapron par sab se pehle demand aati hai, sab se pehle sale bhi.
- **Footwear**: sizes tez khatam hote hain - pehle order karo.
- **Gifts**: mithai ke bajaye home items (bedsheet, crockery, perfume).
- **Bachon ka saman**: sale me sasta + size barhne ka masla hota hai - thora bara lein.

> Jo cheez wajib nahi, us par sale dekh kar kharcha karna "bachat" nahi, kharcha hai. List pakri rakho.

## Budget kaise banayein (aam ghar)

- Kapre: 40%
- Joote/chappal: 15%
- Gifts: 20%
- Ghar/kitchen: 15%
- Bachon ke liye: 10%

Aur har hissay me **10% cushion** rakho - delivery, size exchange ya aik chhoti cheez ke liye.

## Online order ki 6 practical tips (Eid season)

1. **Sizes naap lo** - kapron me 1 inch farq Eid ke din mood kharab karta hai.
2. **Landmark likho** - courier city me dhoondta hai, gaon/gali me landmark.
3. **Phone uthao** - confirm call na uthai to order cancel.
4. **Aik order me poora set** - delivery aik dafa, bachat Rs 250 tak.
5. **Gift ho to note likhwao** - price slip na lage, aur packing simple ho.
6. **Return window yaad rakho** - 7 din me check kar ke complaint karo.

## COD aur eidi ka hisab

COD me parcel aata hai, aap dekhte ho, phir paisay dete ho - eidi ka behtareen tareeqa. Sab se pehle **zaroori** cheezein kharido, phir jo bacha us se "dil ki cheez". Yehi asli plan hai.
