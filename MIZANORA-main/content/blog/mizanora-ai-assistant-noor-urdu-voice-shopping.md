Shopping is easier when you can simply say what you need. **Noor** is the AI assistant built into Mizanora. She understands **Urdu, Roman Urdu and English**, and she can answer by chat or by voice.

## What can Noor do?

- **Find products:** "Rs 1000 se kam jacket dikhao"
- **Show deals:** "Aaj ki best deals"
- **Answer questions:** delivery, cash on delivery, returns and order help
- **Help with prices:** ask for a better price and the shop's rules decide what is possible
- **Speak back:** turn on voice and she answers in a natural voice

## How to use her

1. Open the Mizanora website or app.
2. Tap the assistant button.
3. Type or tap the microphone and speak.
4. Tap a product card to see the details.

## Why she answers fast

Noor uses a quick AI model first, prepares the first sentence of her voice while the rest is being made, and speaks at a comfortable, slightly faster pace. If one AI service is busy, another takes over so you are not left waiting.

## Safe by design

Noor cannot see your password or payment details, and she does not take orders without you pressing the order button yourself.

## Tips

- Use short sentences.
- Use headphones if you are in a noisy place, so she does not hear her own voice.
- If the voice does not start, tap the speaker icon once.
