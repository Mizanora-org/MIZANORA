# Mizanora content plan — 100 blog topics (aur 12 jo live ho chuke hain)

> Yeh file plan hai, publish queue nahi. Har topic ke saath **angle** likha hai taake do posts ek
> jaise na hon. Jo topic aap ke asli tajurbe/figures par nahi khara, usay publish na karein —
> Google duplicate aur jhooti biography dono ko pakadta hai (E-E-A-T).

## Sach pehle: 100 posts ek raat mein kyun nahi

- 100 posts jo ek hi baat 100 tarah se kahein = **doorway pages**. Google unhein index karta hai,
  rank nahi karta, aur poore domain ki quality gir jati hai.
- Ek banda (Muaaz Iqbal) ke bare mein 100 biography posts likhne ka matlab hoga 100 baar wahi
  maloomat, ya **bana hui** maloomat. Dono kharab.
- Isi liye: **12 posts live** (yeh release), aur neeche **100 topics ka queue** — har hafte 3–5
  publish karein to 6 mahine mein 100+ ho jayenge, aur har post ka apna maqsad hoga.

Live posts (is release mein): Kasur story · Asaan Lang · HijriAnchor · BarakatWave TV ·
AI orchestration · Movies/IMDb system · Actor pages · Songs player · Sitemap SEO playbook ·
Coverage ka sach · Analytics/100k plan · Live TV + product ads · Quran tools · Urdu AI assistant ·
Pakistan COD guide.

## Publishing rules (har post par)

1. **Ek sawal, ek jawab.** Title hi sawal ho.
2. Kam az kam **2 asli internal links** (movie/song/tool page).
3. Koi jhooti maloomat nahi; numbers ho to source ya "hamara data".
4. Cover image: site ki apni (`/img/...`) ya IMDb official.
5. 400–900 words; Urdu/Roman Urdu + English mix.
6. Publish ke baad sitemap (`/sitemaps/blog.xml`) khud update hota hai.
